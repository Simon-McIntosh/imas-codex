Research · standard names · 2026-10-12

The catalog has no house style

Every accepted name passed its own review, and the WEST batch scores a median of 0.94 on documentation. Read side by side, the same entries define one concept four ways, open in four styles, and sit in physics domains chosen by the IDS they were extracted from. Consistency is a property of the catalog, and nothing in the pipeline measures it.

§1 — What was measured

The population is every live accepted standard name: name_stage = accepted, docs_stage = accepted, status <> superseded, which is 2,472 names, read from the live graph on 2026-10-12 through bounded read-only Cypher. The WEST population is the frozen roster imas_codex/standard_names/manifests/reviews/v0.4.0rc7+west-task-2e.sn_names.yaml (220 names, minted 2026-09-11). Of those 220, 218 are accepted on both axes; one is superseded with accepted documentation and one superseded with pending documentation. Nothing was written.

§2 — The scores cannot see it

Per-name review judges each entry against its source and the grammar, alone. It cannot notice that the next entry says the same thing differently. That is why the WEST batch looks finished by every pipeline measure while reading as inconsistent to a physicist.

The boundary row is a physics problem rather than a style one. The 80 names spelled *_at_plasma_boundary gloss their locus in eight different combinations of "plasma boundary", "last closed flux surface", "last closed magnetic flux surface" and "separatrix", and 24 of them equate the boundary with the separatrix. Those coincide only for a diverted plasma; for a limited plasma the last closed flux surface touches the limiter and there is no separatrix inside the vessel. The ISN locus registry defines plasma_boundary only as "the surface used to delimit the plasma domain", so the catalog has nothing to agree with.

Notation drifts the same way. In the WEST roster the magnetic-axis major radius is written R_0 in 9 entries and R_{axis} in 5, and the toroidal-flux coordinate is \rho_{tor} in 7 entries and \rho_{tor,norm} or \rho_N in 3. Across the corpus the poloidal field is B_p in 20 entries, B_\theta in 5 and B_{pol} in 4.

§4 — Families share no template, because the code cannot see the family

Grouping the accepted names by name template with the subject token wildcarded gives 46 species families of four or more members (430 names). The share of members that open their description with a distinct phrasing has a median of 0.90, and in 22 of the 46 families every member opens differently. The worst are ratio_of_volume_averaged_<S>_density_to_volume_averaged_electron_density (14 members, 14 openings) and <S>_source_rate (13 of 13).

The generation prompt already demands one template per family (imas_codex/llm/prompts/sn/generate_docs_system.md, "Family Parallel Structure"), so this is not a prompt failure. The family it is handed is the wrong one. fetch_sibling_family in imas_codex/standard_names/context.py and assemble_family in imas_codex/standard_names/harmonize.py both build a family from shared HAS_PARENT edges. Each species variant has its own parent: flux_surface_averaged_argon_density_at_plasma_boundary reduces argon_density_at_plasma_boundary, and so on. So the 17 boundary-averaged densities are 17 one-member families, the sibling block is empty, and every entry sets its own template.

The WEST documentation shows the result at the scale of a batch. Its 218 accepted entries open in four styles in near-equal shares: "The … is …" (77), "This quantity is …" (55), another noun phrase (53) and "<Name> is …" (33). Only 59 carry a sign-convention paragraph, which may be right, but nothing checks it against the COCOS type.

§7 — Thirty-eight names cannot be found by meaning

38 accepted names fall into four clusters whose members share a bit-identical embedding while carrying different descriptions: flux_due_to_pumping, permeability_of_ferritic_element, plasma_heating_power, width_of_spectrometer_channel and 14 more in the first cluster; time, momentum_source_rate, energy_flux_limiter_coefficient and 11 more in the second. Each vector was computed from something other than its own text, so semantic search, near-duplicate detection and the comparator block in the compose prompt are blind to these names. Several of them (diffusion_coefficient_due_to_diffusion, flux_at_first_wall with unit W.m^-2) also look like naming defects in their own right.

§8 — What this implies

Consistency is a property of the set, and a per-name loop cannot produce it however good its prompts are. Four things are missing, and each is a plan:

  • An authored house style: one definition per shared concept, one notation per quantity, one gloss per locus, one opening template per name pattern. Choosing these is physics judgement, made once by the coordinator and the lead rather than by a pipeline run — sn-house-style.
  • Families built from the grammar, so the template rule has siblings to apply to, with domains assigned by quantity and the vocabulary overlaps adjudicated — sn-catalog-grouping.
  • A command that applies the style family by family, from an anchor outward, and measures conformance before and after — sn-harmonize-command.
  • A WEST batch brought to that style and read end to end by a person before a physicist sees it — sn-west-prototype-release.
