§8 — signal role stored at enrichment and filterable

A Jev Choice over six roles judges each signal once at enrichment and stores it as signal_role. The 300-signal stratified prototype agreed with the hand labels on 287 of 300 (95.7%), and the lead's random-sample check on 194 of 200 (97%) against its 90% threshold, at about USD 0.0000264 per signal. The JT-60SA reprocess stored a role on every described signal the sweep selects: measured 4,185, derived or computed 5,117, configuration parameter 3,621, status or flag 1,042, reference or setpoint 656, commanded 280; the 210 underspecified signals carry none, which the sweep excludes by design.

search_signals accepts role=, applied before the rank cut; the physics-domain filter is kept beside it. On the live graph over the 22 golden questions, a role=measured search returns only stored-measured signals, holds no grade-2 command or reference in any top 5 and returns rows with grade-3 measurements; without the filter, 4 of 22 questions (plasma current, booster supply voltage, TF coil current and voltage) keep a grade-2 command or reference in the top 5. Every grade-2 signal the filter keeps is the same quantity at an adjacent point. tests/llm/test_signal_role_filter_live.py, marked graph: 4 passed; removing the filter reds the two filter assertions and an empty filtered arm reds the positive control.
