Verdict

The plan asked whether a free local model could triage the standard-name graph. The answer is a qualified no, and the work it was meant to enable was delivered anyway by a cheaper route. DSv4-flash is a competent defect detector and an inconsistent judge; the graph-wide advisory census was retired rather than deferred; and the recovery triage in the plan's title was produced deterministically, for nothing, from signals the graph already held.

The instrument, and what it cost to build honestly

Phase A built two mechanisms the reuse map marked genuinely new. A non-persisting three-seat panel adapter (aa553671) retains all raw seat judgments with median, spread and contested status, because the review pipeline's arithmetic mean discards exactly the disagreement a benchmark exists to surface. A frozen-batch runner (edb57f02) carries three distinct hashes — population, ordered inputs, ordered results — so a later run cannot silently redefine what was measured. Both prove their advisory contract by structure: static dependency tests fail if the module ever reaches graph mutation, review persistence or the budget manager.

A first gate run refused to execute (a1f98d21) because 31 of 51 positive slots were unpopulated and no numeric threshold existed for any of the five measures. Gates with no numbers cannot pass or fail, and running anyway would have manufactured the authority the census was conditional on. The refusal cost USD 0.00 and was the correct result.

The replacement corpus (a90c9dca) is 40 ordered rows — 20 accepted valid identities plus 20 deterministic corruptions across 18 domains, hash 216e738a — each carrying path, identity, unit and provenance, the four things all 31 legacy stubs lacked. The builder fails closed on a corruption that leaves an identity unchanged, which a dry run had caught and which would have inflated every recall figure. A parallel disposition of the legacy surface (c334e821) found 32 of 51 positive rows recoverable and the 19 losses concentrated in three whole domains rather than scattered.

Calibration cost USD 2.544742 of a USD 10 ceiling (0ebbf133); the pinned re-run cost USD 0.9617504 (4a0fbd7a), a third as much, because determinism is cheaper as well as more repeatable.

The shape of the failure is the result. The model catches every critical defect and does not cry wolf — the two properties a triage instrument needs. What it cannot do is give the same answer twice or agree with the panel that would adjudicate it.

Why the instability was not fixable by configuration

A diagnosis (a28cdeb6) found all 7 unstable rows among the 20 good rows and 0 among the 20 corrupted: a deliberate corruption is unambiguous, while a clean name near the accept boundary is a judgement call. Six of seven flips were reject-to-accept, so the model was inconsistently too harsh, not randomly wrong. The mechanical cause was unpinned decoding against vLLM defaults of temperature 1.0 and seed None.

Pinning temperature 0.0 and seed 20260825 (3990b9c6) moved repeatability to exactly its threshold, confirming the sampler diagnosis, and traded it for Brier — greedy decoding sharpens probabilities, so a model that stops wavering also stops hedging. The residue is provider-side and irreducible on this stack: three calls sharing an equal complete semantic request hash returned 1.000, 0.625 and 0.9875, and three serial full-corpus passes spread Brier across 0.043953 with 30 of 40 rows moving. Removing it would need a controlled deterministic-serving variant — five settings varied one at a time, ~26 min cold start each, two dedicated H200s at reduced throughput.

The census, retired rather than deferred

Relocked by the lead 2026-09-01 to small-pilot-only. At the moment of asking, 7 of the node's 8 H200s were allocated, so the determinism sweep was not merely unbudgeted but unavailable without displacing a live serving seat — and it would have settled only whether gates read as values or bands, producing no better standard name. Against a review-only drain that came in at USD 61.56, characterising the instrument cost more than the work it would have ranked.

The recovery, delivered without the census

The ranking the census was going to produce came instead from signals already in the graph (27f0b78a). The parked population is 275 — not the ~349 previously in circulation — partitioning into 32 rescore-candidate, 4 vocabulary-gap and 239 stay-parked, residual 0. 239 of 275 are correctly parked, so the actionable worklist is an order of magnitude smaller than the census would have justified.

It carries the one property the advisory route could not buy at any price: the ordered-row hash d2335280 is identical across two consecutive passes, and the committed query hash 2dad8167 is embedded so the population can be rebuilt rather than trusted. Every signal read carries a positive control proving the property exists rather than silently evaluating null — which matters because a wrong property name returns a clean, plausible, empty partition, and nobody interrogates good news.

The rescore, and the three defects it exposed

Resubmitting the 32 recovered 12 to accepted and returned 10 to the tail with fresh scores, at USD 4.982846 across 115 attributable LLMCost rows. The recovery thesis holds.

Its identity-retention gate failed on a real defect, which is what the gate existed to catch: sn rescore entered REFINE_NAME and minted a reworded successor, particle_convection_velocity yielding particle_velocity_due_to_convection, against a documented unchanged-identity contract. Two further defects came with it — an unaffordable mandatory reviewer seat made the runner repeat a budget-refused deferral instead of terminating, and 3 of the 32 had drifted out of deterministic validation, including toroidal_angle_of_secondary_x_point, which asserts an angle while carrying the metre unit its own DD binding supplies. The worker refused a retry under the time fence and the two-attempt rule; a re-roll would have bought a better number and hidden all three.

The contract is now structural (90444b9e): an exact-rescore run selects only the review pool and cannot reach refine, so a poor draw has nowhere to go but back onto the same node. Budget saturation in rescore scope terminates as budget_exhausted without restating non-rescore saturation. 2 passed on the contract suite, 39 passed and 0 failed across targeted suites. One caveat kept: the fail-before was a collection error rather than an assertion failure, so the empirical proof of the old defect remains the live run.
