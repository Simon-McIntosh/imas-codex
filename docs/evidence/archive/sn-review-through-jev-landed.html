§4 — Why the documentation score sits near chance

A calibration-only diagnosis of the frozen measurement found three compounding causes. It reproduced the measured AUC of 0.610 as its control and did not open validation.

  • Blind to links and house style. Of the 144 failing labels, 81% fail on house style (52%) or links (29%), but the 15 code-decided checks for those families never entered the score.
  • Claims starved of evidence. 69% of claims say nothing, because the DD text is a median 22 characters (65 of 216 entries are under 8). The claim state carries no units, links, siblings or source paths.
  • Components that cancel. Two named checks are inverted and one is flat, so the check mean partly cancels.

Reweighting alone lifts calibration AUC from 0.610 to 0.657 [0.575, 0.743] with no new calls. The incumbent's 0.514 is explained by its own flatness: only 31 distinct values. Review 101. Merge 3251a887f.

  • Each Choice question in review_checks.yaml carries a conforming list; the loader refuses an empty list, one that is not a subset of the options, or one on a Noul.
  • One reader gives a check's failure probability: a Noul's true case, a Choice's non-conforming mass, an audit finding as 1 or 0. A documentation claim conforms only on supports, so an unverifiable claim counts as a failure.
