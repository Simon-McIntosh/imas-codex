§2 — Jev selects each grammar segment from the closed vocabulary

imas_codex/standard_names/segment_selection.py is an unwired prototype. For each grammar slot it asks Jev one Choice over that slot's closed ISN vocabulary, read through grammar_tokens_by_segment. Large vocabularies are walked as a beam of three, scored by the geometric mean of the per-level choices. The chosen tokens render through compose_canonical_ir, so a token the grammar lacks is recorded as that item's failure and never repaired (merge 00a070a95).

Its first review found a blocking defect: when every expansion of a beam node failed, the node was re-queued and asked again without bound, past 200 calls in a stubbed run. The repair narrows the beam instead. A second round moved rendering onto the existing compose_canonical_ir and put the new test file into the gate. Reviews 95, 112 and 118/120; the gate passes 39 at head. A follow-on test drives an empty slot through select_segments and shows the item records the failure with no rendered name (merge 22e130c4a, review 118/120). The measurement on golden A is in flight.
