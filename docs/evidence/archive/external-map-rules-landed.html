§2 — External rule record designed

The design (fragment, merged at d80bfb497) proposes four classes: ExternalMapBundleVersion keyed by the content digest of the exact files read, with a tag held as a label rather than an identity; ExternalMapRule carrying only an immutable selector identity; ExternalMapRuleRevision holding every provider field plus the raw JSON; and ExternalMapRuleChange holding field-level old and new values. A signal rule's identity is facility, provider and physical channel (source group and array). A machine binding adds phase, role and struct-array entry, with a differential pair canonicalised unordered, so the FL7-FL1 to FL1-FL7 rename keeps one rule id: across both phases that transition is 104 changes and no additions or removals. The bundle at v0.2.0-rc4 holds 61 signal rules, not 45 (magnetics alone is 45). 60 of them join a SignalSource; the unjoined one is GEQDSK PSI, which has a candidate EQDB alias that is not adopted. All 61 signal rules and all 432 machine bindings join an IMASNode. The flux-loop ψ label sits on magnetics/flux_loop/flux, not on its /data leaf, so a class must be resolved through ancestors. A changed rule re-queues every source its old or new revision joins; bookkeeping alone does not.
