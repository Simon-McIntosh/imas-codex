An objective gate vector, and a holdout large enough to use it

Mechanism — and a retraction of this plan's own headline

The generalization failure had an obvious story (off-holdout paths carry less relationship context) and a boring one (the gate is measuring the wrong thing). Testing before acting found the boring one, plus a defect in the measurement apparatus that qualifies every comparison above.

The gate is a witness check, not a content check

essential_relationships passes on any Markdown link or one of nine broad phrases. In the off-holdout receipt all 14 passes carry a link, only 2 also carry a phrase, and all 39 failures carry neither. The representative false negative is vertical_coordinate_of_antenna_strap: it does state its relationship in prose — that the coordinate works together with the other cylindrical coordinates — and fails solely for lacking a link or an allow-listed phrase.

The holdout was flattering it. The holdout repeats 13 identities across 85 paths while the off-holdout sample draws 53 unique identities, so name-family composition rather than documentation quality governs incidental lexical-witness emission. The 80/85 was inflated by repetition, and the −0.677 is substantially a property of the gate and the sampling rather than a real collapse in quality.

The harness never measured production generation

Both receipts used the benchmark docs generator, which reconstructs a minimal prompt item and bypasses production graph enrichment. Verified directly: across all 138 prompts in both populations, zero carried a candidate-specific parent, component, base, derivative, peer, related or sibling context block.

So this plan retracts its own headline. The +0.056 artifact-free result was measured with the production model and the production prompt but without the production context, on a set whose gate behaviour is governed by identity repetition. It is not evidence that current documentation matches or beats the curated catalog, and it was reported to the release-readiness coordinator as though it were before this diagnosis landed — corrected there the same session.

What survives unqualified: the content-gated template, rich DD context and COCOS injection are live; the COCOS relationship defect is fixed; the holdout, gate vector and priced-admission machinery exist; the independent-obligation prompt framing is a genuine instruction improvement. What does not survive is the quality verdict. The apparatus needed to earn one now exists, which is the actual deliverable, and two folded nodes route the harness through production enrichment and rename the gate to what it detects.

The uncomfortable reading is the useful one: three paid comparison runs, a prompt change defended by them, and a peer plan briefly unblocked on their strength — all resting on an evaluator that silently answered a different question. It was caught by asking why a result failed to generalize instead of accepting the first plausible cause, which is the same move that earlier rejected the word-cap explanation. The documentation content gates, and why two were dropped

The plan required mandatory typical values and a general measurement or calculation section. The live faithfulness policy forbids both, to prevent plausible but ungrounded filler, and permits measurement content only where it is constitutive or discriminating — enforced by test_normative_documentation_policy.py and a dedicated prose-adjudicator seat. Both scouts flagged the conflict independently; the currency audit ruled the plan claims wrong.

Locked 2026-08-23 in favour of the live policy. The reasoning that decided it: the policy is a refinement of the plan's intent rather than a rejection of it, since it already admits measurement content exactly where it carries physics. The rejected alternatives both cost more than they returned — a grounded-only synthesis needs a per-path value source the DD does not carry for most paths, so its gate would degrade into the very filler the ban exists to stop, while inverting the policy outright would make roughly 2,400 doubly-accepted names non-conformant on two gates. The operative gate set is therefore definition, governing equation, variable definitions in prose, semantic scope and exclusions, essential relationships, sign convention where COCOS applies, and inline name: cross-references.

Keep the instrument, delete the gate

The blunt question, asked by the lead through the concurrent plan's coordinator: are we holding ourselves to regex gates that neither improve nor guarantee quality? Largely yes, and the evidence for it was already sitting in this record.

docs_gates.py imports re, a dataclass, one LaTeX audit and the link parser. Zero model calls. Four of the nine gates tested for a word rather than for content:

Every gate that moved between two identical runs was one of those four. Every structural check held at exactly its prior value. That is not a property of this corpus; it is the difference between measuring the text and measuring the model's phrasing luck.

Which reframes this record's own headline. A metric on which curated human prose scores 0.888 and our generator scores 0.975 is not evidence the generator writes better physics. Curators wrote physics; they did not write to a token list. That gap is closer to a measure of our generator matching our own prompt vocabulary — a self-agreement score wearing a quality score's clothes. Every nine-gate mean recorded above is therefore history, not evidence. It is retained because it is how we found out, not because it certifies anything.

What the vector did earn

Twice, and both times as a diagnostic rather than a bar. It caught the instruction-competition trade — +0.165 of gains against −0.165 of losses, a net zero that any aggregate score would have reported as "no change" while symbol definitions were quietly traded away. And it exposed the length bound as mis-set, by scoring curated prose at 0.235 against a cap it had never agreed to.

What survives

Six gates, each comparing the documentation against something authoritative: defining_equation (a relation is present), symbol_definitions (the relation's symbols are defined in prose), relationship_link (at least one link resolving against a live identity — a phrase no longer suffices), sign_convention (canonical form), link_hygiene (references parse and resolve), minimum_word_count (a floor). The four retired patterns are gone from the module, with a grep asserting it, so they cannot return.

The refresh is re-gated on the mechanism that already decides documentation quality — the existing generate/review/refine quorum, the same bar every accepted document passed — with revision snapshots as the reversible net. The human expert-review packet is the ultimate authority: physicists reading 210 documents is the only signal in this system that measures physics rather than form, and the six survivors exist to avoid wasting that attention rather than to certify quality.

Recorded as the next increment rather than bundled into a deletion: dimensional consistency of the equation against the DD-declared unit, and sign_convention made conditional on the COCOS transformation type. Both convert a presence check into a physics check.
