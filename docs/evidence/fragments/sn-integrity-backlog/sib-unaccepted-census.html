How a class is decided

A source with an accepted binding is not in the population. Otherwise its class comes from its binding and its own recorded state, in this order: a bound name at drafted or reviewed is awaiting review when it has no score or a score at or above the floor, and scored below the floor otherwise; a bound exhausted name is scored below the floor when its score is below it; a bound superseded name, and a name that cleared the floor yet ended exhausted, is refused by a writer. With no binding, an empty DD description is documentation missing, a vocabulary reason is a vocabulary gap, an unclaimed extracted row is never claimed, a row at the compose claim cap is attempt-capped, and a recorded binding-collision, attachment-rejection or pre-validation refusal is refused by a writer. For exhausted names, a missing score is awaiting review, a score below the floor is scored below the floor, and a score at or above it is documentation missing when its docs are not accepted and otherwise refused by a writer.
