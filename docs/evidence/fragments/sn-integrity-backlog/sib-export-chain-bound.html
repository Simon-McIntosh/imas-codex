The waiver refusal refuses a successor chain past its bound

_fetch_waived_source_chain_identities read a waived source's successor chain through HAS_SUCCESSOR*0..25, so a chain longer than the bound returned a truncated identity set and any identity published beyond the cut was absent from the waiver refusal's input. The refusal failed open at its own boundary.

The traversal keeps a bound (_WAIVED_CHAIN_DEPTH_BOUND = 25) but now detects reaching it in the same query: the WITH carries length(trail) AS depth and marks a member at the maximum depth that still has an outgoing HAS_SUCCESSOR. A non-empty boundary set now raises WaivedSourceDeclarationError through the same refusal path §13 uses, naming the waived source and the identity at the bound, instead of returning the truncated set.
