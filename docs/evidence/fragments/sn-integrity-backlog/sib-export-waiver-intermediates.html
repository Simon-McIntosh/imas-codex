The waiver refusal sees every identity a waived source's chain publishes

What changed

The export waiver refusal compared only the terminal identity of a manifest source's successor chain against the cut's published set. A declared-waived source whose chain accepted an identity at an intermediate stage could therefore be published while the exemption stood. The refusal now reads each waived source's full successor-chain identity set from the graph before any staging output exists and hands it to the one waiver decision, so a published intermediate is named and the cut is refused.
