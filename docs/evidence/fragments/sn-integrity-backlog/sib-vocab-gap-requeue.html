How the sources left vocab_gap

The repository's own status-transition path is the startup maintenance call retry_vocab_gap_sources_on_grammar_change (imas_codex/standard_names/graph_store/graph_audits.py): a source whose recorded vocabulary signature differs from the live one is returned to extracted for one compose attempt under the new vocabulary. It ran at 2026-11-10 21:18:38 UTC in a peer pipeline's startup, and its own log line is the transition receipt: "vocabulary-bump retry - 15 of 15 parked source(s) returned to 'extracted' under the current ISN vocabulary". Two of the 17 were then claimed and composed by that peer run and read attached with a produced name. This node wrote no Cypher: it read the live state, recorded one receipt row per source (transition.json) and reran the sources through the CLI.

Deviation, stated plainly. The retry pass is keyed on the vocabulary signature, not on per-source resolvability, so it also revived the parked source whose token this release did not add: summary/global_quantities/current_alignment/value. It therefore stood at extracted rather than at vocab_gap as the node's brief expected, and it was excluded from the rerun scope. It now reads extracted still, reporting current_alignment_factor as the missing physical_base token, and re-parks as vocab_gap on the next vocabulary bump.
