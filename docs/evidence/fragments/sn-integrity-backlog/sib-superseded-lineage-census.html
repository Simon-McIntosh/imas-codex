Read-only census on the live graph. 2,166 StandardName nodes sit at name_stage = 'superseded'; 1,649 of them carry a successor relation and are excluded; 517 carry neither an outgoing HAS_SUCCESSOR nor an incoming REFINED_FROM and are the census. The two counts sum exactly to the population (1,649 + 517 = 2,166). The census equals the 517 the §16 close readback found, so there is no difference to state.
