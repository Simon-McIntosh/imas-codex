Landing record: the derivative children of area_of_flux_surface move to its successor; the toroidal-surface child does not

Two of the three live structural children of the superseded scalar area_of_flux_surface were renamed, through sn edit, onto its successor poloidal_plane_cross_sectional_area_of_flux_surface and both landed accepted with their sources and their spelling-derived parent carried. The third child, surface_area_of_flux_surface, is the toroidal surface area — a different quantity — and is not renamed. This record establishes where its wrong parent edge comes from and why no sanctioned route removes it: the derivation re-mints it from the spelling, so a release cannot hold. That is the one precondition §21’s done-when still needs, and it needs a code change, not a graph write.

Each renamed name carries its PRODUCED_NAME source after the rename (dd:equilibrium/time_slice/profiles_1d/darea_dpsi and …/darea_drho_tor, both composed) and its derived parent is the successor: derive_edges() returns … → poloidal_plane_cross_sectional_area_of_flux_surface (operator_kind=unary_prefix), and the graph edge agrees. The two old spellings are now superseded and no longer live children.

surface_area_of_flux_surface — where its parent comes from. ISN parses the spelling as qualifier surface (category=geometry) on base quantity area with locus of flux_surface. The derived-parent derivation peels the outer qualifier and recomposes area_of_flux_surface, so derive_edges('surface_area_of_flux_surface') returns → area_of_flux_surface (operator_kind=qualifier, operator=surface). The edge is therefore a pure function of the spelling, re-derived on every structural reconcile.

Its correct parent. The name denotes the toroidal surface area, a distinct quantity from the poloidal cross-section the catalog now attaches to area_of_flux_surface. Its natural decomposition is the locus of flux_surface on a generic surface-area quantity, but the grammar exposes only area as a base token, so surface rides as a qualifier and the peel lands on the retired generic. No live, semantically-correct structural parent exists for it: the only name that could serve — a neutral “area of flux surface” — is precisely area_of_flux_surface, which the catalog is retiring.

Why no sanctioned route removes it. Three candidate paths were checked against the code:

  • sn edit offers h2 hint / rename / docs / kind only; none releases a derived parent. Renaming is ruled out by the lead.
  • The reconcile path (NO) — rederive_structural_edges → _write_standard_name_edges (re-derives and re-mints from the spelling) and _rewire_has_parent_off_superseded (migrates an edge off a superseded parent only onto a true-rename successor that derive_edges(child) authorises). Here the tip poloidal_plane_cross_sectional_area_of_flux_surface differs in component (poloidal vs None) so it is not a true rename, and derive_edges('surface_area_of_flux_surface') authorises area_of_flux_superseded, not the tip. The edge is neither migrated nor dropped — it is left in place and re-minted. The function’s own comment prescribes the remedy for exactly this shape: un-supersede old, it is the legitimate generic parent.
  • The signed structural release (apply_signed_manifest with a recompute_projection row whose new_end_id is null) exists and is exercised on exactly this child/parent pair by tests/standard_names/test_structural_release.py — but only against a disposable graph, and it is not durable: the release deletes the edge and then calls _apply_structural_edge_reconcile → _write_standard_name_edges, which re-derives the edge from the spelling and writes it back within the same transaction. A release cannot hold.

What change it would need. One of two, both outside this node’s fragment-only scope: either (1) a lifecycle veto in the parent-admission gate (imas_codex/standard_names/parents.py::is_admissible_parent_name), so _write_standard_name_edges does not emit a HAS_PARENT edge onto a superseded target — which area_of_flux_surface is — and therefore stops re-minting it; or (2) keep area_of_flux_surface un-retired as the legitimate generic parent and exclude it from §16’s removal, which is what _rewire_has_parent_off_superseded’s comment already names as the correct remedy when a successor is not a true rename of the old.
