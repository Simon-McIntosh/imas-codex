One unit spelling per quantity

A quantity now has one recorded unit spelling, and the extraction path writes that spelling whatever the Data Dictionary spells. The census over every live name runs from the same record, so the rule, the write path and the measurement cannot drift apart.

What changed

  • The spellings: section of config/unit_overrides.yaml records one canonical spelling per quantity (torque, torque density, source rate, rate) and the differences that carry meaning (temperature K beside eV, frequency Hz beside rad.s^-1, particle flux s^-1 beside m^-2.s^-1) with the reason each stands.
  • unit_overrides.resolve_unit rewrites a Data Dictionary unit to the recorded spelling when the path belongs to a recorded quantity and the two spellings are dimensionally equal. Every other row passes through untouched.
  • audits.unit_spelling_census groups live names by quantity, partitions each quantity's spellings by dimension, and reports duplicates, divergences and name-versus-quantity dimension mismatches.
  • unit_audit gains the quantity reading the census uses: quantity_of_name, unit_dimension, units_are_dimensionally_equal, quantity_dimension_issues.

Differences that carry meaning and stand

  • temperature in K beside eV: a material or detector temperature is thermodynamic; a plasma kinetic temperature is an energy per particle.
  • frequency in Hz or s^-1 beside rad.s^-1: a cycle frequency and an angular frequency differ by 2*pi.
  • particle flux in s^-1 beside m^-2.s^-1: a wall-integrated arrival rate beside an areal flux. Whether such a name is renamed or re-united is not settled here and is passed to §5; the census lists it, the record does not unify it.

Names whose unit does not fit the quantity they state

26 names carry a unit whose dimension does not fit the quantity their head states. 43 further quantities carry more than one dimension under one head without a recorded exception. The census carries every one of them with its unit, quantity and domain in unit-spelling-census.json. They are passed to §5 as names that do not say what they are: changing a live name's unit is not changed here.

A catalog write takes the spelling on a disposable graph

The write is shown end to end on an empty disposable Neo4j instance, never on the live graph. A leaf is planted carrying kg.m^-1.s^-2 for a torque density. The extraction path rewrites its row to N.m^-2, and the catalog write — driven through its package-bound entry point, which supplies the wired graph root itself — stores the name with N.m^-2. The leaf is read back still carrying kg.m^-1.s^-2, so the stored name disagrees with the dictionary spelling by design and agrees with the record. The test asserts both readings, and it fails if the rewrite is removed. Suite log: ~/.cache/imas-codex-sdbc-unit-spelling/suite.log, 16 passed on job 1311501.

The census firing on planted cases

The census counts a quantity planted with two dimensionally equal spellings, counts a name planted with a mismatched dimension, is silent on a planted clean pair, and reports a meaning-carrying difference under the record rather than unifying it. The declared mutation — a quantity planted with two dimensionally equal unit spellings is not counted by the census — turns the census suite red, naming the duplicate spelling test among its failures.
