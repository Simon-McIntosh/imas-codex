Name-against-definition check as compared slot probes

Outcome: the endpoint is built, the bars are not met. The probe check answers the definition slot by slot and compares in code, as the design asks, and the two planted tests carry their failing controls. On the held-out labels it finds 75 % of the mismatches against the 80 % floor and flags 74 % of the agreeing names against the 10 % ceiling. The false-flag rate, not the recall, is what fails: the probes read a definition correctly and the comparison has no way to tell an extra qualifier from the name's own meaning.

What was built

imas_codex/standard_names/review/name_definition_probes.py holds the closed slot vocabulary as data: five slots (aggregation, normalisation, counting unit, restriction, weighting), a name token table per slot, and the conventions a recorded ruling states. The model is asked one closed question per slot about the definition text alone with the name withheld; code compares the answer against the name's own slots in both directions. A convention suppresses a candidate in code, so plasma_temperature and vertical_electron_velocity do not flag. The check joins the named review checks as definition_slot_disagrees_with_name and emits name_definition_probe_disagreement.

Prototype: can three probes separate the seat's misses from its false flags?

Run over the 14 names the chat seat missed and the 15 it wrongly flagged. It cannot separate them: 8 of the 14 misses are found and 11 of the 15 false flags stay flagged. Two question changes were made and are recorded here — a default option per slot (a local value is what an unstaged name means, so it adds nothing), and a sharper restriction question that asks for an explicit exclusion rather than a phrase naming what the quantity belongs to. The second change did not help: restriction flags 14 of the 29 prototype names on its own.

What the readings say

The closure and the confirming question work as intended on precision: the false-flag rate falls from 0.744 to 0.023, and the (slot, option) table drops 19 of 23 pairs. They also take the recall with them: 249 candidates become 39 flags, and the judge answers already implied on 182 of the 249. The name's implication closure is too generous — a species name is read as implying the energy channel or the device part the definition names.

The union of the seat and the probes reaches 0.778 recall at 0.087 false flags on the held-out names, and 0.782 / 0.070 over all 313: the closest any arm comes to both bars, short of the 0.80 floor by 0.80 − 0.778.

No bar was weakened and no name moved between splits. The run is reported blocked with the per-slot table above.
