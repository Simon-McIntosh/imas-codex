Name-against-definition check as compared slot probes

Outcome: the instrument is built, the bars are not met. The probe check answers the definition slot by slot and compares in code, as the design asks, and the two planted tests carry their failing controls. The comparison now closes only the name's exact implications, grades the confirming judgment instead of bucketing it, and tunes a threshold per slot on the development third. On the held-out labels the probes reach 42 % recall at a 7.6 % false-flag rate; the union with the chat seat reaches 77.8 % recall at 13.4 %. The union is the closest any arm comes to both bars: short of the 80 % recall floor by 2.2 points and over the 10 % false-flag ceiling by 3.4.

What changed in this scoring

The previous reading closed a name's implications generously — a species token was read as implying the energy channel, an object token as implying the object as a whole — and then asked the judge for a hard relation class. That over-corrected: false flags fell to 0.023 and recall to 0.250. This reading narrows the closure to the exact implications only, and converts the confirmation into a graded probability.

  • Closure. Recorded as data, and only where the name states it exactly: a density or an m⁻³ unit is per unit volume; the species' own ions are what a species name counts; the locus a position token names is local; the aggregation a transformation token names is the named one; an extensive base is its natural integral. An object token suppresses no restriction — neutral_beam_injector does not imply "only its ion-source surface", so every restriction, effective or equivalent form, weighting, band, channel, layer and branch goes to the confirming question.
  • Confirming judgment. One closed choice question per surviving candidate (name tokens, the closure in words, the definition phrase), four options, of which only narrower part and different form flag. The finding carries p_flag, the judge's summed probability for those two, instead of a hard class.
  • Thresholds. Per slot, and raised jointly: a slot threshold that keeps its own slot under the ceiling is not enough, because five slots at six percent each clear a third of the agreeing names. The levels rise one grid step at a time from the worst-contributing slot until the arm's own development false-flag rate is at or under 0.10. Each slot's own curve and the joint trace are committed in the results file.
  • (slot, option) pairs. A pair whose development precision is at or below chance is dropped even when its finding clears the threshold (20 pairs in the chat arm, 21 in the Jev arm). A dropped pair is re-admitted when it recovers a development mismatch no admitted finding already recovers and the development false-flag rate stays at or under the ceiling (3 pairs in each arm).
  • A Jev extraction arm. The same five extraction probes asked of the judge through the existing choice-question kind, one closed question per slot with the slot's own options as the criteria, the name withheld exactly as the chat probe withholds it. Its answers flow through the same comparison, closure and confirmation, so nothing downstream differs.

What the readings say

The narrowed closure and the graded judgment do put the instrument between the two earlier runs: 0.417 recall at 0.076 on the probes alone, against 0.333 / 0.099 and 0.250 / 0.023. The Jev arm extracts more conservatively (0.250 recall, 0.052) and, being two orders of magnitude cheaper per name, is the arm to run over a large population; the chat arm is the one that finds more.

The probes add little to the seat where the seat is already right and little where it is wrong: on the held-out mismatches the seat misses, the probes find one. Against this, they add 11 false flags to a union that starts at 12. That is why the union lands at 0.778 / 0.134 rather than clearing the bars, and why the honest report is that the recall floor and the false-flag ceiling are not both met by any arm in this reading.

No bar was weakened and no name moved between splits. The run is reported blocked with the readings above; the live-graph pass over every published name is not started because the bars do not hold.
