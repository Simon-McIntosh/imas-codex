A name check measured against the ceiling the labels support

Every arm is scored once, on the 1021 held-out names (244 flagged, 777 standing) that neither the adopted question nor any fitted arm saw. The same readings are scored twice: against the settled blind-reader labels, and against an adjudicated subset in which two fresh readers of different model families re-judged every name where the question and the label disagreed.

The adopted question recalls 82.0% of the flagged names at specificity 84.9%. No arm measured here improves on it. The 7 arms that buy specificity pay recall for it, and the price is not uniform: they give up between 25.0 and 81.6 points of recall for between 5.8 and 14.5 points of specificity (recall given up / specificity gained): the repository name-unit audit 81.6/14.5, the prototype pre-checks 80.3/13.1, C Drex alone 44.3/9.8, C Jev alone 33.2/9.3, B with Drex 27.5/5.8, B with Jev 25.0/6.8, F 25.0/6.8. The prototype pre-checks ahead of the adopted question gains 0.4 points of recall and loses 1.4 points of specificity. The repository audit ahead of the adopted question ties the adopted question on recall and loses 0.5 points of specificity. Scored against the adjudicated labels the same question recalls 92.6% at 93.2%, where the same comparison is given in full in the answer to the first question below.
