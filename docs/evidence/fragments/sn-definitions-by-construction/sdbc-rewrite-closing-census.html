Closing census of the standard-name wording rewrite

Every clause of the section’s done-when, measured over every live name after the rewrite. Live means name_stage != superseded and is 3,266 names. The rewriting pass ran under another node; this node measured its result, changed nothing in the census subject, and reports what it found.

What this node did not do

  • No graph write. The live graph carries a watermark, read before the first measurement and again after the last: 5,498 nodes at 2026-11-14T19:06:08.357Z both times. A write would move both. Every statement this node issued to the live graph was a MATCH.
  • No rename. All 485 names the rewrite touched kept their name. The pass rewrote definitions, so the signed rename path had no entrants and no sources or paid history could be displaced by it.
  • No model spend beyond the node cap. The review seat over all 3,266 live names cost USD 8.7992 against a node cap of USD 12.00. It is the only model call this node made; each of its costs is read per batch from the provider’s own usage record.

The seven clauses

A verdict is given per clause, and per half where a clause has two halves that land differently. A clause the evidence cannot settle is marked not met rather than argued.

Limits of this measurement

  • The wording test counts paraphrase as omission. It accepts a sentence only when it shares at least half its token set with the stored sentence; 173 of 179 operator-bearing names mention their operator, so the bucket is not absence. Both readings are recorded, and the stricter one is what the counts above use.
  • Two populations are compared. The pre-write readings are over the published catalog (1,975 names); the post-write readings are over the live graph (3,266 names). Denominators are stated on every row that needs one, and no reading is subtracted from a reading over another denominator.
  • Pre-rewrite documentation scores are not recorded for the 15 held names, so whether a score fell as a result of the rewrite cannot be settled from the evidence. That clause is reported on its listing branch.
  • Spend is per class, not per family. No per-family split exists in the evidence for this pass.
  • The 817 seat flags are counted, not adjudicated. They are reported by qualifier and listed in the census JSON; nothing here decides whether any individual flag is right.
  • Two rewritten names are no longer live (curvature_of_arc_of_circle_center, electron_number_density), so 483 of the 485 names the pass touched are in the population measured here.

How to reproduce

The census instrument committed beside this page produced every number above. It runs in four phases, each saving the census JSON before the next begins:

  • --phase live reads the live graph only, and writes the operator, family, population, unit, score and sourceless readings.
  • --phase planted starts a disposable Neo4j, plants the family, measures it and removes it.
  • --phase seat judges every live name against its definition. This is the only phase that calls a model, and its per-batch costs are recorded beside its verdicts.
  • --phase figure draws the figure from the census JSON.

Every phase was submitted to a compute partition with SLURM_JOB_ID unset, per the workstation rule that graph reads and model calls do not run on a login or fleet node. A single command reruns the whole census: --phase all.
