A reader labelled 381 published names against their definitions; 71 do not agree under the species ruling

The sample is 381 names drawn from the 1975 published at catalog v0.4.0rc8 (4a48c6c5): 371 drawn with seed 20261112 across all 28 domain files in proportion to each file's published count, with a floor of 10 per file that has that many, plus the ten names the plan's opening census cites. Each name was labelled by reading its name, description, documentation and unit together. The labels as first recorded give 270 agreement and 111 defects. The lead's ruling of 2026-11-12 — a bare species name counts ions summed over all charge states, with neutral atoms excluded — moves the 40 names whose only defect was that wording to agreement, giving 310 agreement and 71 defects. That is the headline reading; both are reported below.

The ten names the opening census cites

Nine of the ten carry the class the census gave them. The tenth, incident_neutral_particle_flux_at_wall, is recorded as unit-does-not-fit-name rather than under the census's qualifier row: the census's reading is not wrong, but the discriminating evidence is the unit (s^-1, a rate, on a name that states a flux), and the unit census already committed in this directory independently lists the same name under particle_flux s^-1. Recording it by the unit keeps this class readable as one instrument's verdict, and gives the near-zero unit count its positive control — the check demonstrably fires on a conflict that is known to be present.

Where the work is least

Read per file on the labels as first recorded, the defect share is not evenly spread, and each pattern is local to a particular domain vocabulary. The pop-ion-only convention is carried by edge_plasma_physics (14 of that file's 18 defects, 58 % of its 31 drawn) and transport (12 of its 32, 30 % of 107 drawn), and the parent pattern is almost entirely transport's (12 of 19). The eq-electron-weight convention belongs to plant_systems, which holds 10 of its 13 and runs at 65 % defects against a sample-wide 29 %. equilibrium, the third largest file at 145 published, sits at 14 %, so the defect population is not a property of the catalog as a whole but of the conventions two or three domain vocabularies adopted. Small files drawn whole (1–5 names) show shares of 50–100 % that are one or two records, not a rate.

Under the ruling the picture sharpens rather than broadens: edge_plasma_physics falls from 18 defects to 4 of 31 drawn, and transport from 32 to 20 of 107, while plant_systems is unchanged at 11 of 17. The population wording was one domain's convention; the gas-accounting wording is another's and the ruling does not touch it.

The second reading

A separate reader, with no sight of this record's labels and told the species ruling before it read, labelled the 60-name subset drawn at seed 7714 across all 28 files. Joining the two sets by name through the sheet's item id joins all 60. 45 of 60 carry the same class (75.0 %) under the ruling, and 39 of 60 (65.0 %) under the labels as first recorded. On the cruder question the labels answer first — mismatch or agreement — the two agree on 78.3 % of the subset, against 59.0 % expected by chance, giving Cohen's κ = 0.472. The agreement is moderate rather than near-perfect, and it is stated as measured.

Method and limits

  • Read-only over the published catalog YAML at /home/ITER/mcintos/Code/imas-standard-names-catalog/standard_names/*.yml, tag v0.4.0rc8, commit 4a48c6c5. No graph query was made.
  • Every label was produced by one reader reading name, description, documentation and unit together. No model judge, review check or decide_batch produced any label, so the record can serve as the reference the later check is measured against.
  • No class was added beyond the four the brief names and agrees: no single wording family outside them reached five names as a distinct defect kind.
  • The classes are a reader's judgement on prose, so a re-reading would move a few borderline records between qualifier-missing and narrower-than-name; the aggregate shares are the robust part, the per-name class of a handful of records is not. The projection assumes the sample is representative within each file, which the seeded uniform draw supports.
