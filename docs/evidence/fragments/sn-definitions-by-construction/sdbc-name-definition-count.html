A reader labelled 381 published names against their definitions; 111 do not agree

The sample is 381 names drawn from the 1975 published at catalog v0.4.0rc8 (4a48c6c5): 371 drawn with seed 20261112 across all 28 domain files in proportion to each file's published count, with a floor of 10 per file that has that many, plus the ten names the plan's opening census cites. Each name was labelled by reading its name, description, documentation and unit together. 270 agree and 111 carry one of four defects. Projected per file onto the published set, the qualifier family alone accounts for about 461 names.

The ten names the opening census cites

Nine of the ten carry the class the census gave them. The tenth, incident_neutral_particle_flux_at_wall, is recorded as unit-does-not-fit-name rather than under the census's qualifier row: the census's reading is not wrong, but the discriminating evidence is the unit (s^-1, a rate, on a name that states a flux), and the unit census already committed in this directory independently lists the same name under particle_flux s^-1. Recording it by the unit keeps this class readable as one instrument's verdict, and gives the near-zero unit count its positive control — the check demonstrably fires on a conflict that is known to be present.

Where the work is least

Read per file, the defect share is not evenly spread, and each pattern is local to a particular domain vocabulary. The pop-ion-only convention is carried by edge_plasma_physics (14 of that file's 18 defects, 58 % of its 31 drawn) and transport (12 of its 32, 30 % of 107 drawn), and the parent pattern is almost entirely transport's (12 of 19). The eq-electron-weight convention belongs to plant_systems, which holds 10 of its 13 and runs at 65 % defects against a sample-wide 29 %. equilibrium, the third largest file at 145 published, sits at 14 %, so the defect population is not a property of the catalog as a whole but of the conventions two or three domain vocabularies adopted. Small files drawn whole (1–5 names) show shares of 50–100 % that are one or two records, not a rate.

The second reading is not in this record

A 60-name subset was drawn with seed 7714, covering all 28 files, and left unlabelled for a reader who has not seen this record's labels; its indices are recorded in the JSON beside this file. No agreement figure is reported, because no independent second reading exists. A nested agent cannot be dispatched inside this run, and labelling the subset a second time here would produce agreement between a reader and itself. The done-when clause is left unmet for a separately dispatched reader, and the disagreement between the two readings is what the later check should be calibrated on.

Method and limits

  • Read-only over the published catalog YAML at /home/ITER/mcintos/Code/imas-standard-names-catalog/standard_names/*.yml, tag v0.4.0rc8, commit 4a48c6c5. No graph query was made.
  • Every label was produced by one reader reading name, description, documentation and unit together. No model judge, review check or decide_batch produced any label, so the record can serve as the reference the later check is measured against.
  • No class was added beyond the four the brief names and agrees: no single wording family outside them reached five names as a distinct defect kind.
  • The classes are a reader's judgement on prose, so a re-reading would move a few borderline records between qualifier-missing and narrower-than-name; the aggregate shares are the robust part, the per-name class of a handful of records is not. The projection assumes the sample is representative within each file, which the seeded uniform draw supports.
