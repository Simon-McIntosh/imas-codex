The species-family re-render: its three-member probe, its cost, and the placement conflict

The live re-render did not run past its probe. Two findings stop it, both measured here: the probe family averaged USD 0.073 a member, above the USD 0.05 the brief allows; and the placement the lead ruled — the family sentence first in a member's definition — contradicts the registry template-opening conformance the same review gate holds on.

The population, re-read after the renames

The stored sentences were keyed on six gas-family spellings that the equivalent-electron renames had already moved: <X>_prefill_count, <X>_source_rate_due_to_gas_injection, accumulated_<X>_count, accumulated_<X>_count_due_to_gas_injection, accumulated_<X>_prefill_count and accumulated_<X>_prefill_count_due_to_gas_injection. No live name matched those keys except the eight names the renames left un-renamed, so argon_equivalent_electron_source_rate_due_to_gas_injection — and 26 other live names — rendered no family sentence at all. The six keys now carry the renamed spellings, and a test holds each key to the spelling its live member now has.

Read from the live graph after the re-key, the store matches 23 families and 308 members. The closing census, unchanged, counts 21 families and 305 members because its family floor is three members and two of the renamed families now hold one and two members. Its reading before this node was 0 of 21 families at one wording, against the recorded 0 of 23 of the wording section's own census. After the probe family, the census reports 1 of 21 at one wording, the probe family itself.

Every member was edited with sn edit --axis docs, one at a time, which resets the docs pipeline and regenerates through the writer that installs the family sentence. The placement conflict

The lead ruled that the writer rewrites a member's opening so the family sentence comes first, and that the renderer appending it at the end is not used. The pipeline as merged does append it: the probe member's stored documentation opens with its own prose and closes with “Cumulative equivalent-electron count of the neutral helium-3 atoms delivered by gas injection as prefill before plasma breakdown.” Its description is the family sentence whole, which is what the census measures and what puts the family at one wording.

Making the family sentence the documentation opening was implemented and measured, then reverted. Two tests went red: test_house_style_checks.py::test_fixture_census_counts_each_class_once counted four template_opening findings instead of one, and test_docs_conformance_gate.py reported “documentation opens as 'Flux-surface-averaged number density of argon ions at the pl', which does not follow any selected template: Number density of {subject}”. Every averaged family's sentence begins with its operator word, and the selected registry template requires the bare quantity opening, so the two settled wordings cannot either of them be first. The review gate holds on registry_conformance, so prepending would send every member back.

This is a decision the plan does not settle: either the family sentence supersedes the template opening for a family member and the conformance check learns that exemption, or the family sentence is not the documentation's opening and the ruling means the description, which already carries it. The three probe members are left with the description rendering and the sentence appended, listed as exceptions until that is ruled.

What is left

304 members stand unedited and keep their earlier text; their scores and descriptions are recorded in family-rerender.json. Live names without a source are 10 before and 10 after, at the ceiling the brief allows. No member of the population has a Data Dictionary source that names neutral atoms or a single charge state; the audit scanned every producing source of all 308 members and returns none, with a positive control on summary/gas_injection_rates/argon/value.
