What this set is and is not

Only the 161 names the fresh readers disagreed over were re-read for a verdict. The 40 agreement controls were read blind to measure the third reader's own agreement where no dispute existed, and leave no verdict in the set. 1722 names carry no further reading at all (of 2044), so a label that is wrong where the fresh readers agreed with it is still wrong here. This set corrects the settled labels; it does not replace them with a re-reading of every name.

The third reader is openrouter/x-ai/grok-4.7 at high reasoning effort, a family distinct from both fresh readers (Anthropic and OpenAI) and from the label readers (DeepSeek and OpenAI). It read 40 of its 40 control names and agreed with the earlier readers on 38 of them; the names it split on are left_hand_circularly_polarized_wave_electric_field_real_part, parallel_neutral_internal_state_energy_flux.
