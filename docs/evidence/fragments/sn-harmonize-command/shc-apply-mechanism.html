Applying a pattern family anchor-first, then its members

Builds the apply mechanism of sn harmonize (§3 of this plan): the anchor-gated two-batch campaign for one pattern family, proven on fixtures with the pool drain stubbed. Write paths: imas_codex/cli/sn.py, imas_codex/standard_names/harmonize.py, imas_codex/standard_names/campaign.py, imas_codex/standard_names/families.py, tests/standard_names/test_harmonize_apply.py, tests/standard_names/test_harmonize_command.py.

One family, two batches, the anchor first

A pattern family has no parent node, so its members are drained through one CampaignRunner with an explicit batch plan: the anchor alone, then the remaining members. The anchor batch has one member, so its measured acceptance rate is 1.0 when the anchor reaches documentation acceptance and 0.0 otherwise — the runner's ordinary convergence gate halts the family before the member batch is ever marked or drained when the anchor did not converge. The members are then never reset and no DocsRevision snapshot is taken. A family that converges is stamped with its fresh harmonized_group_signature, so a repeated apply over the unchanged scope plans nothing.

What an apply decides, per family
DecisionConditionPlanned batches
harmonized Every member already carries the current harmonized_group_signature. none
deferred No member qualifies as an anchor (select_anchor is None). none
apply An anchor exists and the family is not yet harmonized. the anchor, then the other members

The one change to the campaign runner

The fixed-size split (plan_batches) cannot express a batch sequence that must judge the anchor before marking the members, so CampaignRunner.run gained an optional batch_plan override and the module gained anchor_gated_batches(anchor, members). No second runner was written. A one-member family yields a family with no member batch. The campaign spec's describe() now falls back to its raw string, so a hand-built spec names the change event it records.

Gates and the negative control

The gate suite is the harmonize, family-harmonization, campaign, pattern-family and Cypher-property tests. Baseline at the orientation base revision: 161 passed. After the change: 166 passed (+5, the new apply tests). The negative control removes the anchor acceptance gate (SHC_NO_ANCHOR_GATE sets the acceptance floor to zero), and test_failed_anchor_leaves_members_untouched then fails: the member batch runs when the anchor has not converged. The log is negcontrol-apply.log beside the run.
