What moved

Why a signed transition, and not the fold owner

  1. The PRODUCED_NAME move has an owner and it is not the obstacle. retarget_standard_name_sources (imas_codex/standard_names/provenance_lifecycle.py:505) moves PRODUCED_NAME, its scalar mirror produced_sn_id, the upstream HAS_STANDARD_NAME projection and both names' source_paths in one compare-and-set transaction, and verifies the count. It requires the target to be live: when the only live holder is the old name and the new name is retired it raises SourceRetiredSuccessorConflictError. That is why the revival is a precondition rather than a parallel step.

  2. The revive owner is the fold-revival inside supersede_into (imas_codex/standard_names/edit.py:2886, the writer behind imas-codex sn supersede; target-tombstone revival at edit.py:2931-2945, stage chosen by _fold_revival_stage at edit.py:2212-2231). Every donor the family offers was exercised in preview (dry_run=True, no write):

    donor (old)target (into)verdict
    distance_at_outboard_midplane radial_separatrix_distance_at_outboard_midplane ok=false — "superseded identity has missing or ambiguous fold ledger"
    radial_gap radial_separatrix_distance_at_outboard_midplane ok=false — "name 'radial_gap' has another successor lineage; fold is ambiguous"

    So there is no admissible donor in this family: one retired member lacks a clean fold ledger, the other carries a competing successor lineage. No live name outside the family may be sacrificed to revive a tombstone, because the fold destroys the donor it consumes.

  3. And even with an admissible donor, the owner's revival stage is terminal for this input. _fold_revival_stage (edit.py:2212-2231) returns target_properties["superseded_from_stage"] or "drafted", capped only by _FOLD_REVIVAL_STAGE_CAP = {"accepted": "reviewed"} (edit.py:2144). This tombstone records superseded_from_stage = "superseded", which is not a live stage and is not in the cap, so a fold would write name_stage = "superseded" while supersede_into reset the catalog status to draft — live on neither axis. The fallback at edit.py:2230 is recorded as a follow-on, not changed here.

The move, through its owner

The expected_current_bindings mapping is what makes the move a compare-and-set rather than a blind migration: a source that had moved since the readback would have failed the call instead of quietly migrating a different cohort. Follow-on

_fold_revival_stage (imas_codex/standard_names/edit.py:2230) falls back to "drafted" when superseded_from_stage is unset, but passes through a recorded value that is itself terminal ("superseded"), producing a fold that leaves the name live on neither axis. Treating a recorded-but-terminal prior stage as absent would repair every identity tombstoned twice, not only this one. It is recorded here, not changed: an owner edit changes a lifecycle rule, and this node ran under an authorisation for the signed route instead.
