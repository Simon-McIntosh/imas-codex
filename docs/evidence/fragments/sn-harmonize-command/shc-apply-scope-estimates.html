Estimates the spend of the anchor-gated family apply (§4 of this plan) by dry run, on the boundary family §3 completes and on one non-WEST family per pattern kind. Every number below is read from the live graph by imas-codex sn harmonize and the evidence script; nothing was written. The four families measured

A pattern family is a cohort of live StandardName nodes that share their invariant grammar segments and differ only along the variation axes; the pattern kind names the axis that distinguishes its members. The three chosen families each sit outside the frozen WEST roster, carry at least four members, and carry a current lint finding. The species family also exercises a template placeholder beyond {subject} and {locus}: its templates.yml entry is {modifiers} number density {locus}.

The dry run: planned calls and estimate

Each row is one imas-codex sn harmonize --family <seed> --apply --dry-run. The estimate is the planned call count times the mean measured per-call documentation cost; the basis the CLI prints is the recent LLMCost rows for the documentation seat that mean was taken over. A family that plans no calls prints no basis, because its $0.00 is exact by construction rather than extrapolated.

Total for the four families as the apply would run it: 12 planned calls, estimated $4.97. Twelve of the 34 members are actually planned: the boundary family is refused outright and the component family is already harmonized, so neither spends. The spend of the completion apply is therefore governed less by family size than by how much of each family is already harmonized or frozen.

The plan and the guard disagree, and both numbers are given because the gap is the point. Unguarded, the anchor-gated plan would batch all four families: 29 planned calls, estimated $12.01 (total_planned_calls and total_estimate_usd in the results JSON). Guarded, the recommended-name protection refuses the boundary family for its one member carrying an open catalog request, so the apply plans 12 calls, estimated $4.97. The $5.15 difference — nearly half the unguarded figure — is spend the guard prevents on a family this plan is otherwise trying to complete, which is the honest cost of the freeze rather than a defect in the estimate.

Nothing was written

Every command above is read-only. Each dry run ended with the CLI's own line, “Wrote nothing.”, and each reported decision=refused|apply|harmonized without marking, resetting or draining a member. The report rows ran through run_lint_checks and run_link_integrity, never the audit path that persists findings. The evidence script opens the graph client for reads only and returns "wrote_graph": false in its payload. All nine CLI pass logs carry EXIT=0, and each log's first line is the command it records.
