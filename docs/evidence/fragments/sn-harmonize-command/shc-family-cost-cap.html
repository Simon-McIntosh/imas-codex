One cost cap covers a whole family apply

The second forced apply over the boundary-density family drained the anchor batch and the member batch each against the whole -c/--cost-limit, so the family spent $6.259 against a $5.00 cap (anchor $1.026 + members $5.233) and stopped unstamped. The cap is now one ceiling shared by the anchor and its members, so the members are drained against whatever the anchor leaves.
