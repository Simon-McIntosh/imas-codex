Domain-scoped findings reach the family rows

The lexical lint detects mixed position forms across a physics domain's names and keys the finding on the synthetic id [domain:<d>] (review.audits.run_lexical_lint), which matches no member id. report_family_rows keyed every finding by finding.name_id, so those findings were dropped from every family row and from the scope total the plan judges later steps against. The report now splits findings into member-scoped and domain-scoped and attributes a domain-scoped finding by the domain its members carry.

The attribution and counting rule for a domain-scoped finding
WhereRule
Family rows (domain_lint) Attributed to every family in scope with at least one member whose physics_domain resolves (via domain_key) to the finding's domain — so a domain that spans families shows in each of their rows.
Scope total (lint, lint_total) Counted exactly once: at the first family in report order (-drift, -n, physical_base) that belongs to the domain. Summing the rows' lint_total is then the number of distinct findings.

The scope total the CLI prints is sum(row["lint_total"]), so counting a shared finding in one owning row keeps that figure equal to the distinct-finding count while every qualifying row still shows the finding in domain_lint.
