One domain decision per family

A name's physics domain is decided for the concept it names, and every member of a family receives that one decision. The family is the repository's pattern family with the locus held fixed: names sharing quantity, operator, process and entity, and differing only in species, charge state, component or device. The decision offers the union of every member's candidate domains, asks one Choice, stores one record on a StandardNameDomainFamily node, and writes it to every member through write_name_domain, the single writer.
