The per-family domain decision measured against blind readers

Two readings, both over the decision as it landed (decide_family_domain), run read-only against the live graph with nothing written to it: 1,247 live families scanned, every family decided once and every member reading that one decision. The reference labels are the items two readers agreed on in docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-family-labels/ (reader one labels.json, reader two labels-second-reader.json), read without sight of any stored domain. They agreed on 37 of 40 families and 64 of 73 single names (61 drawn, 3 named). The per-name arm is the domain each name already carries in the graph, not a fresh per-name decision: the per-name path is what the per-family decision replaces, so the comparison is per-family decision against stored domains. The seat was typesafe/jev-1.14 at confidence floor 0.68, and 209 judgments were paid for 218 families decided at USD 0.0297 — one judgment per family, not per member.

Calibration regression

On the 169 calibration names of sn-catalog-grouping that are live and labelled (20 lead-approved, 149 from the drawn sample), the per-family decision reaches 72.19 % accuracy against 54.44 % for the stored domains read from scg-domain-incumbent.json. It is not lower, so the regression passes. That reading files an undecided name at the stored domain it keeps, which is what the landed decision leaves the catalog holding; 36 of the 169 were left below the floor, and 20 of those 36 land on the label that way rather than by the decision's own choice. Counting every undecided name as wrong, the accuracy is 60.36 % (102 of 169) — still above the stored reading, so the regression passes on either basis and both are recorded apart in labelled-comparison.json. 92 of the 169 sit in a single-member family; the rest are members of families of up to seven, decided once for the family.

Reproducing it

Every number above is emitted by labelled-comparison-run.py, which records the revision it ran at, the seat, the confidence floor and the graph access in the artifact's provenance block. Re-running re-spends one judgment per family; --from-existing re-derives each reading from the committed rows and spends none, which is how the strict reading and the undecided summary were added to an artifact whose rows were already measured. python labelled-comparison-run.py --from-existing --output labelled-comparison.json reproduces them exactly, because they are functions of the rows.
