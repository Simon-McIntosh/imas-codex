Widening a family's domain options past its sources

Blocked. Two option sets that lift the sources' bound were built and measured against the agreed reader labels in one run each. Neither clears the section's bars: the calibration accuracy with an undecided name counted wrong falls below the 0.597 floor in both, and the count of multi-member families below the confidence floor rises instead of falling to at most 20. No bar was weakened and no name was added to or removed from the measurement basis.

What changed

The one family decision in imas_codex/standard_names/domain_decision.py gained a widened option set and an explicit bounded_by_sources switch, so both sets are measured through the same decision in the same run rather than through a second decision. widened_candidate_domains offers every domain of every band a source domain falls in, every band umbrella, and general; a source in transport therefore no longer pins the family to transport, and a concept in another band is reachable at that band's umbrella. The source-bounded set stays the shipped default because the widened sets regress the calibration reading; the widening is opt-in until a set clears the bars.

Every reading is over the same committed reference: the items both blind readers gave the same domain (37 families, 64 single names; the 3 families and 9 single names they disagree on are excluded and no disputed item was resolved). The band-widened set is the better of the two: it ties the family member share, raises the drawn single-name share by 3.3 points over the source-bounded arm and 13.1 points over the stored domains, and gives the planted name its concept domain. It still fails two bars. Its calibration accuracy is 2.96 points below the source-bounded arm and 2.3 points below the 0.597 floor, and it leaves 32 multi-member families below the floor against the bound of 20 — two more than the source-bounded arm. The two-stage set is strictly worse: splitting the choice into a band step and a domain step and multiplying the two probabilities drives 39 families below the floor and the calibration reading to 45.56 %, because the product of two decided probabilities is usually below the floor even when both stages are confident.

On the three named names the two arms agree and both match the readers: safety_factor is filed under equilibrium, greenwald_density under core_plasma_physics and voltage_of_valve under fueling. The drawn single names leave 11 of 61 below the floor under both arms, and they keep their stored domain there, so the drawn share is given over all 61. Two agreed sheet families straddle more than one decision family and are listed in the artifact rather than resolved: item-077 (the current-density pattern splits by viscosity process) and item-084 (the magnetic-probe coordinate pattern splits by probe object and by geometric base).

Spend

Two hundred and eighteen families were decided per arm; the widened arm paid 218 judgments and the source-bounded arm 209, the difference being families whose source domains already collapsed to one option and so needed no call. The band-widened run cost USD 0.0664 at USD 0.000305 per decision; the two-stage run cost USD 0.0618 at USD 0.000285; the source-bounded arm costs USD 0.0297 at USD 0.000142. Projected over the 1,247 live families, a live pass under the band-widened set costs about USD 0.38, well inside the section's USD 15 ceiling.

What is not measured, and why

The catalog-wide transport and general share, with the twenty largest moves by family, was not measured: it needs a decision over all 1,247 live families and the node's time budget did not allow it after two option sets had been measured. The catalog was never written and no disposable-copy write pass ran, so this is a missing measurement rather than a write left half done. The section's node also owes the lead's ruling on the twelve disputed sheet items; no disputed item was used here.
