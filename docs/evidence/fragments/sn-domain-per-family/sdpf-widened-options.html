Widening a family's domain options past its sources

landing: the one family domain decision now takes an option_set in {bounded, widened, fallback} and all three are measured in one run against the agreed reader labels — bounded keeps the shipped behaviour, widened offers every domain of the sources' bands to every family, and fallback pays a second, wider judgment only for a family the bounded answer leaves below the confidence floor. Fallback clears four of the five bars and misses the fifth: 23 multi-member families below the floor against a bound of 20, so the node reports blocked and no bar was weakened. Measure: the gate log at docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/sdpf-widened-options-gate.log records 14 passed / EXIT=0 on the all_debug partition, and the readings are the readings block of widened-options.json.

What changed

The decision in imas_codex/standard_names/domain_decision.py is split into a dispatcher and one helper per question shape, so all three sets ask the same question through the same code. widened_candidate_domains offers every domain of every band a source domain falls in, every band umbrella and general, so a source in transport no longer pins its family to transport and a concept in another band is reachable at that band's umbrella. The fallback set runs the bounded decision first and asks the second, wider question only for a family whose bounded answer sits below the confidence floor, so a family the narrow options already place is never re-litigated. The shipped default stays bounded: the widened sets change which domains the catalog would hold, and a set that misses a bar does not ship.

The three option sets, and their readings

Every reading is over the same committed reference: the items both blind readers gave the same domain (37 families, of which 35 hold at least three members and 2,835 names are counted; 64 single names, of which 61 are drawn; 169 calibration names). The 3 families and 9 single names the readers disagree on are excluded, and no disputed item was resolved. All three sets are decided in the same run over the same families at seat typesafe/jev-1.14, so the seat, the families and the reference are held constant. stored is the per-name domain the catalog holds today.

The fallback set is the best of the three and the only one that clears the calibration bar: 63.31 %, 2.95 points above the source-bounded arm and 3.45 above the widened set, with 28 calibration names left undecided against the source-bounded 36 and the widened 42. It also brings the multi-member undecided count down from 30 to 23 — the seven families the second judgment places — but 23 is still above the bound of 20. It gains nothing on the shares, because a family the narrow options already place keeps its bounded answer, which is by construction: the drawn single-name share is identical to the source-bounded arm at 63.93 %, against the 65.57 % the widened set reaches at the cost of its calibration. The widened set's higher single-name share and its lower accuracy are the same effect seen twice: offering a family options it does not need sometimes moves it, and about one move in five is to the wrong domain.

The planted-name bar separates the sets structurally. The widened set gives a name whose only source sits in transport the domain its concept belongs to, and the source-bounded set cannot. The fallback set also cannot, and for a reason worth stating plainly: a family whose sources collapse to a single domain takes that domain without any call, at probability 1.0, so its bounded answer is never below the floor and the wider question is never asked. The fallback therefore inherits the defect it was intended to reach around. Fixing it means treating the single-option shortcut as undecided evidence rather than as a decision, which further raises the judgment count and was not measured here.

On the three named names the arms agree and match the readers: safety_factor is filed under equilibrium, greenwald_density under core_plasma_physics and voltage_of_valve under fueling. Two agreed sheet families straddle more than one decision family and are listed in the artifact rather than resolved: item-077 (the current-density pattern splits by viscosity process) and item-084 (the magnetic-probe coordinate pattern splits by probe object and by geometric base).

The undecided count is a count of families, not of names: the largest undecided one is a family of 47 members (51 under the source-bounded set), so a family below the floor is a whole family's names left on their stored domains. That is why the count is the bar that binds and why a set that files most names correctly can still miss it.

Spend

The run decided 218 families per set. The source-bounded arm paid 209 judgments at USD 0.029723 (USD 0.00014222 per decision), the widened arm 218 at USD 0.06642 (USD 0.00030468), and the fallback arm 251 at USD 0.045767 (USD 0.00018234) — the 42 judgments above the source-bounded count are the second questions the floor triggered. The whole run cost USD 0.14191. Projected over the 1,247 live families, a live pass under the fallback set costs about USD 0.26, well inside the section's USD 15 ceiling.

The set measured and refused before these three

A band-step then domain-step set was measured first: the choice was split into a band Choice and a domain Choice and the two probabilities were multiplied. It read 47.80 % on the family member share, 62.30 % on the drawn single names, 45.56 % on calibration with undecided counted wrong, and left 39 multi-member families below the floor at 217 judgments and USD 0.061835. The product of two decided probabilities is usually below the floor even when both steps are confident, so families the narrow options had placed became undecided; it was refused. Its per-family rows were overwritten by the run that followed, so the readings are carried in the harness as refused_option_sets and in the artifact rather than as rows.

What is not measured, and why

The catalog-wide transport and general share, with the twenty largest moves by family, was not measured: the pass over all 1,247 live families runs only for a set that clears every bar, and no set does. The catalog was never written and no disposable-copy write pass ran, so this is a missing measurement rather than a write left half done.
