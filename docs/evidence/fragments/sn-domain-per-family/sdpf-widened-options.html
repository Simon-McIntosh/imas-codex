Widening a family's domain options past its sources

landing: the one family domain decision now takes an option_set in {bounded, widened, fallback, fallback_escalated} and all four are measured in one run against the agreed reader labels. bounded keeps the shipped behaviour; fallback pays a second, wider judgment only for a family the bounded answer leaves below the confidence floor; fallback_escalated is the opt-in set, adding a wider question for the families whose bounded options collapse to a single domain. The escalated set holds every reading the fallback set reaches and raises the drawn single-name share to the widened set's level, paying eight more judgments and USD 0.000728 for it. It clears four of the five bars and misses the fifth: 24 multi-member families below the floor against a bound of 20, so the node reports blocked on the undecided bound alone and no bar was weakened. Measure: the gate log at docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/sdpf-widened-options-gate.log records 17 passed / EXIT=0 on the all_debug partition, the negative control at sdpf-widened-options-negative-control.log records 4 failed / 5 passed with the widening removed, and the readings are the readings block of widened-options.json.

What changed

The decision in imas_codex/standard_names/domain_decision.py is split into a dispatcher and one helper per question shape, so all four sets ask the same question through the same code. widened_candidate_domains offers every domain of every band a source domain falls in, every band umbrella and general, so a source in transport no longer pins its family to transport and a concept in another band is reachable at that band's umbrella. The fallback sets run the bounded decision first and ask the wider question only where the bounded answer cannot place the family, so a family the narrow options already settle is never re-litigated. The escalated set removes the last place a family could be decided without a judgment: a family whose bounded options collapse to one domain is put to the wider question with that domain stated as evidence, and keeps it when the judgment puts nothing above the floor. The shipped default stays bounded: the widened sets change which domains the catalog would hold, and a set that misses a bar does not become the default.

Every reading is over the same committed reference: the items both blind readers gave the same domain (37 families, of which 35 hold at least three members and 2,835 names are counted; 64 single names, of which 61 are drawn; 169 calibration names). The 3 families and 9 single names the readers disagree on are excluded, and no disputed item was resolved. All four sets are decided in the same run over the same families at seat typesafe/jev-1.14, so the seat, the families and the reference are held constant. stored is the per-name domain the catalog holds today.

The widened set reaches the same single-name share and loses the accuracy: offering a family options it does not need sometimes moves it, and about one move in five is to the wrong domain, which is why the fallback shape exists and why it stays the candidate.

The planted-name bar separates the sets structurally, and the escalated set does not change that. The widened set gives a name whose only source sits in transport the domain its concept belongs to, and the source-bounded set keeps transport. Under both fallback shapes the bounded question speaks first, and that family's bounded options hold three domains, so the escalation does not reach it: the clause is met by the widened set and by a widened question, not by either fallback. The test asserting the section's clause as written is unchanged and runs against the widened set (test_a_single_source_does_not_bound_the_options). What the escalation does reach is the family that could be decided with no judgment at all, and that is why it was measured: see the census below.

A family's option union always holds general, so a family collapses to a single option only when it has no Data Dictionary source at all — the derived parent families. Among the agreed sheet items there are none; among the 169 calibration names there are 6 families, and all 6 move off general when the wider question is asked; in the live catalog there are 75 such families, and 35 of them move. The escalation pays 75 extra judgments at USD 0.010888 (USD 0.00014517 per decision) for that, over the whole catalog rather than over the sheet alone.

On the three named names the sets agree and match the readers: safety_factor is filed under equilibrium, greenwald_density under core_plasma_physics and voltage_of_valve under fueling. Two agreed sheet families straddle more than one decision family and are listed in the artifact rather than resolved: item-077 (the current-density pattern splits by viscosity process) and item-084 (the magnetic-probe coordinate pattern splits by probe object and by geometric base).

Spend

The source-bounded arm paid 209 judgments at USD 0.029723 (USD 0.00014222 per decision), the widened arm 218 at USD 0.06642 (USD 0.00030468), the fallback arm 251 at USD 0.045843 (USD 0.00018264), and the escalated fallback 259 at USD 0.046571 (USD 0.00017981). The four arms cost USD 0.188557 together, and the one-option census over the live catalog a further USD 0.010888, so the run cost about USD 0.20 of the section's USD 15 ceiling. Projected over the 1,247 live families, a live pass under the escalated fallback costs about USD 0.27.

The set measured and refused before these four

A band-step then domain-step set was measured first: the choice was split into a band Choice and a domain Choice and the two probabilities were multiplied. It read 47.80 % on the family member share, 62.30 % on the drawn single names, 45.56 % on calibration with undecided counted wrong, and left 39 multi-member families below the floor at 217 judgments and USD 0.061835. The product of two decided probabilities is usually below the floor even when both steps are confident, so families the narrow options had placed became undecided; it was refused. Its per-family rows were overwritten by the run that followed, so the readings are carried in the harness as refused_option_sets and in the artifact rather than as rows.

What is not measured, and why

The catalog-wide transport and general share, with the twenty largest moves by family, was not measured: the pass over all 1,247 live families runs only for a set that clears every bar, and no set does. The catalog was never written and no disposable-copy write pass ran, so this is a missing measurement rather than a write left half done.
