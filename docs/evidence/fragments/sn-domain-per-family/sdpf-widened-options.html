What changed

The decision in imas_codex/standard_names/domain_decision.py is split into a dispatcher and one helper per question shape, so all four sets ask the same question through the same code. widened_candidate_domains offers every domain of every band a source domain falls in, every band umbrella and general, so a source in transport no longer pins its family to transport and a concept in another band is reachable at that band's umbrella. The fallback sets run the bounded decision first and ask the wider question only where the bounded answer cannot place the family, so a family the narrow options already settle is never re-litigated. The escalated set removes the last place a family can be decided without a judgment: a family whose bounded options collapse to one domain is put to the wider question with that domain stated as evidence. The default is now the escalated fallback, on the lead's ruling that the undecided bound for families of two or more members is 24.

A family the floor refuses is no longer left whole and unwritten. split_undecided_family puts each member to the per-name form with the member's own sources, writes the members it can place, and leaves a member it cannot place on its stored domain; the family record stays undecided, so the family is still reported rather than quietly resolved. The test test_a_family_below_the_floor_is_held_split_across_its_members plants a three-member family whose sources carry three different domains, and asserts one per-name judgment per member, every member offered the domain its own sources carry, and every member written with that domain. Its control, test_removing_the_split_leaves_the_members_unwritten, removes the split and finds every member unwritten; the same removal run as a mutation in the gate job fails the positive test (EXIT=1).

The set measured and refused before these four

A band-step then domain-step set was measured first: the choice was split into a band Choice and a domain Choice and the two probabilities were multiplied. It read 47.80 % on the family member share, 62.30 % on the drawn single names, 45.56 % on calibration with undecided counted wrong, and left 39 multi-member families below the floor at 217 judgments and USD 0.061835. The product of two decided probabilities is usually below the floor even when both steps are confident, so families the narrow options had placed became undecided; it was refused. Its per-family rows were overwritten by the run that followed, so the readings are carried in the harness as refused_option_sets and in the artifact rather than as rows.
