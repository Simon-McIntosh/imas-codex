Docs search ordered its score ties arbitrarily

_search_docs in imas_codex/llm/search_tools.py ranked wiki chunks with sorted(set(chunk_ids), key=score, reverse=True) and the keyword term enters unbounded, so the hybrid score ties at the same value for many chunks and, where it does not, a text-only hit enters at its raw BM25 score (9 to 16, far above any cosine). The tie order was the set-iteration order, and the facility's nearest embeddings were outranked by keyword-only hits. Measured over the 18 §9 questions at the §9 label depth (k=30), with Q16 as the preservation control, using the candidate labels of search-rerank-prototype/labels.json keyed by candidate id; candidates it did not cover were labelled by the same rule and recorded in docs-score-ties/new_labels.json.

The defect reproduces

  • Q14 — 29 of the union's chunks clamp to exactly 1.0 and the answering chunk jt-60sa:use_compiler-en.html:chunk_0 (raw cosine 0.856) is among them, yet it is absent from the returned k=30 window; 26 BM25-only chunks (scores up to 16.0) sit above the entire clamp tie block.
  • Q18 — 26 chunks clamp to exactly 1.0, the answering chunk jt-60sa:use_login-en.html:chunk_0 (raw cosine 0.882) is among them, and it is absent from the returned window.

A bare cosine tie-break cannot lift the answers: they tie at 1.0, but the BM25-only chunks outscore the whole tie block, so tie-breaking within it changes nothing at k=30. Only a reserved-vector-slot change moves them, because the answering chunks are the facility's nearest embeddings and the reservation gives them a rank regardless of the keyword term. The deployed variant keeps every question's top 10 exactly (Q16 unchanged at 0.9675) and lifts both answers into the top 30, and its tie-break makes the tie order deterministic by cosine instead of set iteration. The no-clamp + reserved variant scores higher still but changes the displayed score range (values above 1.0) beyond this node's goal, so it was not deployed.
