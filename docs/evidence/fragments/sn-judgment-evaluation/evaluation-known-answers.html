Known answers for copy-group resampling and the paired-difference interval

tests/discovery/test_evaluation.py now pins the two capabilities the library lacks. Copy-group resampling is checked to keep near-duplicate copies together: resampling by copy group must give a wider interval than resampling by observation at a fixed seed, and collapsing every copy of an item into one group must reproduce the interval of the de-duplicated data exactly. The paired-difference interval is checked with arms whose answers are known independently of the code: a perfectly separating arm against its reverse gives a difference of exactly 1.0, identical arms give a difference of 0.0 bracketed by its interval, and on correlated arms the paired interval is narrower than a difference of two independently resampled arms.
