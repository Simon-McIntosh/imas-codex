Why the sheet was rebalanced, and how. As drawn, the sheet's acceptable share was 29.5%, below the plan's 35% floor. The reserve exists for this. The coordinator labelled reserve rows in draw order until 28 acceptable ones were found, then swapped each in for one not-acceptable sheet row. The removed row is the latest-drawn not-acceptable sheet row of the same pattern family, falling back to the same physics domain and then to any. No lead-overlap row was removed. Each swapped-in row carries replaces_draw_rank. The rebalancing selects on the label, so the golden's prevalence is not the catalog's: as drawn, about 30% of accepted documentation passes the rubric. Prevalence-dependent figures measured on this golden (accuracy, precision) must be read against 36.5%, not 30%.

What the labels say about the catalog. Four in five rejections are mechanical: a missed template opening, a symbol other than the registry's, or a bracketed reference that leads nowhere. A gate that runs the existing template_opening lint, a symbol check against the registry notation, and link resolution at write time would have refused them before review. The semantic fifth is where a judge earns its cost: a name bound to a DD path that measures something else (a detector outline under an aperture name, a pellet centre-of-mass velocity under a fragment name, a squared field intensity under a magnitude), a false statement (a safety-factor relation off by 2π, a vacuum vector potential with no plasma contribution), or a general name narrowed to one diagnostic.

Registry defects the labelling exposed

Some house-style failures are the registry's fault rather than the writer's. The labels follow the rubric and fail the row, but the repair belongs in the registry:

  • radial means the major-radius direction, but it is applied to flux-coordinate transport names (radial_electron_energy_diffusion_coefficient, radial_ion_momentum_diffusivity). Their text is right for the source and wrong for the registry. Earlier rows that followed the registry into the major-radius reading failed subject instead.
  • flux is Γ, a particle flux, but energy-flux names in W m−2 select it (maximum_of_back_surface_neutron_flux, flux_at_limiter, energy_flux_at_divertor_target). Writers use q, correctly, and fail.
  • radius is R, which collides with the major radius in every minor-radius, aperture and filament equation; writers use r or a and fail.
  • ellipticity is e, which collides with the electron subscript.
  • The template lint has no word boundary. {modifiers} count becomes .+ count, which passes "a common counting convention"; a hyphenated "current-density" fails {component} current density {locus}.

Unit defects

Three rows fail physics because the documented quantity's dimensions disagree with the unit the name carries: neutron-detector direction cosines in m (y_direction_unit_vector_of_neutron_detector, z_direction_unit_vector_of_neutron_detector) and an unnormalized potential amplitude with unit 1 (perturbed_electrostatic_potential_amplitude). A fourth, vertical_magnetization_of_ferritic_element in A m−2, is bound to a magnetic moment, A m2, and documents a magnetization, A m−1.
