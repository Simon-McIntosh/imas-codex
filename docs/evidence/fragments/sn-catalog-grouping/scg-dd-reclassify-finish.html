The run

One pass per IDS group, through the driver scg-dd-reclassify-finish-run.py. Each group was reset once, on its first pass, and never twice. A pass writes its classification once, after every batch completes, so a pass cut off before its write leaves the graph unchanged and loses only its spend. The summary pass ran alone because a reset triggers a graph-wide re-embed and cluster phase that two concurrent passes would contend on; each group pass was stopped after its own classify phase for the same reason.

Spend

The node overspent its allowance. Its nine pass logs carry 234 billed classifier calls summing to USD 31.78, against an allowance of USD 14.27 and a prior spend of USD 15.73 — a cumulative USD 47.51 against a USD 30.00 ceiling, over by USD 17.51.

The cause is visible in the shape of the run: the group passes were dispatched in parallel waves to fit them alongside the summary pass and under the time fence, so cost was measured only after it had been incurred, and the brief's stop-before-the-projection-crosses-the-ceiling check had no pass boundary to act on. The largest single contributor was the summary pass at USD 5.80 (1103 Tier-1 paths); the eight groups together account for about USD 25.98. The work is complete and correct; the cost control is what failed, and it is the reason this node is held rather than closed.
