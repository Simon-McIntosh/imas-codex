Pattern families — grammar-identical cohorts of live standard names

A pattern family is the set of live standard names whose grammar parses identically except along one variation axis. The invariant segments are physical_base, geometric_base, transformation and process; the variation axes are subject, component, coordinate, position, region and device. An empty segment is a value of its axis, so a parent with no locus and its locus-qualified variants share a family. transformation is deliberately invariant: it carries the reduction operator, so a reduced quantity and its unreduced parent are joined only by the explicit HAS_PARENT route.

The definition lives in imas_codex/standard_names/families.py (pattern_signature, fetch_pattern_family, fetch_family_base, count_pattern_families). fetch_sibling_family (context) and assemble_family (harmonize) read it; both keep their return shapes, so workers.py and cli/sn.py are unchanged. The physical_base fallback in assemble_family is retired; the family-eligible HAS_PARENT route is retained for operator-derived children.
