A source IDS contributes once to each domain and band it touches. When every classified source is in one band, at least two domains occur, and no domain exceeds the threshold share of distinct IDS, the rule selects that band's umbrella. When sources span bands and no band exceeds the threshold, it selects general. Otherwise it uses the base selector. Thresholds are 0.50, 0.60 and 0.667. A tie at the threshold is not a majority.

Umbrella choices from guidance scopes

BandUmbrellaScope and limitation
core_physicscore_plasma_physicsWhat a plasma is in its core: the values of its particle and energy distributions, the profiles built from them, and the global scalars integrated from those profiles, whether measured or modelled. Judge by whether the quantity is a state of the plasma rather than a rate of change, a diagnostic response or a data product.
heating_current_driveauxiliary_heatingThe external systems that heat a plasma and their launch and coupling quantities, judged by whether the quantity belongs to a heating actuator or to the power it delivers. The heating method may be beams or waves; what decides the domain is that the quantity is about the injected power, not the transport it later causes.
edgeedge_plasma_physicsThe physics of the plasma outside the core and its boundary: the scrape-off layer, the pedestal, the edge transport barrier and the flows that carry plasma to the walls. Judge by whether the quantity describes plasma in the edge region rather than the core or a material surface.
diagnosticsplasma_measurement_diagnosticsGeneral plasma-parameter measurement instruments that do not belong to one of the more specific diagnostic families. Judge by whether the instrument measures a plasma parameter directly and has no stronger home above.
operations_engineeringmachine_operationsThe operation of the machine around a discharge: pulse scheduling, the shot cycle, workflow orchestration and operational metadata. Judge by whether the quantity concerns when and whether the machine runs rather than the physics it produces.
datadata_managementThe dataset as such rather than the physics it records: dataset description, provenance, FAIR metadata and the archives kept for downstream use. Judge by whether the quantity says something about a dataset — what it contains, where it came from, when it was written — rather than about the plasma. A quantity that names a physical quantity takes that quantity's domain even when it is stored under a container such as `summary`, because the container records where the value is filed and not what it measures.
generalgeneralQuantities that genuinely appear across many domains or carry no single physics subject, so no narrower domain fits. Hold this back: it is a last resort, and a path whose subject is knowable belongs in that subject's domain.

These are testable umbrella proxies, not name-level production overrides. The edge, heating, operations and data scopes each cover only part of their band; the measurement tests whether that loss of specificity helps the labelled names.
