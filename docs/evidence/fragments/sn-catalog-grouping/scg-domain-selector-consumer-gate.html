Consumer gate: does the declared-order domain selector break a standard-name caller

The declared-order selector was measured against the whole tests/standard_names/ directory at the base revision (before the selector change) and at its head. Twelve test ids that pass at the base fail at the head. Every one fails the same way: the new select_primary_domain raises ValueError on a domain string that is not a PhysicsDomain member — eleven refuse core_profiles (an IDS name), one refuses magnetics.
