First-level question: physics_category

Choose the one category of physics or machine function this IMAS Data Dictionary path belongs to, judging what the quantity is rather than which IDS carries it.

Each option is a category with its guidance scope — 10 options

core_plasma
The physics of the confined plasma itself: how it is shaped, what it is made of, how it moves energy and particles within itself, and how it sets its own field. Choose this first when the quantity is a property of the plasma column, not of a device, a diagnostic or a data product.
heating_current_drive
The systems and physics that add energy or momentum to the plasma and the waves that carry that power. Choose this when the quantity belongs to an actuator that heats or drives the plasma, or to the wave physics that transports its power to the particles.
edge_divertor
The plasma boundary and the surfaces it touches: the scrape-off layer, the divertor, plasma-wall interaction and fueling of the boundary. Choose this when the quantity concerns plasma outside the core, or the material surfaces that plasma meets.
energetic_particles
Populations far from the thermal bulk: fast ions, fast electrons and runaway electrons, their distributions, slowing-down, and their losses.
diagnostics
The measurement systems of the machine and the quantities they produce. Choose this when the quantity is a diagnostic response or the instrument that records it, rather than a plasma state, even where it is calibrated to a physical quantity.
control_operations
Real-time control, pulse scheduling and the operational metadata of running a discharge: what the control system commands and when, in an equilibrium or a shot cycle.
machine_systems
The engineering plant and auxiliary systems of the device rather than its plasma: magnets and coil systems, structural components and balance of plant.
data_computing
Data products and computational infrastructure: archived and summary results, atomic and molecular reference data, geometry definitions and workflow tooling that carries no single physics subject.
general
Quantities that genuinely appear across many domains or carry no single physics subject. A held-back last resort, never a default for a path whose subject is knowable.
advanced_physics
Specialised theory or simulation frameworks whose quantities describe a model world rather than the measured machine, and do not reduce to one experimental domain.

Second-level question: physics_domain

Asked within the core_plasma category, the one this path's first level leads to. Each option states the domain's ISN one-line description, its guidance scope, the quantity kinds it includes and the exclusions that separate it from a named neighbour.

Choose the one physics domain within the 'core_plasma' category this IMAS Data Dictionary path belongs to, judging what the quantity measures rather than the IDS it lives under.

Each option is a domain of that category with its guidance — 5 options

equilibrium
MHD equilibrium, flux surfaces, magnetic geometry, coordinate systems
The static magnetic configuration of a plasma in force balance and the coordinate system it is expressed in: the flux-surface geometry, the poloidal flux and its radial derivatives, the safety factor, and the shape of the boundary. Judge by whether the quantity states where the field is and what shape its surfaces have, not by whether it is stored with a time coordinate.
Includes: flux-surface geometry — boundary outline, major and minor radius, elongation, triangularity; poloidal flux and its radial derivative — `equilibrium/time_slice/profiles_1d/psi`, `psi_norm`; safety factor and magnetic shear along the profile; the equilibrium reconstruction itself — the currents and coils fitted to it; coordinate systems and the mapping between real space and flux space — `equilibrium/time_slice/profiles_1d/rho_tor`, `equilibrium/grids_ggd`
Excludes a heat, particle or momentum flux, a diffusivity or a confinement time (→ transport): A quantity that says how fast the plasma redistributes energy or particles — a flux, a diffusion coefficient, a confinement time — is transport; equilibrium holds only the field's shape and the mapping to it, never a flow across it.
Excludes the numerical value of a kinetic profile at a surface, such as the classical electron temperature (→ core_plasma_physics): The amplitude of a temperature or density profile is a property of the plasma, so it is core_plasma_physics; equilibrium owns the surface on which that value sits, not the value.
Excludes a growing instability, its growth rate or its stability limit (→ magnetohydrodynamics): A mode that destabilises the configuration is magnetohydrodynamics; a property of the converged, static configuration is equilibrium.
transport
Transport coefficients, fluxes, neoclassical and anomalous transport modeling
The rates and sources by which a plasma redistributes particles, energy and momentum, and the coefficients and model parameters that predict them. Judge by whether the quantity measures or models a transport rate, not by the IDS that happens to carry it — an equilibrium IDS with a diffusion model still holds transport quantities.
Includes: particle, energy and momentum fluxes, including their decomposed parts and errors; diffusivities, conductivities and pinch velocities from neoclassical or anomalous models; source and sink terms for the transport equations — `core_sources/source/profiles_1d/*`; flux-surface-averaged transport and the transport solver's own parameters — `plasma_transport/*`; heat and particle confinement times
Excludes a static shape property of the magnetic field, such as the safety factor or the separatrix position (→ equilibrium): If the quantity describes the configuration's geometry rather than a movement of particles or energy through it, it is equilibrium. A transport model that references the safety factor does not make that factor transport.
Excludes the value of a kinetic profile such as temperature or density (→ core_plasma_physics): The plasma's own profile value is core_plasma_physics; a flux or diffusivity derived from it is transport.
Excludes a turbulent fluctuation amplitude, spectrum or correlation length (→ turbulence): The fluctuating field and its spectrum are turbulence; the mean flux it produces crosses into transport.
core_plasma_physics
Core plasma profiles, kinetic quantities, global parameters, integrated quantities
What a plasma is in its core: the values of its particle and energy distributions, the profiles built from them, and the global scalars integrated from those profiles, whether measured or modelled. Judge by whether the quantity is a state of the plasma rather than a rate of change, a diagnostic response or a data product.
Includes: electron and ion temperature, density and pressure profiles — `core_profiles/profiles_1d/electrons/temperature`; toroidal and poloidal rotation and current-density profiles; composition, impurity and effective-charge profiles; moments of the distribution function — density, mean velocity, pressure tensor; global scalars integrated from the profiles — stored energy, plasma current, line-averaged density
Excludes a transport coefficient, flux or source term (→ transport): How the plasma moves energy or particles is transport; the state it is in is core_plasma_physics. A profile's gradient is core; the flux that gradient drives is transport.
Excludes a raw diagnostic measurement that infers a profile, such as a Thomson scattering spectrum or a line-integrated chord (→ particle_measurement_diagnostics): The instrument response and its calibrated signal are a diagnostic domain; the profile derived from it is core_plasma_physics.
Excludes a geometry definition or a data-product wrapper (→ computational_workflow): A core-plasma quantity is core_plasma_physics even when its IDS is named like a profile container or carries solver numerics; the domain follows the subject of the quantity, never the folder it is filed under.
turbulence
Turbulence physics, fluctuations, turbulent transport, correlation analysis
The fluctuating part of a plasma's fields and distributions: the amplitude, spectrum and correlation structure of turbulent fluctuations in density, temperature, potential and magnetic field. Judge by whether the quantity describes a fluctuation or its wavenumber-frequency spectrum rather than a mean value or a mean flux.
Includes: fluctuation amplitudes and their spatial or wavenumber spectra — `turbulence/*`; correlation lengths and times, and cross-phase between fluctuating fields; radial and poloidal flux spectra of the turbulent fields; the turbulent-transport model inputs that describe the fluctuations themselves
Excludes a mean heat or particle flux produced by the turbulence (→ transport): The fluctuation and its spectrum are turbulence; once the quantity is the mean flux those fluctuations drive, it is transport — the essence of transport is the average, not the fluctuation.
Excludes a gyrokinetic simulation quantity from a gyrokinetics IDS (→ gyrokinetics): A quantity expressed in the gyrokinetic model's own coordinates (gyrocenter moments, normalised fluxes) is gyrokinetics, even when it is turbulent in character.
Excludes an MHD mode amplitude or growth rate (→ magnetohydrodynamics): A coherent, macroscale unstable mode is magnetohydrodynamics; the broadband turbulent fluctuation is turbulence.
magnetohydrodynamics
MHD stability, disruptions, instabilities, sawteeth, tearing modes
The stability and macroscopic dynamics of a plasma as a conducting fluid: instabilities, their growth and saturation, magnetic reconnection and the events a loss of stability produces. Judge by whether the quantity describes a coherent mode, its mode numbers and growth, or the evolution of a disruption.
Includes: linear and non-linear mode amplitudes, mode numbers, growth rates and mode frequencies; tearing, kink, ballooning and neoclassical tearing-mode quantities; disruption quantities — the thermal quench, current quench and their timing; sawtooth and other macroscopic relaxation events — `sawteeth/*`, `ntms/*`, `mhd/*`
Excludes a static property of the equilibrium the mode sits in (→ equilibrium): The shape of the field is equilibrium; a mode that grows on it and its stability is magnetohydrodynamics.
Excludes a turbulent broadband fluctuation (→ turbulence): A single coherent mode with definite mode numbers is magnetohydrodynamics; a broadband turbulent spectrum is turbulence.
Excludes a plasma-control command that reacts to a stability event (→ plasma_control): What the mode does is magnetohydrodynamics; what the controller commands in response is plasma_control.
