The eleven new misses are all summary/*, and they all land on data_management — the IDS's own subsystem domain rather than the quantity's physics: summary/fusion/neutron_rates/total/value reads data_management against a label of neutronics, and the five summary/global_quantities/* and summary/gas_injection_rates/* paths do the same against core_plasma_physics and fueling. That is a classifier behaviour worth its own node, not a labelling defect: a container IDS whose children are copies of other IDSs' quantities is being classified by its container rather than by the quantity.

The earlier mismatches are the opposite shape — a coordinate path taking its parent's subsystem domain, e.g. core_profiles/profiles_1d/grid/rho_pol_norm reads transport against a label of equilibrium.
