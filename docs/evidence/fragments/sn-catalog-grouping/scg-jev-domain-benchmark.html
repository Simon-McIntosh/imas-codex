The typed-Choice domain classifier does not match the chat classifier: do not adopt

Verdict: do not adopt. Its spend of USD 0.0506 for 856 paths clears the cost criterion by a wide margin; the accuracy criterion fails, so the rule fails. Every one of the 856 paths was answered and nothing was written to the graph.

Reproduction

The recorded answers sit in scg-jev-domain-benchmark-results.json under domains and probabilities (probabilities rounded to four places). A scoring pass over that file with no --fresh flag makes no paid call and rewrites a byte-identical results file (scg-jev-domain-benchmark-run.py --cache scg-jev-domain-benchmark-results.json); the scorer is positive-controlled by scoring the recorded chat answers and requiring the source-set and stored counts to equal the published ones.
