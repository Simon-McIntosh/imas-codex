Physics domain classification guidance

The lead should read imas_codex/definitions/physics/domains.yaml directly, as the lead read concepts.yml: this record shows four entries in full and lists what grounded them, but the file is the deliverable.

Four entries in full

transport

The rates and sources by which a plasma redistributes particles, energy and momentum, and the coefficients and model parameters that predict them. Judge by whether the quantity measures or models a transport rate, not by the IDS that happens to carry it — an equilibrium IDS with a diffusion model still holds transport quantities.

includes

  • particle, energy and momentum fluxes, including their decomposed parts and errors
  • diffusivities, conductivities and pinch velocities from neoclassical or anomalous models
  • source and sink terms for the transport equations — `core_sources/source/profiles_1d/*`
  • flux-surface-averaged transport and the transport solver's own parameters — `plasma_transport/*`
  • heat and particle confinement times

excludes

  • a static shape property of the magnetic field, such as the safety factor or the separatrix position → equilibrium
    If the quantity describes the configuration's geometry rather than a movement of particles or energy through it, it is equilibrium. A transport model that references the safety factor does not make that factor transport.
  • the value of a kinetic profile such as temperature or density → core_plasma_physics
    The plasma's own profile value is core_plasma_physics; a flux or diffusivity derived from it is transport.
  • a turbulent fluctuation amplitude, spectrum or correlation length → turbulence
    The fluctuating field and its spectrum are turbulence; the mean flux it produces crosses into transport.

equilibrium

The static magnetic configuration of a plasma in force balance and the coordinate system it is expressed in: the flux-surface geometry, the poloidal flux and its radial derivatives, the safety factor, and the shape of the boundary. Judge by whether the quantity states where the field is and what shape its surfaces have, not by whether it is stored with a time coordinate.

includes

  • flux-surface geometry — boundary outline, major and minor radius, elongation, triangularity
  • poloidal flux and its radial derivative — `equilibrium/time_slice/profiles_1d/psi`, `psi_norm`
  • safety factor and magnetic shear along the profile
  • the equilibrium reconstruction itself — the currents and coils fitted to it
  • coordinate systems and the mapping between real space and flux space — `equilibrium/time_slice/profiles_1d/rho_tor`, `equilibrium/grids_ggd`

excludes

  • a heat, particle or momentum flux, a diffusivity or a confinement time → transport
    A quantity that says how fast the plasma redistributes energy or particles — a flux, a diffusion coefficient, a confinement time — is transport; equilibrium holds only the field's shape and the mapping to it, never a flow across it.
  • the numerical value of a kinetic profile at a surface, such as the classical electron temperature → core_plasma_physics
    The amplitude of a temperature or density profile is a property of the plasma, so it is core_plasma_physics; equilibrium owns the surface on which that value sits, not the value.
  • a growing instability, its growth rate or its stability limit → magnetohydrodynamics
    A mode that destabilises the configuration is magnetohydrodynamics; a property of the converged, static configuration is equilibrium.

core_plasma_physics

What a plasma is in its core: the values of its particle and energy distributions, the profiles built from them, and the global scalars integrated from those profiles, whether measured or modelled. Judge by whether the quantity is a state of the plasma rather than a rate of change, a diagnostic response or a data product.

includes

  • electron and ion temperature, density and pressure profiles — `core_profiles/profiles_1d/electrons/temperature`
  • toroidal and poloidal rotation and current-density profiles
  • composition, impurity and effective-charge profiles
  • moments of the distribution function — density, mean velocity, pressure tensor
  • global scalars integrated from the profiles — stored energy, plasma current, line-averaged density

excludes

  • a transport coefficient, flux or source term → transport
    How the plasma moves energy or particles is transport; the state it is in is core_plasma_physics. A profile's gradient is core; the flux that gradient drives is transport.
  • a raw diagnostic measurement that infers a profile, such as a Thomson scattering spectrum or a line-integrated chord → particle_measurement_diagnostics
    The instrument response and its calibrated signal are a diagnostic domain; the profile derived from it is core_plasma_physics.
  • a geometry definition or a data-product wrapper → computational_workflow
    A core-plasma quantity is core_plasma_physics even when its IDS is named like a profile container or carries solver numerics; the domain follows the subject of the quantity, never the folder it is filed under.

computational_workflow

Computational and auxiliary infrastructure: atomic and molecular data, geometry definitions, generic temporary containers and the numerics that support a solver. Judge by whether the quantity is tooling or reference data rather than physics.

includes

  • atomic and molecular data services — `amns_data/*`
  • geometry definitions and computational geometry — `geometry/*`
  • generic and temporary containers — `temporary/*`
  • solver numerics and workflow tooling that carry no physics subject

excludes

  • a physics quantity that a numeric or profile container happens to hold → core_plasma_physics
    A physics quantity is its own domain even when it is stored in a solver-numerics or temporary container. The failing case this file exists to separate: a kinetic profile or a fast-particle distribution must not fall to computational_workflow merely because its IDS also carries numerics.
  • an archived summary product → data_management
    Tooling and reference data are computational_workflow; a summary product assembled from a run is data_management.
  • a structural geometry of a real component → structural_components
    A computational geometry definition is computational_workflow; the structure of a real component is structural_components.
