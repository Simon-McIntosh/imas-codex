Which speed controls held

  • Conditional review: an exact target-label copy may skip review; word overlap and existing citation IDs cannot prove support. The observed cohort skipped zero reviews, so its elapsed-time difference is not evidence of a gating speed benefit. A positive unit control exercises the literal-copy branch. This option remains off.
  • Pooled reviews: context IDs are namespaced per request, indices remapped, missing or duplicate coverage refused, feedback retained and shared usage counted once. A two-second deadline flushes the tail. Calls fell to 281, but delivery fell to 197 and wall time rose relative to individual review at the same width. Three terminal refusals report review must refer to unique existing draft statements. The option remains off.
  • Review reasoning off: the fast 2989.90-second projection delivers 200 and parses 199, but a separately sealed twenty-case subset of the original forty contains four wrong, two partly right and fourteen right. Four wrong already exceeds the whole-forty limit. It is rejected; review reasoning stays on.
  • Concurrency: three in-flight signals meet delivery, raw parsing and both quality bars. Eight deliver all 200 but parse only 190 and take longer in this run. Both widths remain below the published generation gate of twelve, recorded in admission receipts. A seat-specific max-workers = 3 makes the measured width reproducible in the real worker group; lower caller counts and other seats retain their behavior.
  • Schema-constrained decoding: this extra arm delivers only 190 and parses 95 first responses. The server aborts several responses, and all 190 delivered targets reach the literal fallback. The raw audit finds 194 statement objects without citation items across the constrained review responses, so their text is refused. The selected ordinary-review arm provides a positive instrument control: 199 raw statement objects do carry citations. These counts include replacement responses; they are not additional delivered descriptions. It fails the delivery and parsing bars; the setting remains off.
