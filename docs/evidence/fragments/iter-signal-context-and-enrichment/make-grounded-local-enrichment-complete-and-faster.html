Recovery mechanism and controls

The new per-seat controls leave other seats unchanged. A failed structured call or coverage check may make a bounded replacement call with its actual validation failure. Only the failed draft or review is repeated. A provider budget refusal and a telemetry refusal propagate. Where filtering or review removes every statement, the fallback copies the first supported statement of that exact signal's catalogue label. It does not reuse the rejected draft, borrow another signal's label or infer a meaning from a tag. Its index and source-item identity are marked in the stage result and its context quality stays low.

Conditional review admits only a literal copy of the target's catalogue label after case, punctuation and whitespace normalization. Word overlap and citation existence are insufficient. Pooled reviews namespace every context item and remap signal identities, refuse duplicate or missing coverage, preserve validation feedback, allocate consumed token counts once and flush an incomplete group within two seconds. The existing review reasoning switch is measured independently.
