3142 live names were read in one pass at stage accepted, drafted, pending, reviewed. Reading each name’s group from the data structures of its sources, 884 settle in their stored group (814 from their structures and 70 with no data structure to read), 1946 move to the group their sources’ structures name, and 312 span more than one group. transport holds 1042 live names; the division places 950 of them in 5 parts and lists 92 with a reason. No stored domain changed; the parts are put to the lead as a choice.

The rule, stated before its numbers

A source is a Data Dictionary path. Its structure is the pair (IDS, first structural container), for example core_profiles/profiles_1d or core_transport/model. A structure names a group: the four quantity classes the plan’s opening paragraph names (profile_quantities from profiles_1d/profiles_2d/ggd/ggd_fluid, source_quantities from source, distribution_quantities from distribution(s), summary_quantities from the summary IDS and the global_quantities container) and transport_coefficients from the model container of a transport IDS; every other structure takes the domain the Data Dictionary’s own IDS map gives its IDS. A name with DD sources reads its sources’ structures; a name with none is a derived parent and unions its live children’s readings, guarded against cycles. A name whose closures read one group is settled, and is moved when that group is not its stored domain; a name whose closures read more than one group spans; a name with nothing to read keeps its stored group and is counted apart. The three partition buckets therefore sum to the live names read. Nothing is written.
