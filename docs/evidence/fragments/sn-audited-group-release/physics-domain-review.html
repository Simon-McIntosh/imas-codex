3. The proposed list

Each entry names either a physics area or a grouping of data structures. The five quantity-form parts carry the transport division the lead ruled; every proposed domain has a description, a category, an IDS list, and scope, includes and excludes in the form domains.yaml uses.

auxiliary_heating physics_area kept

Reason. Names the heating actuators and their hardware.

Description. Auxiliary heating systems including neutral beam, electron cyclotron, ion cyclotron, and lower hybrid

Category. heating_current_drive   Count. 199

IDS. ec_antennas, ec_launchers, ic_antennas, lh_antennas, nbi

Scope. The external systems that heat a plasma and their launch and coupling quantities, judged by whether the quantity belongs to a heating actuator or to the power it delivers. The heating method may be beams or waves; what decides the domain is that the quantity is about the injected power, not the transport it later causes.

Includes. neutral-beam injection quantities — beam power, energy, current drive by injection — `nbi/*`; electron, ion cyclotron and lower-hybrid launch quantities — `ec_launchers/*`, `ic_antennas/*`, `lh_antennas/*`; antenna and launcher hardware, and the coupling of power to the plasma; absorbed and launched heating power, and the heating system's efficiency

Excludes. a wave field propagating in the plasma as such → waves: The antenna and the power it delivers are auxiliary_heating; the wave it launches, its dispersion and its wave-particle interaction inside the plasma are waves.; a non-inductive current driven by the heating → current_drive: The deposition of heat is auxiliary_heating; the driven current and its efficiency and control are current_drive.; a plant component that supplies the heating system → plant_systems: A supply or cooling element serving an antenna is a plant component, plant_systems; the antenna and its launched power are auxiliary_heating.

core_plasma_physics physics_area kept

Reason. Names the core plasma state, narrowed to quantities that are not a transport coefficient, a source or a profile form.

Description. Core plasma profiles, kinetic quantities, global parameters, integrated quantities

Category. core_plasma   Count. 48

IDS. core_instant_changes, plasma_profiles, radiation

Scope. What a plasma is in its core: the values of its particle and energy distributions, the profiles built from them, and the global scalars integrated from those profiles, whether measured or modelled. Judge by whether the quantity is a state of the plasma rather than a rate of change, a diagnostic response or a data product.

Includes. electron and ion temperature, density and pressure profiles — `core_profiles/profiles_1d/electrons/temperature`; toroidal and poloidal rotation and current-density profiles; composition, impurity and effective-charge profiles; moments of the distribution function — density, mean velocity, pressure tensor; global scalars integrated from the profiles — stored energy, plasma current, line-averaged density

Excludes. a charge-exchange channel's ion concentration and charge state → spectroscopy: A charge-exchange channel's ion concentration and charge state are spectroscopy; the charge_exchange IDS is a spectroscopic diagnostic.; an interferometer's electron line density → electromagnetic_wave_diagnostics: An interferometer's electron line density is electromagnetic_wave_diagnostics; the interferometer IDS is the wave-probe family.; power radiated, taken as an integral over the plasma → radiation_measurement_diagnostics: Power radiated as an integral over the plasma is radiation_measurement_diagnostics; a bolometer's own quantity is the family's, not core_plasma_physics.; a transport coefficient, flux or source term → transport: How the plasma moves energy or particles is transport; the state it is in is core_plasma_physics. A profile's gradient is core; the flux that gradient drives is transport.; a raw diagnostic measurement that infers a profile, such as a Thomson scattering spectrum or a line-integrated chord → particle_measurement_diagnostics: The instrument response and its calibrated signal are a diagnostic domain; the profile derived from it is core_plasma_physics.; a geometry definition or a data-product wrapper → computational_workflow: A core-plasma quantity is core_plasma_physics even when its IDS is named like a profile container or carries solver numerics; the domain follows the subject of the quantity, never the folder it is filed under.

current_drive physics_area kept

Reason. Names non-inductive current generation, a physics distinct from the heating that accompanies it; its ten names are current-drive quantities, not heating-system ones.

Description. Non-inductive current drive physics, efficiency, and profile control

Category. heating_current_drive   Count. 10

IDS. —

Scope. The physics and profiles of current driven in the plasma by non-inductive means and the control of the current profile. Judge by whether the quantity states an amount of driven current, its efficiency, or the target current profile, rather than the power that drove it.

Includes. driven current density and its profiles — `nbi/*/current_drive`, `ec_launchers/*/current_drive`; current-drive efficiency and the figure of merit for a drive method; the target and achieved current profile and its control; bootstrap and other self-generated current fractions

Excludes. the injected power or deposition of the heating system → auxiliary_heating: The energy that was launched is auxiliary_heating; the current it drove is current_drive. Both may come from one beam, and the quantity's subject decides.; the total plasma current as a global plasma parameter → core_plasma_physics: The total current the plasma carries is a global state of the plasma, core_plasma_physics; the part of it driven externally and its efficiency are current_drive.; an ohmic-field loop voltage from the central solenoid → magnetic_field_systems: The transformer that induces the current is a machine system; the current it drives in the plasma is current_drive.
