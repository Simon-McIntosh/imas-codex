Where the ruling and the audit records are read from

The bar is the lead's ruling of 2026-11-17, committed as imas_codex/standard_names/release_bar.json beside the package and read on every host, so a checkout cannot cut against a bar its own environment supplies; IMAS_CODEX_SN_GROUP_RELEASE_BAR or [tool.imas-codex.sn].group-release-bar override the path only for a bar being tried before a ruling lands, and a document that is missing or carries no numeric max_defect_rate refuses with release_bar_not_ruled rather than reading as looser. The document carries max_defect_rate (required, numeric; ruled 0), class_maxima (per defect class; 0 for all seven), defect_exceptions (member ids a ruling lets stand although the adjudication read them defective or could not decide them - the one way past the audit), sourceless_exceptions, family_exceptions (the 225 spanning families section 2 ruled exceptions for the first cut, carrying the ids that measurement published - the id is the measurement's own form, families.family_identifier over the parent path, the family type and the sorted member paths, and a test recomputes all 225 from the measurement data file and asserts the committed list is exactly that set, so a re-framing that would leave every committed id unmatched fails before it lands) and groups_cut_together. A class the ruling gives no maximum of its own is governed by max_defect_rate, so a zero bar cannot leave a class unguarded. The audit record is one <group>.json under the configured audit directory, one row per member carrying a verdict and the digest of the text read - the digest is the claim ledger's own combined content hash of description and documentation (content_hashes.combined_sha256, taken through the same hashing service), so a name's text has one identity and an audit can be compared with a claim record rather than holding a second, incomparable spelling of it; the verdict is sound, defective with at least one defect class, or undecided, and a record that names no verdict or reads a member defective without a class cannot be read, so the group refuses as never measured rather than being read as clean. The rate the bar reads is the adjudicated defective share over every member, uncorrected, with undecided members counted defective and reported as their own share.

What the ruling states, and what is still open

  • max_defect_rate: ruled 0 on 2026-11-17 - a group may carry no known defect.
  • class_maxima: ruled 0 for every defect class; the ruling reads the rate with the members the adjudication cannot decide counted defective.
  • defect_exceptions: member ids the ruling lets stand despite a defective or undecided verdict. None is ruled yet, so every adjudication verdict stands.
  • sourceless_exceptions: none is ruled yet for the first group; section 4's repair either binds a source or brings the member to the lead.
  • family_exceptions: section 2's ruling gives every family that spans groups as an exception for the first cut, and the document carries the 225 ids that measurement published.
  • groups_cut_together: none is ruled; no pair is released as one cut.
