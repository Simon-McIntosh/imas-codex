Transport divided along the data dictionary’s structures

3142 live names were read in one pass, and each name’s group was read from the data structures of its sources: the structure is the path’s (IDS, first structural container), the five quantity classes name their structures and every other structure takes its IDS’s domain. 884 names settle in their stored group (814 from their structures, 70 with no data structure to read), 1946 move to the group their sources’ structures name, and 312 span more than one group; the three sum to the live names read. transport is 1042 live names and divides into 5 parts named for the quantities their structures hold, with 950 placed and 92 listed with a reason. The 498 DD families are recounted on one family set: 225 span stored domains, the count section 2 records, and 274 would span under the rule, 81 of them across two parts. No stored domain moved; the proposal is put to the lead as a choice. The full evidence page is data-structure-groups.html and its data file is data-structure-groups.json.
