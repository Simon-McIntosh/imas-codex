Three shared defect causes traced to the stage that makes them

Reading the 208 judgments of the equilibrium adjudication gives 14 causes shared by three or more defective members, 89 defect instances in all — the plan's figures exactly. The three largest are traced here to the stage that produces each: physical_scope_qualifier_lost (18 members) to the naming (compose) stage, source_bindings_conflate_distinct_definitions (13) to the source-binding attach gate, and boundary_forced_to_lcfs (11) to the locus-token binding and its documentation injection. 12 traced members are shown from their recorded packets, four per cause, each with the line of the record that states the qualifier the artifact loses. The remaining eleven causes are listed with their stage and whether the same repair reaches them: 3 covered, 4 partial, 4 needing their own repair. No product source changed and the graph was not written.

Full page: stage-cause-trace.html. Trace data: stage-cause-trace.json. The page is rendered from that data by stage-cause-trace.py, whose --check re-derives every claim from the adjudication record and from the product source.

How the trace is checked

The check re-reads the adjudication record and the product source, prints one line per traced member, and exits 1 if fewer than 9 are printed. It fails when a traced member's recorded stage disagrees with the stage table, when a quoted line leaves the packet, when the recorded name changes, when the definition starts carrying the operation it was accused of dropping, when the names-mode template starts rendering the lineage, when the binding guard gains a text parameter, or when the locus registry redefines plasma_boundary. Three mutations were run against it in a shadow tree: altering one traced member's stage, adding an ancestor render to the names-mode prompt, and adding a dd_documentation parameter to the attachment guard. All three exit 1 with the reason; the unmutated shadow tree exits 0.
