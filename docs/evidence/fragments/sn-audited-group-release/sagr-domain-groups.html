Sensitivity of the order to the measure

Across the ranked groups the M1 order against M2 correlates at Spearman ρ = 0.99, and against M3 at ρ = 1.00; M2 against M3 at ρ = 0.99. The largest single move is 4 places under M2 and 0 under M3. The order M1 gives is what the proposal uses; the move columns beside each group's numbers show where a ruling that prefers M2 or M3 would put it instead.

What M1 does and does not measure: the schema says a name's stored primary domain is promoted from the domains of the sources attached to it, and a catalog following that rule would settle every name that records a source. The live catalog does not: 1173 names carry a primary that is none of their recorded source domains, and the promotion rule would not produce the largest of those shapes, so the stored primary and the recorded source set are written by paths that do not reconcile with each other. A settled name is a name whose stored domain agrees with its recorded provenance, not a name filed under the right domain, and a group whose members all draw on one domain settles by construction: gyrokinetics settles 42 of 42 names and section 1 still read 3 of the names it sampled there as wrong-domain. The dominant shapes are transport over core_plasma_physics sources, 291 names; transport over fast_particles sources, 192 names; transport over edge_plasma_physics sources, 160 names. A name whose sources are core_plasma_physics paths but whose stored domain is transport is a flux or a coefficient named for what it does while its source sits elsewhere — the disagreement the plan's opening paragraph already describes, counted here rather than judged. Settlement is the consistency measure and section 1's reading is the independent one; the order puts the internally consistent groups first, and the wrong-domain counts are the caution on all of them.

What section 1 read as wrong-domain, by group

The counts are joined from the section-1 audit records (blind-audit.json, 339 names read), matched to a group by the name's stored domain. No group rate is stated, and none can be. A group contributes 14 or 15 names to that sample, and a group with no wrong-domain name at all would still carry a 95 % interval of 0–20.4 %, 20.4 pp wide: section 1's own rule — a rate only where the interval is narrower than twenty percentage points — admits no group. A group's rate is measured when the group is read in full, as sections 4 and 5 do.

The join matches every name it should: 15 of the 15 names section 1 reads as wrong-domain by at least one reader carry a group row, so the zeros in the per-group column are read after the join is shown to see names known to be present. No name it read as wrong-domain has dropped out of the live catalog; 0 of the audit's 40 planted rows appear among the sampled records, so the planted names enter no count above.
