Shot selection. JET's imas_codex/config/facilities/jet.yaml declares 14 base device configurations from shot 68613 onward, and its magnetics indexr selects calibration/position files by shot; a JEC2020 first_shot=79951 alone is not an end-of-validity claim. JT-60SA maps/jt-60sa/machine_map.json selects OP1 for 100001–101173 and OP2 for 101174–999999. The OP1 magnetic channels were observed on E100383 and later OP1 pulses; the OP2 description is inherited from the phase deck, with no acquired OP2 EDDB shot in the cited record. The OP1/OP2 receipt.json files record source hashes and deck line ranges. Applying either phase's geometry to another shot range requires the phase selector, not the accessor's example shot.

Join limits. The JET PPF accessors identify arrays, while the geometry names individual array elements. The array/index join is deterministic at the identity level, but no graph edge currently records it, and the live graph has no SignalSource for any of the seven selected JET measurement records above. The JT-60SA digit-pattern grouping pattern merges channels beyond the available deck rows; attaching geometry to that whole source without checking each MEMBER_OF edge would mislabel 6 probes and 7 loops. The counts above count a source group as fully joined only when every one of its measured members has geometry.
