Registry conformance checks in the review audits

The deterministic checks read the ISN conventions registry through select_conventions and raise findings in four classes: a forbidden variant of a selected concept's written equation, a forbidden locus gloss, a forbidden spelling of a selected quantity's symbol, and a documentation opening that does not follow the selected pattern template. The forbidden locus gloss arrives through the same concept-variant mechanism as the equation variant, distinguished by whether the match lies inside a display equation. Findings join the convention_violation family and are appended to the AuditReport by run_all_audits; each names the registry entry it came from.
