The conventions registry reaches imas-codex through the ISN accessor

tests/standard_names/test_conventions_registry.py calls imas_standard_names.grammar.terms.select_conventions for flux_surface_averaged_argon_density_at_plasma_boundary and asserts the four entries the parse selects, identified by key alone: the concepts flux_surface_averaged and plasma_boundary, the notation density, and the density template over the subject segment. imas-codex holds no copy of the registry; the entries come from the shipped files under the installed grammar package.

The registry is pointed at another directory through the loader's own load_conventions(directory=…) argument — no monkeypatch is needed. The control test builds a scratch copy under tmp_path, deletes the flux_surface_averaged concept entry, and confirms the accessor then returns concepts ('plasma_boundary',) alone. Pointing the positive assertion at that scratch registry is the declared mutation, and it fails on concepts — ('plasma_boundary',) against ('flux_surface_averaged', 'plasma_boundary').
