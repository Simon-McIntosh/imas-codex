The rename rewrites the old name wherever it appears. Three directories keep their names at cutover, and a fourth family of spellings must still be rewritten. A kept context per directory is the only mechanism that can tell them apart, because the shape of the name is the same and only its position says which it is.

Reckoning with the earlier independent apportionment of the kept list

The three paths were previously retained by a broader rule (the reckon-spa-mount context kept any occurrence preceded by a leading slash) which protected them incidentally while also protecting the four roots that must move. Narrowing that rule and naming these three directories explicitly is what makes the difference between them a decision rather than an accident of where a slash sits.
