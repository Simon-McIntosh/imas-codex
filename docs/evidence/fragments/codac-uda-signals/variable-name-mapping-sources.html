Recommendation and limits

Consume full archive metadata alongside type metadata: they tie at 200/200 record availability, while full metadata contributes the measured labels and units. Preserve attribute names, values, empty-versus-absent outcomes and source provenance. Treat the naming convention as a parser authority, SDD or signal lists as prospective definition authorities, and ICP as the prospective component authority. They cannot be ranked against archive metadata until their authenticated reading paths and positive controls are measured.

The unresolved requirement is a demonstrated pipeline path to ICP, plus authenticated document or SDD access sufficient to run source-bound known controls and the same 200-selector intersection. Supply an authorized non-interactive read client or a versioned export and one known matching variable per source, then repeat this survey. The current record does not establish exhaustive source discovery or a complete semantic mapping.
