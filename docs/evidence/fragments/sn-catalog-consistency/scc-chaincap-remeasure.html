Chain-cap identities with no sourced ancestor, re-measured

The 2026-10-12 census recorded 92 chain-cap identities whose refinement lineage reaches no sourced name; it predates the orphan, sourceless-draft and lineage-cycle retirements, so it was re-read on the live graph at commit fc99c61f0. 73 chain-cap identities have no sourced ancestor, and none of them is among those a whole-catalog cut would publish. The 73 are carried by no producer edge, so their only route into a cut is a live structural child, and the one that reaches the export population at all is held by a release-authority rule. The section therefore closes on this reading, as the section's own done-when provides, with no lead choice between rebuilding provenance and holding the identities from the cut yet required.

The definitions, carried unchanged

  • chain-cap — sn.chain_length >= 3.
  • unsourced — NOT EXISTS { MATCH (:StandardNameSource)-[:PRODUCED_NAME]->(sn) }.
  • has a sourced ancestor — EXISTS { MATCH (sn)-[:REFINED_FROM*1..10]->(a:StandardName) WHERE EXISTS { MATCH (:StandardNameSource)-[:PRODUCED_NAME]->(a) } }.
  • would publish — the export gate's own population read, eligibility classifier and score gate, at a whole-catalog cut's defaults (min_score 0.65, include_unreviewed false, no domain restriction, names_only false). The instrument calls _fetch_export_population, _classify_export_population and _run_gate_c rather than restating their rules.

The publish boundary

A whole-catalog cut reads 3,004 identities into its population, classifies 1,683 of them eligible, and publishes 1,682 after the score gate. Exactly one identity from the 73 reaches that population:

The mechanism is structural rather than incidental: an unsourced identity has no PRODUCED_NAME edge, so the export gate's producing-source test can only be satisfied for it by a live structural child, and the one identity that carries one is held by a release-authority rule before eligibility is reached. The remaining 72 are absent from the population at the name_stage boundary, so they never reach a cut.

The instrument

The 2026-10-12 census left no run script, so this one is committed beside this record: scc-chaincap-remeasure-run.py, with its recorded measures in scc-chaincap-remeasure.json. Three modes: capture reads the live graph, control starts the disposable instance and plants the fixtures, verify re-asserts the accounting over a recorded capture. capture writes the summary beside the instrument and, when --out is given, the complete capture there: the full capture names every published identity and every exclusion reason at 429 kB, so it is kept in the run directory rather than in the repository, and the summary carries every number this record cites. 

python docs/evidence/fragments/sn-catalog-consistency/scc-chaincap-remeasure-run.py --mode control
python docs/evidence/fragments/sn-catalog-consistency/scc-chaincap-remeasure-run.py --mode capture

Result

73 chain-cap identities with no sourced ancestor; 0 of them are among those a whole-catalog cut would publish. The census's planted control fires. Every census query names its row set (the StandardName label filtered by chain-cap and the unsourced predicate) and every ancestry walk is depth-bounded at *1..10. The capture recorded every statement's elapsed time itself: the slowest was 0.025 s, two orders of magnitude below the ten-second ceiling, and the whole mode ran inside the one-hour debug partition window. Spend USD 0: no LLM call was made and nothing was captured except by MATCH.
