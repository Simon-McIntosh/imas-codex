A stratified 202-signal JT-60SA sample, reprocessed end to end

A 202-signal sample stratified by source category across all five JT-60SA scanners was reset once to discovered and re-enriched through the current pipeline, without a reset. All 202 signals came back at enriched with a description, an embedding whose text hash matches its own description, and a signal role — none left behind. The run's own cost record reads $0.17 for 1106 signals reprocessed, so the facility's 15,111 signals project to $2.32 of Jev spend and about 1.7 hours on that basis, or $12.72 and 9.4 hours taken over the sample signals alone. Total spend for the node was $0.1709.

The 17 grouped members in the sample — those whose source carries more than one member — show the before and after side by side: a template that named coil 01 sensor 01 for every member of a 144-member strain-gauge group became a description naming that member's own channel, and the same for a 361-member daily-neutron-yield group. The evidence, the census and its negative control are in reprocess.html.
