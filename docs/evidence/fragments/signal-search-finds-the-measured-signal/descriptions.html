JT-60SA signal descriptions after a boundary-respecting glossary

The glossary that steers signal enrichment matched its terms as case-folded substrings of the catalogue text, so a two- or three-letter abbreviation reached every signal whose path happened to spell it: DR reached 522 signals and EFC reached 203, including ones named cryohrs_cdpt171 and refcurEF1. This node replaces the substring test with one that holds a term to whole words and camel-case boundaries, adds the JT-60SA abbreviations the census showed were missing or wrong (SNU, EFC, BPS, QPC, DR, HTS, FPPCC), and re-enriches the signals the census flagged under that glossary.

A term begins a word at the start of the text, after a non-alphanumeric, at a lower-case-to-upper-case turn, and where a capitalised word follows a capital or a digit. That last clause is what keeps the reach of the abbreviation terms: HiTe reaches curCS1HiTe across the digit, and the UFP of curUFPdisr opens onto the word after it, because an acronym written as capitals may do so. A digit inside a token's capital run still starts nothing, so the DR of mC01DR ("radial displacement") stays out of the dump-resistor line, and a capitalised ordinary word that merely begins with the letters does not admit its term.

Description census, before and after re-enrichment

The census is restricted to the signals each abbreviation actually belongs to: the family is the set of eligible signals whose id or name reaches the term under the boundary matcher — 182 for EFC/EFCC, 56 for SNU. Restricting it matters: a case-folded efc selector also pulls in refcuref1bst ("EF1 booster power supply") and the CS1–CS4 base supplies, which are equilibrium field and central solenoid supplies, not error field correction at all.

How the seven CS1 members were reached. Seven CS1 signals — cs1_temsnur12, 13, 14, 211, 212, 221, 222 — read "snubber (SNU)" while their representative cs1_temsnur11 read switching network unit, because the member-propagation path bounded a SignalSource through its representative's own MEMBER_OF edge. A representative may itself be a member of a different source, and this one is, so the sweep and the propagation each matched the wrong source or none. The path now locates a source by its representative_id, and propagation clears members_described so the post-enrichment individualisation pass is re-armed rather than skipping a source it believes it has already described. Resetting those seven members to discovered and re-running the focused pass propagated the corrected description to all of them: the census now reads 49 switching network unit, 0 snubber, 0 superconducting unit.

The seven descriptions that still use SNU without expanding it are the EF3 switching-network signals ef3_temsnur11–ef3_temsnur22 and psc_crcs3snu. None expands the abbreviation as anything else, so no description in the family now reads SNU as anything but the switching network unit.

The 26 EFC-family-adjacent signals the raw census could not label, and the 5 it first recorded as "converter or equilibrium field", are all outside the family once the selector honours boundaries: the equilibrium-field booster and base current references refcuref1bst … refcuref6bse, the CS1–CS4 base supplies, the FPPCC power-supply references and the DHC/PSRC configuration references. Their descriptions name the supplies they are.

The matcher and its tests

glossary_term_reaches(term, text) accepts a match that begins a word — at the start, after a non-alphanumeric, at a term that begins a capitalised word after a capital or a digit, or at a lower-case-to-upper-case turn — and does not run into a following lower-case run unless the term is itself written as capitals. The boundary test passes against it and fails against the substring matcher: with the substring test restored, two tests fail (test_the_boundary_matcher_holds_a_term_to_whole_words, test_a_coil_abbreviation_reaches_its_own_signal_not_a_lookalike) and the declared string holds — the substring test admits EFC reaching refcurEF1.
