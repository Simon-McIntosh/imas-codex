§5 — the golden harness on the re-embedded graph

After every jt-60sa signal's stored vector was re-embedded, the four-arm table on §3's harness refused to score: the new vectors place candidates in an arm's top 10 that the fixture does not label. All 29 such candidates are read against their question's intent and graded into tests/llm/fixtures/signal_search_questions.json (1,675 labels become 1,704). The table test then scores every arm, in two runs back to back at 93b083114.

That is 0.0027 beyond the 0.012 run-to-run spread §3 records, so the "no lower beyond the spread" criterion is not met. The cause is the graph and not the labels: scoring the same current_tool rankings against the fixture as it stood before this node gives the same 0.7773, because none of the 29 new labels sits in current_tool's or rrf's top 10. The re-embedded graph's RRF score also lands on §5's in-memory description-only measurement (0.7773 stored against 0.7761 in memory), where §3's stale stored vectors scored 0.7963 against that same in-memory 0.7761. This reads as the stale vectors having been favoured by labels pooled from their own top tens, and the fresh vectors scoring what a fresh embedding scores; it is a reading of those numbers, not a separate measurement.

The frozen additive arm sorts a set by score, so candidates tied on score reach its top 10 in an order that changes with the process hash seed. A first labelling pass left the table test passing at one seed and refusing at the next (cryoctb_mt14nfv, then psrc_calbr2, then cryoctb_mt02nfv); those three were labelled after enumerating 24 seeds, and 64 further seeds (100 to 163) then surfaced no unlabelled candidate in any of the four arms. Only additive_rule varies; the other three arms were identical at every seed. Making the arm deterministic is a change to the test module outside this node's scope (follow-on).

jt-60sa:general/psc_cref3snu is labelled, but on the settled graph it is in no arm's top 10: it ranks 53 in the vector stream, 18 in the full-text stream and 12 after fusion on switching-network-unit-terminal-voltage, three runs in a row.
