Each enriched signal stores its measured role

The role judge accepted a role for a signal but nothing wrote it down, so a searcher could not filter on it and a reset could not clear it. This node gives FacilitySignal a signal_role slot whose range is an enum of the six roles, has signal enrichment judge each signal's role after its description is written and store the accepted role, and adds a sweep that judges enriched signals carrying a description but no role.

The sweep and its census share one predicate — a signal is a candidate when status is enriched or checked, its description is non-empty, and signal_role is null — so the sweep this node runs is exactly the population the census counts, the same property the re-embed sweep and its hash census hold. A judgment that failed, or returned a choice outside the six roles, stores nothing: the signal keeps no role and stays in the sweep's population for a later run. The reset to discovered clears signal_role alongside description, so a reprocessed signal is judged again on its new text.

The live sweep took the first 20 eligible JT-60SA signals and stored a role on every one: 20/20 written, 0 failed. Jev response-usage spend was USD 0.000552, or USD 0.0000276 per signal. The sample is the eight lcdb_a048717_ate.s003 reconstruction-map settings and their twelve companion metadata fields, and every one carries configuration_parameter — the judged role the stored text itself names, so this is the judge reading its own text correctly rather than a collapsed distribution.
