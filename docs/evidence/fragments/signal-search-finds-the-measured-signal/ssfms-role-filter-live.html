A live role=measured search keeps commands and references out

Twenty-two graded physics questions were each searched against the live graph through the served signal search with the rerank off (_search_signals, the body of the search_signals tool), once with role="measured" and once with no role. Each question carries hand grades from the fixture (tests/llm/fixtures/signal_search_questions.json): 3 measures the queried quantity, 2 is a commanded or reference value of it or the same quantity at an adjacent point, 1 is related hardware, 0 is read and judged irrelevant. Every signal also carries the role the enrichment judge stored for it.

Headline

  • Filtered results carrying a role other than measured: 0.
  • Filtered top-5 positions holding a graded-2 command or reference: 0.
  • Questions whose unfiltered top 5 holds a graded-2 command or reference: 4 of 22.

The control questions are plasma-current-total, booster-power-supply-output-voltage, toroidal-field-coil-current, toroidal-field-coil-voltage. On each, the unfiltered search places a signal stored as a commanded or reference_or_setpoint value in its top 5; the filtered search removes it, and no such signal reaches a filtered top 5 on any question.
