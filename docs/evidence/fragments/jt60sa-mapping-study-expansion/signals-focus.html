Signals discovery gains --focus so a cohort enriches only its named sources

discover <facility> --only signals --focus <id> now scopes enrichment and check claims to the named signal or SignalSource identities, resolved through the shared resolve_focus_items (manifests included). The previous unconditional refusal is gone.

What changed. imas_codex/discovery/signals/parallel.py gains build_focus_predicate, rendered into the four claim queries (enrichment channel-skip and claim, check claim) and the two pending-work probes, and threaded as focus_items through the claim functions, both workers and the engine entry point. An empty focus renders no predicate, leaving the claim unfiltered. imas_codex/cli/discover/signals.py replaces the refusal with resolve_focus_items plus a new _validate_focus that refuses an item naming no signal or source at the facility, naming the offending identities.

What a focus item names. A focus item names a SignalSource either directly by its own id, or through a handle one of its members carries: a signal id, a signal accessor, or a source array — the trailing data_source_path segment a study names, such as magPbTC10. Every member of a named source is claimed, so naming one member of a grouped source reaches the whole group; a focus item that is itself a signal id or accessor also claims that signal directly. This is what makes the study's --focus magPbTC10 reach the magPbTCNNN source, whose id is the digit-normalized accessor key rather than the array name a human writes.

Measure. The focused suite tests/discovery/test_signals_focus.py (13 tests) passes with -n 8: 13 passed in 16.98s, EXIT=0. Adjacent signals modules (test_signals_pipeline, test_parallel, test_signal_checking, test_signal_check_carry_forward, test_singleton_sources) pass 137 passed, EXIT=0.

Live graph check. Against the JT-60SA graph, the rendered predicate --focus magPbTC10 selects exactly the members of the two sources it names — jt-60sa:eddbreadTime('ENNN', 'MDAC', 'magPbTCNNN', t1, t2) and its PSRC twin — 28 signals out of the 10,432 discovered at the facility. The same predicate with the full MDAC magPbTCNNN source id selects those 14 members, and with the singleton member id jt-60sa:general/mdac_magpbtc1 selects that one signal, so a named source reaches every member and nothing else.
