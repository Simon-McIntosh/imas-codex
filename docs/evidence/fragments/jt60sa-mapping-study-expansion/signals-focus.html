Signals discovery gains --focus so a cohort enriches only its named sources

discover <facility> --only signals --focus <id> now scopes enrichment and check claims to the named signal or SignalSource identities, resolved through the shared resolve_focus_items (manifests included). The previous unconditional refusal is gone.

What changed. imas_codex/discovery/signals/parallel.py gains build_focus_predicate, rendered into the four claim queries (enrichment channel-skip and claim, check claim) and the two pending-work probes, and threaded as focus_items through the claim functions, both workers and the engine entry point. An empty focus renders no predicate, leaving the claim unfiltered. imas_codex/cli/discover/signals.py replaces the refusal with resolve_focus_items plus a new _validate_focus that refuses an item naming no signal or source at the facility, naming the offending identities.

Measure. The focused suite tests/discovery/test_signals_focus.py (12 tests) passes with -n 8: 12 passed in 16.73s, EXIT=0. Adjacent signals modules (test_signals_pipeline, test_parallel, test_signal_checking, test_signal_check_carry_forward, test_singleton_sources) pass 137 passed.

Negative control, and it held. Dropping the focus filter from the claim query (build_focus_predicate returning empty) turns the suite red: 6 failed, 6 passed, including test_unnamed_source_in_the_same_facility_stays_unclaimed for both the enrichment and check claims — the unnamed source's signal is then admitted.
