Hold contract

A StandardName owns zero or more ReviewHold nodes through HAS_HOLD. Each hold records axis, kind, finding, evidence, raised-by, raised-at, status, a stable SHA-256 finding key, and optional triage identity, time and reason. The key hashes the kind and whitespace-normalized, case-folded finding; its name-scoped node ID makes raising the same finding idempotent. A dismissed key stays dismissed when raised again. A different finding creates a new hold. Clearing is restricted to kinds the completed review checked and keys it did not raise. Dismissal requires a triager and reason.

The owner in review/holds.py is the only hold graph reader and writer. Its acceptance predicate requires a judged score at or above the supplied minimum and no open docs hold. Clearing or dismissing the last hold promotes a passing reviewed document. The Cypher fragment for an open docs hold is reused in refinement eligibility, so a passing-score document can be claimed for refinement while its hold remains open.
