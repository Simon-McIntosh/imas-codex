Deployment rule and receipt

A Jev judge replaces an incumbent decision only when all four hold:

  • On the golden’s validation split, the lower 95% bound of the paired AUC difference (Jev minus incumbent) is above the margin locked below. The incumbent is reported per model and as the quorum, since it is six models.
  • Its operating point was chosen on the calibration split.
  • Its option-order flip rate and frozen-state variation are reported and inside bounds the prototype states.
  • Its cost per decision is lower.

The locked margin is −0.02 AUC at the 95% lower bound.

The validation quorum interval and several model intervals fail the AUC condition. The 19 missing graph descriptions and the unrun audit-only checks further limit a full replacement claim. Per-name answers, states, costs and latencies are in the run’s name-decisions.jsonl; the committed results JSON carries every figure and the rescoring script recomputes the JSON, fragment and SVG from those receipts.
