Deviation from the brief, and why

The brief asks for 120 cases and for the total to stay under USD 12. At the production seat these two cannot both hold. The refine seat costs $0.127 to $0.138 per call with reasoning at high, so the brief's 240 calls price at roughly USD 30 to 33 — the ceiling is reached at 89 calls, part-way through the cohort.

The spend ceiling was treated as the binding constraint, because the brief states it unconditionally ("at most USD 12 in total… stop and report before crossing it") while the cohort size is a target the draw serves. Dispatch therefore stopped before the ceiling: 91 calls were never made, and 51 cohort cases have one arm or none. 39 cases carry both arms and are the material this run contributes. The remaining 51 are resumable from the per-call receipts, which are appended as each call returns, so no spend is lost to the stop.

Sheet check, and its negative control

The check reads the committed sheet, fails if any row carries an unexpected field or any field that would disclose the arm or the reviewer's judgment, and then plants a row carrying arm and requires that the detector catch it — an empty or over-permissive check would otherwise report a safety it does not provide.
