The predicate defect, and what it cost

The first measurement of arm A lost six planted contradictions to the label-only predicate alone, and that was a defect rather than a trade. The predicate removed the whole DD source whose text was only a field label. A label repeats a coordinate the source path already carries, so the row's path went with the label, and a claim fixing a direction was then compared against nothing at all. What changed in the product

The question text is the base revision's, unchanged. label_only_source is true when a DD source's every word belongs to the vocabulary of field placement, so the text names no quantity; comparison_sources empties that text and leaves the source in place, and a claim left with no evidence at all still answers "says nothing". The deterministic settlement of a locus-or-aggregation disclaimer, which the first record of this retune kept, is removed: it is the arm that lowers recall, and no arm that lowers recall may stay. claim_contradicts and CONTRADICTION_VETO_PROBABILITY (0.30) are unchanged, and nothing in either measurement implicates the threshold.
