What changed

All of it in imas_codex/standard_names/review/jev_review.py. A new label_only_source predicate is true when a DD source's every word belongs to the vocabulary of field placement, so the text names no quantity. comparison_sources drops those sources and build_docs_state records the remainder as the claim's evidence, unobserved when none is left, so the claim answers "says nothing". docs_question_table's source-comparison instruction and its contradicts criterion now state that specialisation is compatible and that a contradiction is a source that denies the claim: a different quantity, an opposite sign or direction, an incompatible unit or dimension, or a different definition. claim_contradicts and CONTRADICTION_VETO_PROBABILITY were left unchanged, and the measurement does not implicate the threshold: the new arm's surviving firings lose the veto through the answer's probability, not through a threshold miss.
