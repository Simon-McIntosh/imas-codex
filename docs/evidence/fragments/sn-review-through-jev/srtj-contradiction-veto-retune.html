The settlement, and the case that sets its limit

A sentence saying that the quantity does not prescribe, impose, require or mandate a locus, an averaging or an aggregation carries nothing a source can contradict: the quantity holds wherever it is measured, so a source that names one locus of it agrees rather than denies. Code answers those sentences "says nothing" with settled_by: "non_requirement_sentence", and the judge is never asked. Two conditions bound it: the documented name must carry no locus or aggregation qualifier of its own, and the sentence must state a non-requirement rather than assert the quantity's own combination. The second condition is what the wider settlement lacked, and contradicts-484 is the row that measures it:

The name is itself an average along a field line and the source states the averaging, so the claim negates both. The wider settlement read "without downstream averaging" as a disclaimer rather than as an assertion and answered it "says nothing", which is how arm B lost the row. B2 sends it to the judge on two independent grounds: the name carries the averaging (_average), and the sentence asserts the quantity's own combination (without … averaging, only the upstream value). Sentences that assert a combination — is not averaged, pointwise, excludes, omits — are never settled, whatever their name.

The predicate defect, and what it cost

The first measurement lost six planted contradictions to the label-only predicate alone, and that was a defect rather than a trade. The predicate removed the whole DD source whose text was only a field label. A label repeats a coordinate the source path already carries, so the row's path went with the label, and a claim fixing a direction was then compared against nothing at all. Seven planted rows have a field label as the whole of their source text; all are rule direction, and in every one the denying evidence is the source path. Six of the seven moved from missed to recalled when the fix landed, and the seventh, contradicts-251 (camera_ir/channel/camera/direction/x), is the one the count does not move.

What changed in the product

The question text is the base revision's, unchanged. label_only_source is true when a DD source's every word belongs to the vocabulary of field placement, so the text names no quantity; comparison_sources empties that text and leaves the source in place, and a claim left with no evidence at all still answers "says nothing". claim_settlement answers a non-requirement sentence "says nothing" only under a name with no locus or aggregation qualifier, and never when the sentence asserts the quantity's own combination; conditional answers from either source are removed from the claims the judge scores. claim_contradicts and CONTRADICTION_VETO_PROBABILITY (0.30) are unchanged, and nothing in any of the three measurements implicates the threshold.
