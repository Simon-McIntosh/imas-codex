Mapping and calibration

Each dimension sees only its own category probabilities and the failure probabilities of checks assigned to it by ReviewCheck.dimension. Category expectation is P(best) + 0.5 × P(middle); the worst category contributes zero. Name categories use _DIMENSION_LEVELS; the three documentation category questions use _DOCS_DIMENSION_CRITERIA. Documentation physics accuracy has no category question and uses its checks. A check contributes compliance 1 − P(failure). The score is score_d = 20 × sigmoid(intercept_d + offset + Σ weight_d,j × feature_d,j), with nonnegative feature weights, so increasing a dimension’s failure probability cannot improve its score. The total is sum(dimension_scores) / 80, the incumbent’s normalisation.

The frozen receipts contain binary defensibility labels, not incumbent dimension ratings. The fits therefore calibrate each dimension’s evidence against that binary target; they do not claim agreement with unavailable dimension gold scores. Five-fold cross-validation groups by copy_group within calibration only. It selects the regularisation penalty from 0.001, 0.01 and 0.1 by the AUC of the mean dimension score; both axes select 0.001. A shared logit offset maximises the check-clear accept cohort at at least 90% precision and five items, using calibration only. The resulting offsets are 1.575000 for names and 2.037926 for docs; calibration accepts are 12/13 and 11/12 respectively. The calibration policy fixes the chat width at 0.05, one point on a 20-point scale. Validation was measured after fitting and was not used to tune these parameters.

A selected veto routes to refine. If its raw rubric total would be at or above acceptance, all dimensions are scaled by the same factor to cap the total at min_score − 0.001; their relative values and exact normalised mean survive. Raw dimension scores remain in the record, so this decision ceiling is visible. Without a veto, the total at or above min_score accepts, the narrow interval below it uses the chat reviewer, and lower totals refine. Calibrated acceptance probability remains separate and does not decide a consumer’s stage.

Failed checks include non-veto failures at the taxonomy’s 0.5 failure threshold, each marked veto: false; selected vetoes carry their fitted cutoff. A source contradiction uses the explicit choice-or-probability rule, with probability cutoff 0.30. An unsupported claim is not automatically a contradiction: contradiction-reproduction.log records the incorrect refine route for contradiction_veto=False and a non-support probability of 1.0, and the test now proves refusal only when the contradiction predicate fires.
