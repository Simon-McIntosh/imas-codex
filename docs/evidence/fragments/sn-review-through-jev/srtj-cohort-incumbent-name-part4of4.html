What this measures

The ceiling was sized from the estimate's per-item price, USD 0.0705 per name item. The pool reserves pre-launch exposure per reviewer seat — about USD 0.07 for each of three seats, per item — and the observed spend reached USD 4.2165, or about USD 0.0727 per selected item, before the item list was complete. A ceiling of USD 4.50 for 58 name items is therefore below what the incumbent quorum costs to finish them. The remedy is a ceiling and a wave-sizing decision for the coordinator: raise the piece ceiling above the observed per-item price times the item count, and run at most one scratch database per node, or place the pieces on separate nodes.
