The fan-out proposer is told which checks opened its search

The refine fan-out fires only on a failed named check, but the trigger still returned an always-empty excerpt and the dispatcher rendered it as a comment-feedback section reading (none). The proposer was therefore asked to plan retrieval with no statement of why, and a candidate that had just failed a check was indistinguishable from one that failed none.

Route chosen, and why

The slot is filled, not removed. The deciding route's failed-check records already carry the check's criterion, the judge's reason and the field evidence that decided it, so the reasons are in hand at the refine call site and no reader of graph state is added. Removing the slot would have been the fallback only if producing them needed a new graph read.

Two functions carry it. check_results_from_failed_checks turns a route's named failures into the trigger's check-result mapping, keeping the criterion, the reason and the evidence beside the probability of 1.0 each failure carries. failed_check_reasons renders the checks that actually fired — retrieval checks, answered, at or above the threshold — as one line each, capped at six lines of 240 characters, and only when the result carries a reason, so a caller supplying bare probabilities still gets an empty excerpt rather than a list of identifiers. The prompt section is renamed from reviewer feedback to the named checks that fired, since that is what it now carries.

Membership is decided by the veto flag, not by presence in the list. The persisted failed-check list is the union of a route's vetoed checks and every check it holds details for, so it also carries checks that were answered and passed; mapping the whole list to a failure probability of 1.0 reported a passing check to the proposer under a heading that said it had failed, and let it fire the trigger. Only veto: True is a failure now. An entry written before the flag existed is still treated as a failure, which is what its inclusion in that list meant.

The firing half of that defect predates this work. Before the trigger rendered anything, the refine call site already mapped every persisted entry to failure_probability 1.0 (worker_refine_names.py at 18daf78f5), and should_trigger_fanout_from_checks reads only that field, so a passing check could open a fan-out search on its own. Filtering at the mapping fixes the excerpt and the decision together, since both read the same results.

What the new tests lock

tests/standard_names/test_fanout_review_feedback.py asserts that a fired trigger puts the check's identifier, its reason and its evidence into the rendered proposer prompt with no (none) left behind; that a check-free candidate keeps the empty slot and reports no failure; that a failure which does not request retrieval never reaches the prompt, so a grammar-only failure cannot manufacture retrieval intent; that a check the route answered and passed is neither listed as a failure nor able to fire the trigger, even at a zero threshold; and that the excerpt stops at its line and character caps.
