The fixes

Claim veto: an undecided distribution is not a denial

The veto had two channels: the selected choice and a contradiction probability at or above 0.30. The second fired on answers whose leading mass was "says nothing", so a judge that had just said the sources do not bear on a claim still vetoed it. It now requires the contradiction mass to reach the silence mass as well. The selected-choice channel is untouched, and a support-selected answer whose contradiction mass leads the silence mass still vetoes.

Evidence: a direction word is placement

A DD field label naming a component stayed evidence because the placement vocabulary held no direction word. Toroidal, poloidal, radial, vertical, horizontal, parallel, perpendicular and axial now empty the label, while the source path and the unit stay.

Settlement: a population disclaimer is a different axis

A sentence disclaiming a species, charge-state or population restriction was withheld from settlement whenever the name fixed a locus or an aggregation. The population axis is independent of both, so the name's qualifier cannot turn the disclaimer into a claim a source denies. Scope disclaimers now settle before that guard; every other claim still goes to the judge.

Recall: the one missed row is an accepted gap, not a rule to widen

A second ask at the cutoff was measured and then dropped by the lead's ruling. It put every "says nothing" answer within 0.05 of the cutoff back to the judge through the same decide_batch path and vetoed when either reading denied. It moved nothing. In the replay that carried it, the missed row's claim was re-asked on both passes and the judge answered "says nothing" again; on the ten stored answers from that row, eight sat at the cutoff, all eight were re-asked, and not one second reading denied the claim. The rule cost a second batch on every claim near the boundary and bought no firing, so it is removed with its four tests. The recall bar is the 59 of 60 planted contradictions the shipped rule fires on, with contradicts-354, the field-current relation row, named as the known gap the lead accepted; a miss on any other row fails the bar.

Instrument: the hold set and a call that never decided

The deterministic hold set now reads the lexical lint family as well as the house-style and documentation-gate families, so it matches the product's audit. A Jev call that fails to decide is retried, then reported as an instrument failure that aborts the run; it is never counted as a refine.

The defining-relation gate is the lead's call

The gate passed only a document carrying exactly one display equation whose dimensions check, and S0062 carries none, so the gate held it. The lead ruled that a name whose value is a coordinate, a position or a locus needs no defining relation, only its frame and axes. The gate now decides that class from the grammar's own parse rather than from words in the text: it reads base.kind, which is geometry for a geometric base token such as coordinate, and projection.shape, which is coordinate for a coordinate projection of a quantity. vertical_coordinate_of_antenna_strap parses to base coordinate of kind geometry with a vertical coordinate projection, so it is in the class; a coordinate document that names no frame or axes still fails, and every quantity keeps the dimensional rule.
