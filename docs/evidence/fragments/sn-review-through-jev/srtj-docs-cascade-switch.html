2. Why the documentation axis moves

The replay at the shipping commit (merge 4bea8b681) measures two bars on two passes.

  • Link defects: all 19 planted link-defect documents refined on both passes.
  • Acceptable documents: 1 of 30 refined on pass 1 and 0 of 30 on pass 2. The single row refined is sheet row S0010, so the stated bar — every link defect caught and no acceptable document refined, on both passes — is met on pass 2 and missed on pass 1 by that one document.
  • Recall: 59 of 60 planted contradictions fire on both passes. The one miss is contradicts-354, the field-current relation row, which the lead accepted as a known gap: the judge answers "says nothing" to that claim under every reading tried, so it is a judge reading rather than an untested rule.

S0010 is the recorded precision miss. The document is flux_surface_averaged_time_averaged_normalized_perturbed_parallel_magnetic_field_magnitude. Its veto fired on the probability channel at contradiction masses of 0.30 and 0.32 for two claims whose leading answer was "says nothing" (silence 0.47 and 0.35 against those contradiction masses) — an undecided distribution at the cutoff, not a denial. The veto now requires the contradiction mass to reach the silence mass as well, so this class of firing is what the fix targets. Recording the row keeps the miss visible rather than tuning the bar to it, and the 5% panel audit is what continues to price that firing in production.

The lead's ruling on 2026-10-24 is to switch now with S0010 recorded as a known precision miss, and to keep the 5% panel audit. The name axis was handed over on 2026-10-22 with the same audit retained; this change makes the two axes agree.

3. The shipped setting is asserted, not assumed

A configuration switch is invisible to code review, so tests/standard_names/test_cascade_deciding_axes.py gains test_the_shipped_configuration_makes_both_axes_deciding. It reads the configuration through the same resolvers the product calls — cascade.deciding_enabled() for both axes and panel_audit.panel_audit_rate(), which the cascade invokes when it samples the audit — and asserts both axes decide and that the rate equals 0.05. The rest of the file drives the resolver's accepted and refused forms by stubbing the settings section, so those tests are unaffected by the shipped value; the new test is the one place the shipped file itself is the subject.
