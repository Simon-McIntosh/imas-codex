Recall of known defects in a Jev risk order

For each golden item the named checks of its axis are read from the review taxonomy. A check's failure probability is its defect probability: a Noul's true-case probability, one minus a Choice's acceptable-form probability, one minus a dimension rubric's acceptable level, or one when an audit finding class is present. The item's risk is the maximum over its checks, ties broken by the mean; the mean is carried as a sensitivity order. The incumbent order reads each item's stored review score lowest-first from the graph (reviewer_score for names, reviewer_score_docs for documentation).
