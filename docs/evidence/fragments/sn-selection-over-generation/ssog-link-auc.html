Jev's link judgment against the coordinator's labels

The link-label sheet holds 180 name: links drawn from the live documentation, every self-link found plus a physics-domain spread of neighbours. A link is labelled 1 when its text describes the target's own quantity and 0 when it names a different one; those coordinator labels landed in commit 9867930cd. Jev scored 180/180 links, so nothing was excluded for a null probability. Resampling is by copy group — documenting_name, because links inside one description move together — with 1000 draws, seed 123 and a 95% level, through imas_codex.discovery.base.evaluation. The sheet carries 116 positives and 64 negatives across 168 copy groups.

Over all rows Jev's probability ranks the links at AUC 0.757 (0.679–0.823): clearly above chance, and no better on the non-self links alone (0.759). On self-links only he is weaker at 0.659 (0.513–0.780), which is the population where a deterministic flag already knows the answer. That flag is the baseline: score 1 - is_self_link, so it predicts a non-self link is good and a self-link is bad. Its AUC over all rows is 0.799 (0.739–0.862), and the paired difference Jev minus baseline is -0.043 (-0.133–0.041). The point estimate favours the deterministic flag, but the interval crosses zero, so this sample does not separate them: on these 180 links Jev's judgment adds no measurable separation over simply flagging self-links.

The disagreements show where the two differ. The worst misses Jev would let through (label 0, highest probability) are mostly self-links whose text states a different quantity than the name's own — "power of an ion-cyclotron heating antenna" scored 0.91 against launched_power_of_ion_cyclotron_heating_antenna, whose own quantity is the power actually launched rather than the antenna's radiated power. The good links he would block (label 1, lowest probability) are unqualified forms of a qualified target, such as "atomic count of pellet" at 0.30 against fast_ion_critical_energy's neighbour, and "power at the outer divertor target" at 0.50 against maximum_power_at_outer_divertor_target.

The negative control shuffles the label column (seed 123) and rescoring gives AUC 0.488 (0.392–0.583), whose interval contains 0.5 as it must — the instrument reports separation only when there is structure to find.
