Comparator rerank acceptance on a large held-out draw

Status: complete. Every drawn extraction batch carries a result in both arms, with no batch left uncomposed, so the figures below are the measure's own final pass summary rather than a partial pass.

Draw seed 20261015 from a population of 7,692 source paths: 160 drawn, 119 extraction batches (137 items). Excluded set: 70 sources — the 40 drawn under seed 20261014 (read from that run's own results record), the 30 sources of the comparator label sheet, and the sheet's 9 boundary-density sources (already inside the 30; recorded as their own group). Composed and reviewed on local/deepseek-v4.1-flash; only arm B reranks the nearby-name comparator block, judged by typesafe/jev-1.13.

One arm runs per pass: the rerank switch is read from the environment at call time, so two settings in flight together would rerank the arm meant to run without it. Each pass serves whichever arm has more batches left, so the two arms advanced in alternation and their unit counts differed while the measure was in flight. Eighteen bounded passes were run in total.

First-pass acceptance

A candidate the reviewer returned no score for is recorded with scored: false, counted separately, and excluded from the rate: it is a reviewer omission, not a rejection, so the rate is taken over scored candidates. Both counts are given per arm because a batch may compose no candidate and a few compose more than one.

Unscored candidates: 5 in arm A, 10 in arm B — 15 in total, which is 11 per cent of all 131 candidates and is why the rate is stated over scored candidates rather than over all of the arm's candidates.

The bootstrap_interval from imas_codex/discovery/base/evaluation.py has point 0.000 and spans [−0.140, 0.140] (95 per cent, 1000 resamples): it does not exclude 0, and the point sits exactly on it. The paired numerator is equal in the table and in the pairing, so this draw shows no first-pass acceptance difference between the arms.

Two limits on what this decides. The paired denominator is 48 sources, under half of the 119 drawn batches, because a source contributes to the pairing only when both arms composed a candidate for it and the reviewer scored both; most batches compose no candidate at all, so the paired comparison rests on the minority of batches that do. And the pairing key is the candidate's own source_id, which the harness's candidate extraction derives per candidate rather than copying from the drawn path; a resume should key the pairing on the batch's drawn path instead.

Residual to record: each arm completed 120 units against a 119-batch draw, so at least one batch was composed twice within an arm. The per-candidate records hold no duplicate (source_id, name) pair in either arm, so no candidate was counted twice; the excess is not explained further by the record.
