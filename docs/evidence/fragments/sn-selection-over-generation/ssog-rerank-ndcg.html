The comparator rerank puts better comparators in the top ten, measured on the labelled 30-item set

The comparator label sheet records 30 DD sources, each with the union of the embedding-ordered top ten and the reranked top ten of its comparator block; every candidate carries its embedding_rank, its rerank_rank over a pool of 20, and a coordinator grade. The grades landed in commit 95231110a, whose body states the 0–3 rubric (3 same quantity, 2 neighbouring quantity, 1 shares subject/locus/ family, 0 unrelated); scoring the 342 candidates gave 130 at 0, 111 at 1, 73 at 2 and 28 at 3. The driver docs/evidence/fragments/sn-selection-over-generation/ssog-rerank-ndcg-run.py scores each item twice with sklearn.metrics.ndcg_score at k=10, once against embedding_rank and once against rerank_rank, both keyed on the negative rank so rank 1 is best. Because every listed candidate is graded, the metric sees only the labelled union and never an ungraded name, so the ideal ordering is taken over the whole graded candidate set — this is what "labelled-only" means. The driver refuses a candidate whose grade is not an integer from 0 to 3 or whose ranks are not both positive integers, and excludes an item whose grades are all zero, where nDCG is undefined.

Over the 30 scored items the mean nDCG@10 rose from 0.7486 in embedding order to 0.9249 in reranked order, a mean per-item difference of +0.1763 with a 95 percent percentile bootstrap interval of [0.1144, 0.2432] — 23 wins, 2 ties and 5 losses. The interval is a plain unit bootstrap over items (1000 resamples, seed 123, resampling item indices with replacement) and is not copy-grouped, so the nine boundary-density items are treated as independent of one another. The same figures split into a strata view: the nine boundary_density items gain the most (0.6097 to 0.9087, a difference of +0.2990, CI [0.1811, 0.4008], 8/1/0), while the other 21 items gain less (0.8082 to 0.9319, +0.1237, CI [0.0578, 0.1930], 15/1/5). No item was excluded: every item carries at least one candidate graded above zero.

As a negative control the reranked order was rescored with the grades shuffled within each item (seed 123), which carries no relation to that order. The shuffled rerank scores 0.6786 on average, a mean difference of −0.0700 against the unshuffled embedding score — near zero beside the +0.1763 the real rerank earns, and the shape of no signal.
