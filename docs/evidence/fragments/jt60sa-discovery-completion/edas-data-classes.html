EDDB class readers

The recorded E101173 catalogue enumeration contains the letters T, O, P, A, C, G, J, K, M, N, V across 6,240 rows. The scanner takes each letter from eddbreadTable's classlist, rather than inferring it from a signal name. The live graph has 3,770 rows currently interpreted as parameter; this positive control confirms that P is present at scale in the catalogue-derived data. P therefore retains its existing parameter interpretation even though the access manual documents J rather than P. A, C, K and V have no read-call definition in the EDDB access manual or the English eDAS quick reference, so their interpreted class stays unset while their raw letter is kept.

The baseline scanner check failed 11 of 11 selected cases: J, M, G and N were unset, and no signal had a raw-letter slot. The edited scanner check passed 16 cases, and a graph transaction confirmed that re-enumeration refreshed accessor, interpreted class, raw letter, unit and catalogue text while preserving the enriched description, name, domain, keywords, status and claim. A J-to-unset mutation failed the J read-call test.
