EDDB class readers

The recorded E101173 catalogue enumeration contains the letters T, O, P, A, C, G, J, K, M, N, V across 6,240 rows. The scanner takes each letter from eddbreadTable's classlist, rather than inferring it from a signal name. In the current graph, all 6,338 JT-60SA EDDB signals have a null raw-letter slot because they have not been re-enumerated since that slot was added. Of these, 3,770 are currently classed parameter, also with no raw letter. Their catalogue paths span CryoHRS (704; for example CryoHRS/cAT105), QMS (645; QMS/PreP12CRYO), CryoMag (413; CryoMag/m1C01CV) and other categories. These paths do not identify whether their EDDB letter is P or J. The graph therefore cannot settle P versus J: P keeps its existing parameter mapping until a re-enumeration records the letters and a native read verifies it. The EDDB access manual gives no read call for A, C, K or V, so their interpreted class stays unset while their raw letter is kept. The graph's English eDAS_QuickReference_E.pdf record is deferred and metadata-only, with no extracted text or chunks; it cannot confirm or exclude a definition for those letters.

The baseline scanner check failed 11 of 11 selected cases: J, M, G and N were unset, and no signal had a raw-letter slot. The scanner and payload checks passed 16 cases, and a graph transaction confirmed that re-enumeration refreshed accessor, interpreted class, raw letter, unit and catalogue text while preserving the enriched description, name, domain, keywords, status and claim. A J-to-unset mutation failed the J read-call test. The remote reader now selects the native call for T, O, P, J, M, G and N; unknown letters produce a refusal without invoking a reader.
