EDDB class readers

The recorded E101173 catalogue enumeration contains the letters T, O, P, A, C, G, J, K, M, N, V across 6,240 rows. The scanner takes each letter from eddbreadTable's classlist, rather than inferring it from a signal name. The live graph has 3,770 rows currently interpreted as parameter; this positive control confirms that P is present at scale in the catalogue-derived data. P therefore retains its existing parameter interpretation even though the access manual documents J rather than P. The EDDB access manual gives no read call for A, C, K or V, so their interpreted class stays unset while their raw letter is kept. The graph's English eDAS_QuickReference_E.pdf record is deferred and metadata-only, with no extracted text or chunks; it cannot confirm or exclude a definition for those letters.

The baseline scanner check failed 11 of 11 selected cases: J, M, G and N were unset, and no signal had a raw-letter slot. The edited scanner check passed 16 cases, and a graph transaction confirmed that re-enumeration refreshed accessor, interpreted class, raw letter, unit and catalogue text while preserving the enriched description, name, domain, keywords, status and claim. A J-to-unset mutation failed the J read-call test.
