How many name-skipped JT-60SA files the content arm would admit

The content arm admits 6 of 75 sampled name-skipped JT-60SA files and 2 of 31 files of the client_eqdbms EQDB cohort when it judges them from their fetched source, in the primary measurement run the manifest cites (measurement.log, content_full, spend $0.0259). The admission fractions stay small — 0.00 to 0.20 across the 0.05 bins of score_composite between 0.15 and 0.40, and 0.065 for the cohort — so lowering the names-arm pass gate would drain a small fraction of the files it newly exposes, at the cost of one fetch over the JT-60SA hop per admitted file. Three further repeats over the same records admitted 3, 3 and 4 of 75, so the sampled total lies between 3 and 6 of 75 (0.04–0.08) across four runs; the primary run sits at the high end. The client_eqdbms miss is a names-arm path-vocabulary defect rather than a gate-threshold defect: the content arm reads two of those files as in-scope (eqrd13.f, eqwt13.f) and keeps the other twenty-nine below both gates even with the source in view.

Method. The eligible population is the name-skipped JT-60SA CodeFiles (status='skipped', relevance_stage='name') whose skip_reason is not a duplicate: 1,029 files between score_composite 0.15 and 0.40, against 1,860 name-skips in total. A seeded draw (random.Random(20261007)) took 15 files from each of the five 0.05 bins (0.15–0.20 … 0.35–0.40), 75 files, and the 31 files under /analysis/src/client_eqdbms.EQ32.Rev1/eqdbms/EQDB/ were added as a named cohort (two cohort paths are also in a bin, so the 106 records cover 104 distinct paths). Every path was fetched through fetch_remote_files (imas_codex/ingestion/readers/remote.py) grouped into its parent directory — 36 directories, tar batches for the multi-file ones — and all 104 distinct paths fetched. Each file was then judged with the content arm exactly as imas_codex/discovery/code/scorer.py defines it: state from build_triage_state(..., with_content=True) with content_head set to the fetched text, questions from build_triage_questions(with_content=True), seat discovery-relevance (typesafe/jev-1.13) through acall_decisions(..., service="facility-discovery"). Nothing was written to the graph. Admission is content_admits: score_composite at or above code-ingest-threshold (0.5), or the strongest facet at or above code-facet-admission-threshold (0.85). Negative control and the facet clause. The facet clause admitted nothing: in the primary measurement run the strongest facet any file reached was 0.81 (recorded as 0.81 signal_processing_depth for /analysis/src/SAselene_v2.0_forPSRC/minpack/lmdif1.f in measurement.log), below the 0.85 gate, so every admission above came from the composite clause. Decision spend: $0.0259 for the 106-file main run plus $0.0430 for the three stability repeats, $0.0689 in total, well under the USD 1 budget.

A production discrepancy worth recording. score_worker (imas_codex/discovery/code/workers.py:452) passes build_triage_questions() — the six identity questions only — while its state is built with_content=True. The four facet questions the facet admission clause reads are therefore never asked in the live pipeline, so content_facet_relevance returns 0 and the facet clause can never fire there. This measurement used the full content-arm question set (with_content=True), as the content arm is documented in scorer.py and as the earlier labelled-sample prototype asked them, because the admission rule and the requested strongest-facet column both read facets. An identity-only repeat over the same files admitted 3 of 75 sampled and 2 of 31 cohort, the same order as the full arm.

Reading. The content arm admits 6 of 75 sampled name-skipped files (0.08) across the 0.15–0.40 band in the primary run, 3–4 of 75 across three repeats (an observed 0.04–0.08 range), and 2 of 31 (0.065) of the client_eqdbms cohort, and every admission is a composite near the 0.5 gate rather than a strong facet. The highest bin fraction seen is 0.200 (bin 0.35–0.40 in the primary run), but it rests on 3 of 15 files and falls to 1–2 of 15 in the repeats, so no bin's fraction is stable enough to justify moving the pass gate to its lower edge; the 0.15–0.20 and 0.25–0.30 bins admitted none in any run. The client_eqdbms miss is largely a names-arm vocabulary defect: its path and filename evidence reads as infrastructure_or_utility (0.16–0.30) before the source is seen, and seeing the source lifts only two of the thirty-one into scope. The next node should change the names-arm question rather than the gate.
