Tangential pickup coils

The PSRC catalogue has 23 pickup channels; the SELENE input maps 17 to R,Z,angle rows. From the COCOS 2 ψ grid, Br=(∂ψ/∂Z)/R and Bz=−(∂ψ/∂R)/R. The listed angle defines N=(−sin θ, cos θ) in the R–Z plane. All 17 mapped measured channels follow −B·N, with median absolute residuals 0.0132, 0.0041, 0.0133 and 0.0051 T at E101163 4.8, 5.0, 5.1 s and E101011 6.8 s. The along-angle projection gives 0.0477 T at E101163 5.0 s, and +B·N is worse still. Thus the observed winding is negative relative to that geometric normal. The measurements are in tesla, with no 2π factor. Six further channels lack sensor positions, so their orientation is unknown.

Profile coordinates

The active EDAS coordinate selector maps FIT_RHOTYPE 0 to equal ψ, 1 to volume-average ρ, 2 to toroidal-flux-average ρ, 3 to poloidal-flux-average ρ and 9 to poloidal flux with SOL. This is a per-record selector, so a profile family cannot inherit one fixed coordinate. A live LCDB/a042181/ate.s001 record for shot E101173 reports FIT_RHOTYPE=1 and positive XEXPDATA 0.563–0.827, but its equilibrium shot is 75000. Its TOPICS_RHOTYPE=6 is absent from that selector map. Neither selector was compared numerically with a SELENE reference at the same shot, so coordinate sign and the TOPICS axis remain unverified.
