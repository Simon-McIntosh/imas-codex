Exclusion record — none for the three trees

The three trees were not dropped by a facility excludes: pattern. Every excluded/skipped directory under /analysis/src carries a FacilityPath node with status='skipped' and a terminal_reason; the only such reasons present are excluded (on directory:__pycache__ and directory:.svn), access_denied (the edas2 v2.1.14 subdirectories) and scan_error (the eqdbms directories under edas2). None of the three target trees carries any skipped/excluded row.

The jt-60sa facility excludes (lines 57–65 of imas_codex/config/facilities/jt-60sa.yaml) are directories /tmp, /var/tmp, /work/edas and patterns *.avi, *.dat.old, *.backup. No pattern matches a tree name, so no exclusion narrowing was needed and the facility YAML was left unchanged.

The real cause is the expansion stamp. The root /analysis/src carries status='discovered', expanded_at=null — it was never re-expanded. Children are created only in the expansion branch of persist_scan_results, and claim_paths_for_expanding claims only expanded_at IS NULL, a property no writer cleared. SAeqread exists from an earlier expansion (expanded_at=2026-03-10) but the two newer trees, added to the filesystem after that, were never listed. Clearing expanded_at on reset is exactly the missing step.
