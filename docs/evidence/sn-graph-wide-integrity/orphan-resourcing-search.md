Classification means:

- **NO-MEASURING-PATH** — two differently worded searches found no field that stores the quantity; the exact queries are retained below.
- **UNCERTAIN** — plausible candidates exist, but their owner, population, axis, mechanism, documentation, or DD-authoritative unit does not settle the identity without physics judgment.

Scores are the path-search similarity score; where a candidate came from a cluster rather than a direct scalar hit, that is stated explicitly. Units written as `—` are absent from the DD field metadata returned by the tool, not inferred values. Algebraically equivalent unit spellings such as `Hz` and `s^-1`, `N.m^-2` and `m^-2.N`, or `W.m^-3` and `m^-3.W` are recorded as matches.
