Eight of the twenty species families with three or more members are split this way. The same defect shows outside species families: two camera unit vectors are filed under plasma_wall_interactions, voltage_of_valve is alone in fueling while the gas-injection names sit in plant_systems, safety_factor and greenwald_density are filed under transport, and transport holds 698 of the 1,975 names. The earlier census in sn-catalog-consistency §1 counted 98 of 125 divergent families diverging on domain.

The cause is in how the domain is decided, not in any one answer. The decision is made one name at a time, and the options offered for a name are only the domains of that name's own Data Dictionary sources, the umbrella of those, and general. Two siblings sourced from different parts of the Data Dictionary are therefore offered different options and cannot agree. A name whose answer falls below the confidence floor keeps whatever domain it had, and a name with a single candidate takes it without a judgment. Nothing compares a name's answer with its siblings', and the export does not check it. The domain a name receives describes where its sources live, which is the grouping sn-catalog-grouping set out to remove.

§2 — One domain decision per family

A name's domain is decided for the concept it names, and every member of a family receives that one decision. A family here is the pattern family the repository already defines for documentation, with the locus held fixed: live names that share a quantity, operator, process and locus and differ only in the species, the charge state or population qualifier, the component axis, or the device object a geometric property belongs to. The species-token grouping measured in §1 is a subset of it. Holding the locus fixed is deliberate: a density at the pedestal top and the same density at the magnetic axis may belong to different domains. The options a family is offered are drawn from all of its members' sources together, so a member with one unusual source no longer narrows or widens the choice alone. A name with no siblings is its own family and is decided as now. The decision is recorded once for the family, with its key, options, choice, probability and model, and each member reads that one record. A recorded lead correction still overrides, and applies to the family. The per-family decision replaces the per-name one as the way a domain is decided; it does not stand beside it.

Done when:

  • for every family, every member reads the same single stored decision, read back from a disposable graph seeded with the eight families in §1, and the number of judgments paid equals the number of families decided, not the number of members;
  • on at least 30 families of three or more members, the eight in §1 among them, each given one domain by a reader who has not seen either decision, the share of member names the per-family decision files under the reader's domain is recorded beside the share the per-name decision does, and is higher; the label set and who produced it are committed with the evidence;
  • on the labelled names the domain decision was calibrated on in sn-catalog-grouping, almost all of them single-member families, the accuracy is recorded as a regression reading and is not lower;
  • a family whose answer does not clear the confidence floor is reported undecided as a family, with its members listed, and none of its members is written;
  • a family planted with two members whose sources carry disjoint domains receives one domain, and the same two names decided by the per-name path, kept only as the test's comparison, receive two;
  • every domain write still goes through the one writer, shown by the existing guard test passing unchanged;
  • the node's gate runs on a compute partition and its log is committed under docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/.

§3 — The live names brought to one domain per family

The per-family decision is applied to every live family. A family, here and in §4, is the family §2 defines: the pattern family with the locus held fixed, so a quantity at the magnetic axis and the same quantity at the pedestal top are two families. The evidence for this section is recorded under docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/. A family the decision reports undecided under the options §5 gives it, whatever it read under §2's narrower options, is not left split unseen: the coordinator brings it to the lead with its options and members, and the lead's answer is recorded as a correction naming the family, which either gives it one domain or holds it split. The options every family is offered are those of §5, so this section applies §5's decision, not §2's alone. sn-catalog-consistency §5 declares a wait on this section: its family-agreement endpoint reads the same grouping census this section moves, and its domain-audit endpoint stays its own. A graph checkpoint is taken before the first write, with every graph-touching worker at rest.

Done when:

  • a read-back over every live name lists zero families whose members carry more than one physics_domain, apart from families held split by a recorded lead correction that names the family; those are listed with the correction, their count is recorded, and the count of split families before the first write is recorded beside both;
  • the eight families in §1 each read one domain, named in the evidence;
  • the share of published names filed under transport and under general is recorded before and after, with the twenty largest moves between domains listed by family so a physicist can read them;
  • the read-back counts a family planted split in a disposable graph, and stays silent on a family planted whole;
  • spend on domain decisions is recorded, and is at most USD 15;
  • the domain audit of sn-catalog-consistency §5 reports no more disagreeing names after the pass than before it.

§4 — A cut refuses a split family

The agreement holds for names created later. A name that joins an existing family takes the family's stored domain, and a catalog export reports any family it would publish across more than one domain file. That report is one more check in the export's existing blocking gates and uses the exclusion vocabulary the export already carries; it adds no command option and no second pass over the candidates.

Done when:

  • a name composed into a family that already has a stored decision is written with that decision and no new judgment is paid for, shown on a disposable graph;
  • an export dry run of a catalog with one family planted across two domains names that family and exits non-zero, and the same dry run on the repaired live graph reports none;
  • a family held split by a recorded lead correction in §3 is reported as an accepted exception rather than a failure, and removing the correction makes the dry run fail.

§5 — A name is offered every domain, not only where its sources live

§2 makes a family agree with itself. It does not make the answer right. A name with no siblings is still offered only the domains of its own Data Dictionary sources, so safety_factor and greenwald_density stay under transport and voltage_of_valve stays alone in fueling. A whole family can also agree on a domain that describes where its sources live, not what it is, and transport holds 698 of the 1,975 published names. In this section every family, single names included, is offered every domain the catalog files names under. The domains of its sources are given to the judgment as evidence and no longer bound the choice. This changes the options of the one family decision §2 builds; it adds no second decision.

Done when:

  • on at least 60 single-member names drawn at random across every domain file that holds one, and separately on safety_factor, greenwald_density and voltage_of_valve, which are not part of the draw, each given one domain by a reader who has not seen the stored domain or either decision, the share the decision files under the reader's domain is recorded beside the share the stored domains reach; the three named names are reported apart, and the share is higher on the drawn names alone; the label set and who produced it are committed under docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/;
  • on §2's family label set, the share of families whose one decision is the reader's domain is recorded beside §2's reading and is not lower;
  • on the labelled names the domain decision was calibrated on in sn-catalog-grouping, the accuracy under the widened options is recorded beside §2's regression reading and is not lower;
  • the number of families that fall below the confidence floor under the widened options is recorded beside the number under §2's options, each such family is listed with its two leading domains, and the count is at most one family in ten, so §3 is not left to settle the catalog one ruling at a time;
  • every labelled name or family where the decision and the reader disagree is listed with both domains, so the lead can rule on the pattern;
  • decided over every live family in a disposable copy and never written live, the share of names under transport and under general is recorded beside the present 698 of 1,975 and the general count, with the twenty largest moves listed by family;
  • a name planted with one source in a domain unrelated to its concept receives its concept's domain, and the same name offered only its source's domains does not;
  • the number of judgments paid and the cost per decision are recorded beside §2's, and the projected cost of §3's live pass is at most its USD 15;
  • the node's gate runs on the all_debug partition and its log is committed under the evidence directory.
