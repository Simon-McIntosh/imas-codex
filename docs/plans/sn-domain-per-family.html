§1 — What the release candidate shows, and why

The catalog release candidate v0.4.0rc8 files each name under one physics domain. A reader looking for the argon density at the plasma boundary expects to find it beside the krypton one. Eight of the twenty species families with three or more members are split this way. The same defect shows outside species families: two camera unit vectors are filed under plasma_wall_interactions, voltage_of_valve is alone in fueling while the gas-injection names sit in plant_systems, safety_factor and greenwald_density are filed under transport, and transport holds 698 of the 1,975 names. The earlier census in sn-catalog-consistency §1 counted 98 of 125 divergent families diverging on domain.

The cause is in how the domain is decided, not in any one answer. The decision is made one name at a time, and the options offered for a name are only the domains of that name's own Data Dictionary sources, the umbrella of those, and general. Two siblings sourced from different parts of the Data Dictionary are therefore offered different options and cannot agree. A name whose answer falls below the confidence floor keeps whatever domain it had, and a name with a single candidate takes it without a judgment. Nothing compares a name's answer with its siblings', and the export does not check it. The domain a name receives describes where its sources live, which is the grouping sn-catalog-grouping set out to remove.

§2 — One domain decision per family

A name's domain is decided for the concept it names, and every member of a family receives that one decision. A family here is the set of live names that differ only in a slot the concept does not depend on: the species, the charge state or population qualifier, the component axis, and the device object a geometric property belongs to. The options a family is offered are drawn from all of its members' sources together, so a member with one unusual source no longer narrows or widens the choice alone. A name with no siblings is its own family and is decided as now. The stored decision stays reproducible: the family key, the options, the choice, its probability and the model are recorded on each member. A recorded lead correction still overrides, and applies to the family.

Done when:

  • for every family, the decision one member stores equals the decision every other member stores, read back from a disposable graph seeded with the eight families in §1;
  • on the labelled names the domain decision was calibrated on in sn-catalog-grouping, the per-family decision's accuracy is recorded beside the per-name decision's on the same names, and is not lower;
  • a family whose answer does not clear the confidence floor is reported undecided as a family, with its members listed, and none of its members is written;
  • a family planted with two members whose sources carry disjoint domains receives one domain, and the same two names decided by the per-name path, kept only as the test's comparison, receive two;
  • every domain write still goes through the one writer, shown by the existing guard test passing unchanged;
  • the node's gate runs on a compute partition and its log is committed under docs/evidence/fragments/sn-domain-per-family/.

§3 — The live names brought to one domain per family

The per-family decision is applied to every live family. This absorbs the grouping half of sn-catalog-consistency §5: that section keeps its domain-audit endpoint, and its family-agreement endpoint is met here. A graph checkpoint is taken before the first write, with every graph-touching worker at rest.

Done when:

  • a read-back over every live name lists zero families whose members carry more than one physics_domain, or lists each remaining family with the recorded reason it stands, and the count before the first write is recorded beside it;
  • the eight families in §1 each read one domain, named in the evidence;
  • the share of published names filed under transport and under general is recorded before and after, with the twenty largest moves between domains listed by family so a physicist can read them;
  • the read-back counts a family planted split in a disposable graph, and stays silent on a family planted whole;
  • spend on domain decisions is recorded, and is at most USD 15;
  • the domain audit of sn-catalog-consistency §5 reports no more disagreeing names after the pass than before it.

§4 — A cut refuses a split family

The agreement holds for names created later. A name that joins an existing family takes the family's stored domain, and a catalog export reports any family it would publish across more than one domain file.

Done when:

  • a name composed into a family that already has a stored decision is written with that decision and no new judgment is paid for, shown on a disposable graph;
  • an export dry run of a catalog with one family planted across two domains names that family and exits non-zero, and the same dry run on the repaired live graph reports none;
  • a family listed with a recorded reason in §3 is reported as an accepted exception rather than a failure, and removing its reason makes the dry run fail.
