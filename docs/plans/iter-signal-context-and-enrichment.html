Plan · ITER plant data

ITER plant signals carry context and are enriched by group

The archive lists about a million plant variables and describes almost none of them. This plan gives each signal context from ITER's own records, enriches signals a component and a field pattern at a time, and ends with an agent finding a plant signal by description and reading it.

§1 — Where this starts

The scanner, loader, plotting and name parser landed under the plan codac-uda-signals. Its full-archive census counted 988,145 variables on the slow archive and 6,861 on the fast one, with none in common. The archive's type metadata gives the same description, "Signal value", for every variable measured, and refuses metadata altogether for about one variable in eight. A second archive interface returns a label for 70 of a 200-variable sample and a unit for some of them. The first 200 signals catalogued are five controllers that each expose the same forty fields.

A test question to the ITER document assistant about three of those names matched one component conditionally, one probably and one not at all, and pointed to the documents that hold the meaning: a per-system signal list, a per-system data file from which control-system variables are generated, a control functional specification, and the plant breakdown structure. A build worker cannot reach any of them: the document system redirects an unauthenticated request to a login page.

The lead has decided four things. ICP and IDM content may be curated into the graph as metadata and links. Every node and link so created references the ITER facility and is covered by the facility-scoped protected backup, and none of it enters the repository. Work that reads that content runs on the locally served model inside ITER, once a benchmark shows that lane is capable. Signals are enriched by component and by field pattern, not one at a time.

The existing signals discovery pipeline already enriches a source once and fills a template for each member, and already exports and pushes graph archives per facility. This plan uses both.

§2 — Every configured model seat names its model's latest version

Delivers. Each model seat the project configuration declares names the latest available version of the model it intends, or the landing record states why a seat stays on an earlier one. Seats that route to the locally served model name the model that lane serves today.

Done when. The landing record lists every declared seat with the model it named before, the latest version found and where that was established, and the model it names after; the configuration tests pass with no added failure against the base revision; and each changed seat answers one live request. Negative control: a seat naming a model its route does not serve fails its live request and is reported, not silently left.

§3 — The local model and a paid model are compared on signal enrichment

Delivers. A comparison of the relative benefit of the locally served DeepSeek model and a paid model on the signals discovery pipeline's enrichment work, so the lead can decide which carries the enrichment of ITER signals once their context exists. One sample is used: signals of another facility that the pipeline has already enriched. Each is enriched again through the pipeline's own enrichment stage with its normal full context, once by each model, with the same prompts and the same retrieved context. No signal is enriched from its name alone; that is not an achievable task and is not measured.

Done when. For at least two hundred already-enriched signals of another facility, chosen before the run across several physics domains, the landing record gives for each model: the share of responses that parse as the pipeline's structured output without repair; the share whose physics domain and unit agree with the stored values; the cost per signal and in total, with token counts; the calls per minute and wall time; and the count of failed or empty responses. Between the two it gives a blind judged comparison of the pair of descriptions for each signal, as local better, paid better or equivalent, with the judge not told which is which, forty pairs also read by hand and the hand reading's agreement with the judge stated; and the number of signals where either description is wrong against the signal's context. The paid model is named, and its spend is estimated from ten signals before the run and stays under twenty US dollars. The test signals' stored enrichment is left unchanged; the trial writes to a private store. The run executed on a compute partition (rigel). The lead's choice of model for ITER enrichment is recorded in this plan before section 6 is dispatched. Negative control: a response that fails to parse is counted as a failure, never repaired by hand and counted as a success; the judge's verdicts are unchanged when the two descriptions are presented in the opposite order for forty pairs, or the disagreement rate is reported; and the stored enrichment of the other facility's signals is byte-identical before and after.

§4 — One plant system's signals carry context from ICP and IDM

Delivers. For one pilot plant system, the magnet cold test bench, the graph holds its plant breakdown structure nodes, the rows of its signal list and data file, and references to its functional specification, each as an ITER-facility node, and each catalogued signal of that system is linked to the rows and component it corresponds to. A link is marked as exact when the source names the archive variable, as derived when it was joined through a component number, and as absent when no source row was found. The content reaches the graph from ICP and IDM through the read-only client that the plan icp-api-access delivers, which can issue no write, and it does not enter the repository. The function-category codes and their official meanings are imported with it, so a component's category has a name.

Done when. The landing record gives the number of source rows imported per source, the number of the pilot system's signals with an exact link, a derived link and no link, and twenty links of each present kind checked by hand against the source; every imported node and link reaches the ITER facility node; a second import adds none; a facility-scoped archive of the imported nodes and links is taken before the section lands, with no graph writer running, so the imported content has a second copy from the day it exists: the registry reports the package as private, and the number of imported nodes and of imported links read back from the archive equals the number in the graph, with both pairs of counts, the package name and its digest in the landing record; and a search of the tracked tree for the imported identifiers and a sample of imported titles finds none. Graph-marked statements pass. Negative control: a signal whose component number appears in no source row receives no link, and a row that names a different system links to no pilot signal; an archive with one imported node withheld fails the count comparison.

§5 — Catalogued signals are grouped by component and by field pattern

Delivers. Every catalogued archive variable, whether in the graph or only in the private catalogue store, is either in a component group or in an explicit ungrouped state, and components that expose the same set of fields share a field pattern. The grouping is derived from the parsed variable names and the archive metadata. For the signals that are in the graph it is stored there under the ITER facility, and it is recomputed without duplicates when the catalogue grows. It is run first on the partial catalogue, while the archive scan is still filling the private store, and run again when the scan completes.

Done when. For each run the landing record states the fraction of the archive the private store held, and gives the number of variables, the number grouped and the number ungrouped, the number of component groups and field patterns; the distribution of group size; the share of signals whose group has a single member; and the twenty largest patterns with their field counts, using invented names in tracked files. The number of group descriptions and pattern descriptions that enrichment would need is stated beside the number of signals. A second run adds no groups. Negative control: a signal whose name does not parse is placed in an explicit ungrouped state and is counted, never attached to another component's group.

§6 — The pilot system's signals are enriched by group and the result is measured

Delivers. Each component of the pilot system has one description written from its linked context, each field pattern has one description of what its fields mean, and every member signal carries a description composed from the two, with its unit and its physics or engineering classification. The language model that writes them is the one the lead chose after the comparison in section 3. A signal with no linked context is not enriched: it is marked as awaiting context and counted. Component and pattern descriptions reach members through the one group enrichment mechanism the discovery pipeline already has, keeping its claim, cost and status behaviour.

Done when. The landing record gives the number of component descriptions, pattern descriptions and member descriptions written and the number of language-model calls and their cost; for forty member signals chosen before the run, a hand comparison against the functional specification with the count that are right, partly right and wrong, where at most four of the forty are wrong. The bar is read by hand, the same instrument on which the refinement round found none of the paid model's forty descriptions wrong and 26 of the local model's; four is the coordinator's proposal and the lead may change it when answering the open model decision. A section that misses the bar is delivered as failed on quality, with the forty readings, for the lead to decide on; the number of signals left awaiting context; and the projected call count and cost for the whole catalogue from the grouping in section 5. A search recorded in the landing record finds one path by which a group description reaches its members. The projection is in this plan and accepted by the lead before section 7 is dispatched. Negative control: a member of a group whose component description failed is reported in a failed state and carries no composed description.

§7 — Every catalogued signal is enriched, checked and embedded

Delivers. The whole catalogue has passed group enrichment and the discovery pipeline's check and embedding stages, so the existing signal search returns ITER plant signals beside other facilities' signals. Signals of systems with no imported context are not enriched; they are marked as awaiting context, counted by plant system, and enriched when their context arrives. The landing record states how much of the catalogue that is, so the next systems to import context for can be chosen.

Done when. The landing record gives the number of signals in each pipeline state, including awaiting context by plant system, with the reason for every failure class, the actual call count and cost beside the accepted projection, and, for twenty described quantities written to a private file before the run with its digest recorded, the rank at which search returns the expected signal. A search for a quantity of another facility still returns that facility's signal. The run executed on rigel. Negative control: a signal whose enrichment failed is reported in a failed state and is not counted as enriched.

§8 — An agent finds an ITER plant signal and reads it through the server

Delivers. Through the project's server, a search for a described quantity returns an ITER plant signal with its component and context links, and a facility read loads it by identity over a bounded window. The read returns metadata, basic statistics and a reference to an internal file holding the arrays, with arrays inlined only under a strict byte limit. It accepts no host or endpoint from the caller, and is absent in the data-dictionary-only mode. The search is the existing signal search and adds no tool. The read is the one tool added, because no existing tool returns archive samples.

Done when. Through a running server, a search for a described quantity returns a pilot-system signal and the read returns its statistics for a two-second window, both in the landing record; the tool list in data-dictionary-only mode does not contain the read; a response never exceeds the transport's bounded size; and the landing record gives the server's tool count before and after, which differ by one, and names any existing tool the read could have been merged into with the reason it was not. Negative control: a call supplying an endpoint, and a call with an unbounded window, are each refused.

§9 — ITER graph content is in a protected facility backup and nowhere public

Delivers. Every node and link in the graph that this work and the catalogue plan created for ITER reaches the ITER facility node, a facility-scoped archive of them is stored as a private registry package, and no public archive or repository file carries them.

Done when. The landing record gives the number of ITER-derived nodes by label, the number that do not reach the ITER facility node, which is zero, and the registry package's name, visibility and digest; an archive built without the facility filter is shown to contain none of the ITER-derived labels' ITER rows; and the search of the tracked tree from section 4 still finds nothing. No graph writer is running when the archive is taken. Negative control: a node created for ITER without a facility link is found by the count and reported.

§10 — Grouping is re-run on the completed archive catalogue

Delivers. The grouping of section 5, run again once the private catalogue holds metadata for every listed variable, with groups and patterns that the earlier partial run left behind retired from the graph, so no component or pattern node remains that nothing references.

Done when. The landing record states that the private store's metadata is complete, and gives every measure of section 5 beside the partial run's: the fraction of the archive the store held, the number of variables grouped and ungrouped and in each parse outcome, component groups, control-node groups, patterns under both keys, the distribution of group size, the share of single-member groups, the share of component variables in a shared pattern, the twenty largest patterns with their field counts using invented names, and the descriptions enrichment would need. It gives the number of component and pattern nodes in the graph that no signal references, which is zero, and a repeat adds no groups. The run executed on a compute partition (all_debug). Negative control: a component whose field set changes between two runs leaves no unreferenced pattern node behind.

§ Decisions

Which model writes ITER signal descriptions in the discovery pipeline?

The comparison in section 3 on 200 signals with identical full-context prompts: the local model delivered 130 of 200 and none of its 20 raw responses parsed without repair; the paid model delivered 200 of 200 with all 20 parsing, for 0.95 US dollars.

This records the choice made on 2026-11-15 and what was then measured; it no longer governs sections 6 and 7, which are held by the open decision local-model-confirmed-on-quality. The lead reverted an earlier choice of the paid model so that ICP-derived context stays inside ITER, and asked for the pipeline to be refined for the locally served model. With the pipeline's defaults that model parsed 0 of 20 responses unrepaired and delivered 130 of 200 descriptions, against 20 of 20 and 200 of 200 for the paid model. After refinement (one signal per call, a prompt fitted to the model, one re-ask on a validation failure) 197 of 200 raw responses parse and all 200 are delivered; schema-constrained output lowered parsing and is left off. Quality on the same 200 signals: in a blind reading of 40 pairs sealed before the judge ran, the paid model's description was preferred in 27 and the local model's in none, with 13 ties, and 26 of the local model's 40 descriptions were read as wrong against none of the paid model's; detail not supported by the signal's context was the dominant defect. The automated judge put 111 of the local model's 200 and 32 of the paid model's as wrong, read preference as nearly even (paid 75, local 71, tie 54), and agreed with the hand reading on 17 of 40 pairs.

Is the projected call count and cost of enriching the whole catalogue accepted?

Decided after section 6 reports the pilot's measured cost and the projection from the grouping.

Is the local lane accepted to build the sections that read ICP and IDM content?

Decided after section 3 reports its comparison. Until then no ICP or IDM content is given to any worker.

Narrowed by the lead's later rulings on 2026-11-15. Build workers that develop and test the pipeline, including the ICP client, may run on any lane; the lead's own account is the identity for ICP access, and that access is read-only by code. Which model writes ITER signal descriptions from ICP-derived context is not settled here: it is the open decision local-model-confirmed-on-quality. ICP-derived context stays inside ITER unless the lead chooses the paid model there, and no section that would send that context to a model is built until that decision is answered.

After the refinement result, which model writes ITER signal descriptions?

Reliability is settled for the locally served model: 197 of 200 raw responses parse and all 200 descriptions are delivered. Quality is not. By hand, on 40 pairs read blind and sealed before the judge ran: the paid model's description was preferred in 27, the local model's in none, with 13 ties; 26 of the local model's 40 descriptions were read as wrong against the signal's context and none of the paid model's. By the automated judge, on all 200: 111 of the local model's descriptions and 32 of the paid model's were scored wrong, and preference was nearly even (paid 75, local 71, tie 54); that judge agreed with the hand reading on 17 of the 40 pairs, so its figures carry less weight. Choosing the paid model sends ICP-derived context outside ITER. Sections 6 and 7 are held on this decision, and section 6's quality bar (at most four wrong in forty, by hand) is the coordinator's proposal for the lead to confirm or change with the answer.
