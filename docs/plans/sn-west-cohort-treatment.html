Draining both tails takes the batch from 196 towards roughly 210 to 228 names: up to twelve of Tail B recovered without any physics judgement, four more through a docs rotation, and eighteen new names from Tail A of which fifteen need only a compose pass.

Sequence matters here. The quarantine instrument must be reconciled before any revalidation sweep, or the sweep reports success and clears nothing. § Decisions

What may a batch-scoped catalog cut publish?

The halted candidate published 411 names from a 355-path manifest: 218 bound to manifest paths, 150 bound to data-dictionary paths outside it, 42 derived structural parents, one with no source. Structural parents are a deliberate inclusion from an earlier change; the 150 are not.

Lead ruling 2026-09-04. A batch-scoped cut publishes only names bound to a path in the batch manifest, plus the accepted structural parents those names require to be well-formed. That takes the candidate from 411 to roughly 260 and the out-of-manifest count to zero, which makes the pull-request body's claim true and lets a reviewer check the cut against the manifest they were given. The rejected alternative of exporting whole physics-domain files is what produced the 150, and would have required rewriting the body to stop claiming the cut was minted from the batch manifest. Withholding structural parents as well was also rejected: it reintroduces the published-child-with-no-published-parent condition that the accepted-parents change was made to remove.

Are constraint weights and solver iteration counts nameable quantities?

They carry units and node category quantity , so the pipeline sees them as eligible, but they describe a fit rather than the plasma. The same skip rule that excluded them also excluded detector humidity, which is a genuine physical measurement.

Lead ruling 2026-09-04: fit weights and iteration counts are neither physical nor geometric quantities and are excluded; every leaf quantity carrying a unit is otherwise named. The lead also asked whether this was already resolved in the graph by the data-dictionary sources carrying a category label, and the check says it is - but inconsistently. node_category already carries fit_artifact on 417 nodes, so the vocabulary is right and no new rule is needed; what is wrong is the classification. The classifier labels plasma_profiles/.../temperature_fit/weight as fit_artifact while labelling the identical construct at equilibrium/time_slice/constraints/faraday_angle/weight as quantity. 54 leaf nodes are misclassified this way - the /weight and /reconstructed pairs across every equilibrium constraint - of which 29 are already bound as StandardNameSource and 2 have produced names. So the treatment is to repair the classification and drive eligibility off node_category rather than a path heuristic, which then names detector_humidity (correctly quantity, unit 1, with a physical definition) and excludes constraint weights (correctly fit_artifact) under one authority instead of two.

multi-binding-batch-membership

Lead ruling 2026-09-04, settling the case the batch-scope rule turns on. A standard name with several StandardNameSource bindings exports when ANY ONE of those bindings is a path in the batch manifest. The alternative, requiring every binding to be inside the manifest, would withhold names the batch genuinely produced merely because the same physical quantity is also measured elsewhere in the data dictionary, which is the normal case for a well-formed name rather than an exception. It also has the practical consequence that a name's eligibility would change whenever an unrelated facility contributed another binding, so the cut would not be reproducible from the manifest alone. The any-binding rule keeps the pull-request body's claim true in the sense a reviewer can check it: every published name is traceable to a path in the manifest they were given, and no published name is bound only to paths outside it. This does not loosen the rule the halted candidate broke; the 150 names it wrongly carried were bound to NO path in the manifest at all.

Whether the disruption instant is named in this batch

The lead ruled directly that the time should not be named at this stage. The vocabulary consequence is that no event-instant base is introduced, so the bare-time rule stands unchanged and continues to decline all five rows that spell that token. The cost accepted is one WEST source path staying out of the batch; the cost avoided is a vocabulary change that would have to separate an event instant from a dimension axis while both spell the same token, with four correctly-declined axes exposed to a mistake in that separation. The row's exclusion is therefore correct rather than pending, and its reason field must say so — declined at this stage, no event-instant base in the vocabulary — rather than reporting the coordinate rule that would suggest it was mistaken for an axis.
