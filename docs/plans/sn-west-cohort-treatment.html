WEST Cohort Treatment and Batch Scoping

The review candidate was cut and then halted, because it publishes 150 names that are not in its own batch manifest and leaves 37 manifest paths untreated. This plan fixes the scoping, treats the untreated cohort, and hands the release plan a candidate whose contents match what its body claims.

§1 — Why the candidate was halted

Fork pull request 13 was cut on 2026-09-04 and closed the same hour, before any physicist saw it. Its body states that it "publishes 392 accepted standard names … minted from the committed west_production_dd_paths batch manifest", and its own arithmetic is internally consistent: 469 spreadsheet paths less 114 excluded by DD node category leaves the 355 eligible sources the manifest carries. The frozen artifact v0.4.0rc2+west-task-2e.sn_names.yaml then records 411 names against manifest_sources: 355 — more names than there are source paths.

The 150 are not near-misses at the manifest boundary. argon_density_at_divertor_target comes from summary/local/divertor_target/n_i/argon/value; beryllium_density_at_plasma_boundary from summary/local/separatrix/n_i/beryllium/value; beta from core_profiles/global_quantities/beta_tor. Beryllium is not a WEST species — WEST is a tungsten machine — so these are generic data-dictionary quantities that were never in the Task 2e spreadsheet. The leakage concentrates exactly where a whole-file export would put it: summary contributes 94 of the 150, while plasma_profiles (20) and edge_profiles (16) appear nowhere in the WEST manifest at all.

Two further defects sit in the same artifact. 184 of the 411 carry a null status, so nearly half the candidate has no catalog lifecycle field. And 18 of the 411 are not accepted on both axes, ten of them at drafted/pending — the frozen artifact carries names that are not publishable.

The consequence is what forced the halt: a physicist opening that pull request would have been asked to review 150 names from outside the batch they were told they were reviewing, with no way to tell which were which. The scoping defect is therefore the first item here, ahead of the cohort work, because every later candidate inherits it.

§2 — Group A: the attempt-cap cohort

All twelve WEST sources with status failed carry the identical error compose claim-attempt cap reached at attempt_count = 5. This is not twelve physics problems; it is one counter. The affected paths are ordinary physical quantities — pf_active/coil/current (A), barometry/gauge/pressure (Pa), summary/global_quantities/tau_energy/value (s), summary/global_quantities/power_steady/value (W), camera_ir/channel/camera/frame/apparent_temperature (K), and seven more.

Graph-wide the same signature accounts for 216 of the 283 failed sources. A further 59 failed sources carry no recorded error at all, which is a second defect: a failure path that does not say why it failed.

Suggested treatment. Reset the claim-attempt counter for sources whose only recorded failure is the cap, then re-compose. Verify by the count of failed sources before and after, and by how many of the twelve WEST paths gain a name. Separately, find the code path that records a failure without an error string and make it record one; a failure that cannot say why is not diagnosable by the next reader. Neither task requires physics judgement, and together they are the highest-leverage action available on this cohort.

§3 — Group B: the extracted backlog

Six WEST sources sit at status extracted — extracted from the data dictionary, never composed. They include calorimetry/group/component/power (W), calorimetry/group/component/energy_cumulated (J), summary/fusion/neutron_rates/total/value (Hz), ece/channel/optical_depth and hard_x_rays/emissivity_profile_1d/half_width_external. Graph-wide the extracted backlog is 1244 sources.

Suggested treatment. A scoped compose over the WEST subset first, since it is six sources and directly gates the candidate. The graph-wide 1244 is a separate, larger drain that is bounded by wall clock rather than budget — the last measured drain composed 151 names for six cents — and it should not be attached to the release critical path.

§4 — Group C: the exclusion rule over-reaches

Six WEST sources are skipped with node category quantity. At least one is wrongly excluded, and it was found by challenging an assumption rather than by any check the pipeline runs:

PathUnitCategoryDocumentation
camera_x_rays/detector_humidity1quantity Fraction of humidity (0-1) measured at the detector level
equilibrium/time_slice/constraints/n_e_line/weight1quantity Weight given to the measurement
equilibrium/time_slice/convergence/iterations_n-quantity Number of iterations carried out in the convergence loop

Detector humidity is a leaf node with a unit and a physical definition. It should carry a standard name. So should camera_x_rays/detector_temperature (K). The constraint weight fields and the convergence iteration count are arguably the "fit or representation artifacts" the manifest's own exclusion vocabulary names — but nobody has made that judgement explicitly, and the rule that dropped them is the same rule that dropped humidity.

This is the WEST-visible face of a defect already on record: signal-structure sources wrongly excluded by the leaf invariant, where roughly 336 quantities under a /data child were being refused.

Suggested treatment. Audit the skip rule against every skipped source that has a unit and node category quantity, graph-wide, not only in the WEST batch. Produce a list partitioned into "wrongly excluded, should be named" and "correctly excluded, with the stated reason". The second half matters as much as the first: an exclusion nobody can justify in words is indistinguishable from a bug.

§5 — Group D: correctly excluded coordinates

Six WEST sources are skipped with node category coordinate: equilibrium/time_slice/time, core_profiles/profiles_1d/time, hard_x_rays/emissivity_profile_1d/time, camera_x_rays/frame/time, camera_x_rays/detector_humidity/time and camera_x_rays/detector_temperature/time, together with summary/disruption/time/value.

These are time axes, not measured quantities, and excluding them is correct. They are recorded here so that a later reader counting "37 untreated" does not mistake them for outstanding work, and so the distinction between this group and Group C is explicit rather than assumed. No action.

That is the same failure mode this sprint met on the volumetric-rate names, where renames were issued against a criticism the reviewers had not made.

§7 — The lifecycle field is missing on nearly half the catalog

Of the 2295 names accepted on both axes, 1377 carry status: draft and 918 carry no status at all. Graph-wide, 2533 names have a null status. This is the field the catalog's own lifecycle depends on, and the null half is consistent with the recorded import that cleared origin and status on 1,091 identities.

Suggested treatment. This plan does not attempt the provenance recovery — that is owned by the origin-provenance backfill plan and should stay there. What belongs here is the release gate: no candidate should be cut while any name in its frozen artifact carries a null status, because the catalog cannot express that name's lifecycle. Adding that as an export pre-flight check turns a silent 45% hole into a refusal with a list.

§ Decisions

What may a batch-scoped catalog cut publish?

The halted candidate published 411 names from a 355-path manifest: 218 bound to manifest paths, 150 bound to data-dictionary paths outside it, 42 derived structural parents, one with no source. Structural parents are a deliberate inclusion from an earlier change; the 150 are not.

Are constraint weights and solver iteration counts nameable quantities?

They carry units and node category quantity , so the pipeline sees them as eligible, but they describe a fit rather than the plasma. The same skip rule that excluded them also excluded detector humidity, which is a genuine physical measurement.

§ Followups

Fix batch scoping, then treat the 37 untreated WEST paths

Start with the scoping defect in §1, because every later candidate inherits it: 150 of 411 published names are bound to no path in the batch manifest. Then Group A (§2), which is the cheapest real progress available — twelve WEST sources and 216 graph-wide share one error, compose claim-attempt cap reached, and need a counter reset rather than any physics judgement. Group B (§3) is six more sources that were extracted and never composed. Group C (§4) needs the skip rule audited, since it excluded detector humidity, which has a unit and a physical definition. Group D (§5) is correctly excluded and needs no action. Group E (§6) is twelve named identities, three of them one docs rotation from ready. The lifecycle gate in §7 should land before any recut, so a candidate cannot be cut carrying names with a null status.
