The lead judges the catalog by its consistency: one definition per concept, one documentation template per family, one grouping. On that measure it is not yet releasable, though its per-name scores are high. This plan carries that work forward from sn-integrity-backlog, which is closing, and from the unfinished repair in sn-catalog-grouping §3.

  • The read-only census sib-consistency-census grouped the 3,034 live accepted and drafted names into 130 physical_base families and 474 shared-concept groups. 125 families diverge: 98 on the phrase used for the shared concept, 98 on documentation template and 98 on physics_domain grouping. 91 concepts are defined more than one way, and the flux-surface average is written with 25 distinct definitions across 25 members. The census instrument and its disposable-graph control are in docs/evidence/fragments/sn-integrity-backlog/sib-consistency-census.json and its fragment.
  • The whole-catalog release candidate v0.4.0rc8, on the fork only, exports 1,975 of 2,865 accepted names, with 890 accounted exclusions and no unaccounted residue. Its warnings name documentation that links to names the cut does not export, and the unit pairs of the momentum_flux and momentum_source families.
  • The lineage-cycle repair left one lineage with two live tips: toroidal_plasma_momentum_diffusivity beside toroidal_momentum_diffusion_coefficient.
  • The sizing node sib-consistency-batches, dispatched 2026-11-11 under sn-integrity-backlog §52, splits the 125 families into sn edit batches of at most 25 names by physics domain, keeps renames in batches of their own, puts the flux-surface-average family in batch 1, assigns the release-candidate warnings and the two-tip lineage to batches, and estimates each batch's cost. It makes no graph write.

Lead ruling, 2026-11-11: the full-DD continuation does not wait for this pass. The batches can start before or beside the run, and §3's final census is taken after the run so it covers the names the run creates.

Batch 1 is the flux-surface-average family, the worked example the rest follow. Each concept ends with the one definition and template the census proposes for its family, unless the lead rules otherwise on a batch the coordinator brings with its evidence. It owns every name and documentation batch, the release-candidate rename batch and the advisory documentation batches included; only the rename batch for the two-tip momentum-diffusivity lineage belongs to §4, and the documentation rows of that lineage's names stay here.

Spend draws on the USD 350 full-DD approval of 2026-11-07, which this pass shares with sn-full-dd-continuation; that plan's run limit is computed after every draw on the approval, this pass's included. The first USD 60 is authorised as a tranche. When the measured rate projects the remaining batches past it, the coordinator brings the lead that projection, and the lead's ruling on the remaining batches is recorded on this section before any of them runs. The sized estimates use the documentation rate, while sn edit also pays for review, so batch 1 measures the real per-name cost and every later batch runs under a ceiling of that measured rate times its name count, plus a quarter.

The census this section measures with lives today as a script in an evidence directory, with no way to scope it to a population. A census entry point with a population argument may be added for this pass; if it is, it replaces that script rather than standing beside it.
