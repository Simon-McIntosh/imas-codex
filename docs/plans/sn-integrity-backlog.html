Plan · standard names · backlog, unscheduled

The integrity backlog, parked

From August to late September the standard-name work was mostly about the machinery that manages names: lifecycle axes, provenance, scalar mirrors, deletion guards and repair receipts. Sixteen plans carried that work, and between them they held 53 open followups while the WEST batch waited. The guards that mattered landed. What remains is real but not urgent, so it is gathered here, one row per item, and deferred until the WEST prototype ships.

§1 — Why this is parked rather than worked

The portfolio review of 2026-10-12 found no standard-name commit after 2026-09-21. It also found the WEST release plan at 538 KB and 293 commits with thirteen open followups, half of them about release tooling rather than the release. Every deferred row below was read against its source plan and kept only if it describes a defect or decision that still exists. Each row names the plan and followup it came from, so the full measurement is one click away in that plan's frozen text. When the WEST prototype ships, this plan is re-sprinted, and each row is re-confirmed against the live graph before any node is dispatched, since adjacent work has fixed recorded defects before.

A row needed by the WEST release has already moved to sn-west-prototype-release and is not repeated here: the export gate chain, the two recovered names, the two vocabulary admissions, the half-width base, rehearsal accounting and roster identity.
