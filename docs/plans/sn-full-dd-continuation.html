§5 — The documentation backlog the stalled drain left

The documentation drain under sn-integrity-backlog §52 stopped on 2026-11-11 with stop_reason: stalled, after USD 11.14 of its USD 20, and drained nothing on balance. The run in §4 is scoped by source, so it does not reach this name-level backlog. sn-integrity-backlog §52 has since attributed the fall to the drain's own source-drift refresh resetting documentation on false-positive drift (400 of the 405 names that left accepted documentation), corrected the drift comparison to each source's own snapshot (1,061 drifted names to 84), and restored 391 names to their own accepted documentation. This section drains what remains, read at its start.

It starts after §4 has taken its post-run reading, so no documentation write overlaps that reading, and its backlog then includes the names §4 created. Spend draws on what remains of the USD 350 approval after §4, less whatever part of the USD 60 tranche authorised for sn-catalog-consistency §2 is still unspent when this section starts, so the two passes cannot both count the same dollars. Its ceiling is the drain's own rate, the spend per name that newly reached accepted documentation in the drain (USD 11.14 over 21 names, about USD 0.53), times the backlog read at its start, plus a quarter. At that rate a backlog of about a thousand names costs several hundred dollars, more than the approval's remaining balance, so §5 is expected to need the lead's spend ruling, recorded on this section, before it runs.

The restoration record, docs/evidence/fragments/sn-integrity-backlog/sib-restore-reset-docs.html, reads accepted names with accepted documentation at 2,107 and with pending documentation at 1,108 before the restoration, and 2,497 and 721 after it; other writes between the drain's last reading (2,028) and the restoration's first account for the rest.
