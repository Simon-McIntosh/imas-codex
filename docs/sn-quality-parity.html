Current scope: make documentation quality measurable, then improve it on evidence

This plan owns the documentation half of the project goal — names whose docs carry the definition, governing equation, variable definitions, semantic scope and exclusions, essential relationships, sign convention where COCOS applies, and the working cross-references a physicist actually needs. Typical values and general measurement recipes are deliberately excluded: ungrounded ranges are worse than none, and the live faithfulness policy admits measurement content only where it is constitutive or discriminating (docs-content-gates, locked 2026-08-23).

Every change proves itself against the holdout; nothing regenerates sound content wholesale. Most of the original programme had already shipped by the time this plan was executed — the 2026-08-23 currency audit reclassified 43 claims and left five open, all of them about evidence rather than capability. The 2026-08-10 evidence-recovery overlay was removed in the 2026-08-11 portfolio review: advisory triage and rescores live in sn-benchmark-evolution, and the backlog drain already ran under sn-pipeline-recovery §4.

§5 — Turn the surviving presence checks into physics checks

The gate-role decision deleted the four gates that tested for a word. Six survive, and two of them are still only presence checks that a well-formed but wrong document would pass. Both can be grounded in data the graph already holds authoritatively, which is the property that separates a real check from a pattern match — the template is the logarithmic-unit rule landed in the release-readiness plan: a contradiction between two authoritative facts, deterministic, no judge, and unable to regress.

  • Dimensional consistency of the defining equation against the DD unit. defining_equation currently passes on the presence of =, \int or \propto. The DD-declared unit is authoritative and reaches the item already; the equation's dimensions are computable. A document whose stated relation cannot produce its own declared unit carries a real physics error that no amount of well-formed prose hides. Deliverable: the gate fails such a document, with a test built from a genuine unit/relation mismatch.
  • sign_convention made conditional on the COCOS transformation type. The gate checks canonical sentence form but cannot tell whether a sign convention was required, which is why absence had to be treated as permissible. HAS_COCOS supplies the transformation type authoritatively — the same edge whose relationship name was fixed earlier in this plan. Deliverable: required when the quantity is COCOS-dependent, forbidden when it is not, with tests for both directions.

Why these two and not a rewrite of the deleted four: scope, physical_meaning and exclusions have no authoritative counterpart in the graph to check against, so any version of them remains a judgement about prose. These two do. That is the whole distinction, and it is the criterion for admitting any future gate.

Sequencing: independent of each other and of the cohort refresh, so either order, and neither blocks §3. Both are additive to a gate set that is already green at 6,653 passing tests.

The plan's §3A requires mandatory typical values and a general measurement/calculation section; the live faithfulness policy forbids both. Which authority wins?

generate_docs_system.md:176-182 bans typical values, experiment ranges and device examples to prevent plausible but ungrounded filler, and 172-182 permits measurement or computation only where it is constitutive or discriminating. The ban is enforced by test_normative_documentation_policy.py and a dedicated sn-prose-adjudicator seat. Both reconnaissance scouts flagged the conflict independently and the currency audit ruled the plan claims WRONG (C21, C22).

Locked by the lead 2026-08-23. The live policy is a refinement of §3A's intent rather than a rejection of it: it already admits measurement content exactly where it is constitutive or discriminating, and bans only the generic form. Ungrounded typical values are a real generation failure mode, and the WEST expert-review packet is better carrying none than carrying invented ranges. The alternatives were rejected on cost and risk: the grounded-only synthesis needs a per-path value source the DD does not carry for most paths, so its gate would degrade into exactly the filler the ban exists to stop; inverting the policy outright would make roughly 2,400 doubly-accepted names non-conformant on two gates and would strip the adjudicator seat of its purpose. The operative gate set is therefore definition, governing equation, variable definitions in prose, semantic scope and exclusions, essential relationships, sign convention where COCOS applies, and inline name: cross-references.

Should generated documentation carry a hard maximum length?

Production documentation with real injected context exceeds the 250-word hard bound on 22.4% of holdout rows (19/85) and 15.1% off-holdout (8/53) — consistent across populations, so it is a property of richly-contexted generation rather than one sample. The curated catalog this plan is trying to match exceeds the same bound on 76.5% of its own rows. A binding cap reallocates content rather than truncating it: an earlier iteration measurably traded symbol definitions away to fit newly required content.

Locked by the lead 2026-08-23: a hard word cap is an anti-pattern; remove it. This extends an already-established principle rather than introducing a preference — the naming rule holds that a name is as short as possible while fully retaining semantic meaning with no arbitrary character cap, and records the former length soft cap as an anti-pattern already removed. The evidence supports it: the cap only stopped binding when documents had no content to state (100% conformance on the context-starved arm, 77.6% once real context arrived), and the reference standard sits mostly above it. Retained deliberately: the minimum word floor, so a document must still say something, and the separate one-sentence description character limit, which governs a different field. Consequence for measurement — the gate set drops from ten to nine, and those nine are exactly what has been reported as artifact-free, so that ceases to be a caveat and becomes the headline; all-gate figures recorded earlier are historical and any comparison spanning the change must state which gate set it used. Surfaced by the release-readiness coordinator escalating it as a question about all accepted names rather than working around it per-cohort.

Are the documentation content gates a quality bar, or an instrument?

The nine gates are pure deterministic regex — docs_gates.py makes zero model calls. Four of them test keyword presence: scope passes on "at the" or "on the"; exclusions on "boundary" or "relative to"; physical_meaning on "is" or "denotes"; the relationship gate on any of nine phrases OR a link. Measured, every gate that moved between two identical runs was one of these lexical checks, and every structural check held at exactly its prior value. A metric on which curated human prose scores 0.888 and our generator scores 0.975 is closer to a measure of the generator matching our own prompt vocabulary than of documentation quality — human curators wrote physics, not to a token list.

Locked by the lead 2026-08-23: keep the instrument, delete the gate. The per-gate vector earned its keep twice as a DIAGNOSTIC — it caught the zero-sum instruction-competition trade (+0.165 of gains against −0.165 of losses, invisible to any aggregate score) and it exposed the word cap as mis-set by scoring the catalog at 0.235 on its own bound. It cost more than it returned as a THRESHOLD — the scope 0.800 gate consumed two nodes and roughly USD 0.5 chasing a margin ten times smaller than the measurement's own swing, and the relationship gate's apparent −0.677 collapse was a lexical artifact over a context-starved generator. Deleted: physical_meaning, scope, exclusions_or_distinctions and the relationship gate's phrase alternative, with their patterns retired from the module so they cannot return. Retained, six gates, each comparing the text to something authoritative: defining_equation, symbol_definitions, relationship_link (a resolving link, not a phrase), sign_convention, link_hygiene, minimum_word_count. Consequence: every nine-gate mean recorded earlier in this plan is history, not evidence — it was computed over a set containing four lexical checks.
