§5 — Verdict: a detector, not a judge — and the triage runs without it

Locked by the lead 2026-09-01: the graph-wide advisory census is retired and the deterministic-serving sweep is not purchased. The benchmark answered its question, and the answer is a qualified negative that is worth more than the census would have been.

What DSv4-flash demonstrably does: it catches every critical defect in the controlled corpus (recall 1.000) and does not cry wolf on clean names (false-positive rate 0.300 against a ≤0.500 bar). Those are the two properties a triage instrument exists for. What it cannot do is give the same answer twice or agree with the panel that would adjudicate it: hosted-quorum agreement sits at 0.800 against 0.909, and the residual variance is provider-side rather than request-side — three calls sharing an equal complete-request hash returned 1.000, 0.625 and 0.9875, and three serial full-corpus passes spread Brier across 0.044, straddling the 0.1038 threshold. So it is a competent detector and an inconsistent judge, and a flag that does not reproduce is noise rather than triage.

The remedy is not to engineer the serving stack until it reproduces. Removing that residue needs a controlled deterministic-serving variant — five settings varied one at a time at roughly a 26-minute cold start each, on two dedicated H200s at reduced throughput, displacing the compose seat — and it would settle only whether gates read as values or as bands. It produces no better standard name. Measured against the review-only drain's actual USD 61.56, it is cheaper to rescore the tail than to characterise the instrument that would have ranked it.

The recovery triage in this plan's title survives the census that was going to deliver it. The parked population can be ranked from signals the graph already holds — refine_stop_reason and the collision target recorded by the attempt-budget machinery, reviewer score, chain length, and vocabulary-gap evidence. That ranking is reproducible by construction rather than by benchmark: the same query over the same graph returns the same order, which is exactly the property the advisory route could not supply at any price. This section owes that ranked triage, splitting the tail into rescore-candidate, vocabulary-gap and stay-parked dispositions.

§6 — The recovery the triage identified

The triage in §5 is a ranking, not a repair, and the plan's title claims recovery. What it identified is small and specific: of 275 parked names, 32 are rescore candidates — each holding a best current or historical name-review score at or above 0.85 on its exact identity, with a stop reason that is null or attempts-exhausted. A further 4 are vocabulary gaps for the ISN rotation, and 239 are correctly parked and should stay that way.

The recovery action is sn rescore on those 32 identities and nothing else: the same unchanged name resubmitted for a fresh quorum draw. It is not a reword and not a hand-accept — a name whose siblings accept at 0.96 typically clears on a fresh draw, and rewriting it instead would discard a sound identity. Sizing from the measured review-only drain, which came in at USD 61.56 across roughly 1,179 names, 32 names is on the order of two dollars, so cost is not the constraint and never was.

This section closes when those 32 have been resubmitted and their outcomes recorded — how many accepted, how many returned to the tail, and whether any revealed a pipeline signal rather than a draw. Repeated exhaustion of structurally sound names is a defect to fix in the prompt, quorum composition, or threshold, not something to paper over.

What should the default benchmark reviewer panel size be?

Use the existing three-seat hosted reference panel for bounded benchmark adjudication. DSv4 itself remains one advisory model and never self-authorizes acceptance.

Should semantic-equivalence consensus lift apply within a batch or across an entire run?

Keep equivalence evaluation within each frozen batch so prompt and cohort instability stays visible rather than being masked by run-wide aggregation.

How often should calibration anchors refresh?

Refresh and version the gold corpus when the grammar/public semantic contract changes; otherwise comparisons lose longitudinal meaning.

Where should the regression baseline live?

Commit the frozen benchmark inputs, expected semantic labels, and thresholds for reproducibility; write measured run results to the established benchmark artifact store.
