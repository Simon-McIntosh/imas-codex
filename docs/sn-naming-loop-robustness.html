§1 — Goal, and the standard this plan replaces

Lead direction (2026-07-28): “we should stop asserting that we are going to 'cut an RC' each time when we do not yet have a robust pipeline … we want a robust pipeline and a stable grammar and vocab that the generators can use effectively.”

Five consecutive sessions of sn-west-review-rehearsal ended with “next: cut the RC” and none did — each surfaced a real pipeline defect first (stranded refine products, a source-laundering write path, ~354 unit-contradicting attachments, an operator-blind gap classifier). That is not bad luck; it is evidence the loop is not yet trustworthy enough to put a nameset in front of a human reviewer, and that asserting the release as the next action makes plan state a fiction.

This plan owns the robustness work as its own deliverable. The release moves behind measurable convergence gates (§5): it happens when the loop demonstrably converges, and is otherwise not asserted. “Robust” here means specifically:

  • a composer that fails is told why, in terms it can act on (which token, which slot, which registered alternative);
  • a “vocabulary gap” verdict is trustworthy — it means the grammar genuinely cannot express the quantity, not that a matcher was blind, a dedup check wrong, or a rule unstated;
  • the grammar and vocabulary are stable: additions flow through one rotation process, drift between ISN and its consumers is caught by introspective tests, and known structural holes are closed or explicitly documented.

§3 — Four mechanisms, four remedies

The tokens decompose by why the composer invented them, and the classes overlap (a one-off can also be a near-duplicate):

mechanism~sizeexamples (with proposal counts)remedy
A — ordinality & DD-primitive structure. The composer names the ordinal sample point of a geometric primitive. 139 (128 position + geometry/object/ geometric_base members) line_of_sight_second_point ×19, first_point ×18, rectangle_centre ×14, thick_line_first_point, arc_of_circle_start_point, sphere_centre, x1_width/x2_width Not vocabulary: a locked decision already forbids these names (ordinality never enters the name; the primitive's locus carries the geometry). The gap reporter and retry guidance must say so — “drop the ordinal, use the <primitive> locus” — instead of “token does not exist”.
B — near-duplicate of a registered token. A registered token plus a structural suffix, a synonym, or an order variant. 139 …antenna_module ~ …antenna (0.97), unit_vector_component ~ unit_vector (0.93), optical_depth ~ opacity (×14, settled twice in the rehearsal plan), relative ~ reference (0.92), vorticity_over_r ~ vorticity, lithium_6 ~ lithium Re-run dedup over the 345 unchecked; re-audit the 139 distinct_confirmed (the structural-suffix cases _module/_component/_channel and canonical-order variants are mechanically detectable); route the verdict into retry guidance (“use opacity”).
C — ratio / product spellings. A quotient or product written as a compound token. ~15 vorticity_over_r, velocity_over_b_field, vacuum_toroidal_field_product, gradient_rho_squared_over_B_squared (gm6) Expressible via binary operators since ISN 331ef7a (reduction may wrap a binary form; pin bumped 7cf404d1). The ratio-aware verdict (2af8658b) already emits operand-level guidance; symbol shorthand (rho, B) still needs an expansion instrument.
D — genuine, narrow vocabulary demand. Real quantities, overwhelmingly instrument-side. ~40 detection_efficiency ×14, focal_length, groove_density, gain, intensity, bandwidth, heat_flux, orbit_integral, detector, shunt, valve The only class that warrants ISN additions — an ordinary vocabulary rotation (verify absence, add with definitions, RC, pin bump), sized in the tens, run after A–C shrink the population.

A fifth input is upstream of all four: until 2026-07-28 the composer's failure loop carried no diagnosis at all (empty reason, retry text “produce a different name”, and the model-facing vocabulary tool answered “Unknown grammar segment 'operator'”). Every mechanism above was free to recur because nothing ever told the model what to do differently. That is fixed forward (b7a51fdc, 4e89da12); the stock above is the residue of it.

§5 — Convergence gates: when the release stops being an assertion

The WEST RC (rehearsal plan §3 stage 2) is cut when all of the following hold, and is otherwise not scheduled and not promised:

  • Gate 1 — honest verdicts. Replaying every stored gap token through the shipped classifier yields zero absent verdicts for tokens expressible from registered grammar (operators, reuse, binary forms with registered operands). Controls (substrate, wibble_frobnicator, …) stay absent.
  • Gate 2 — a diagnosed loop. A scoped drain produces gaps only with non-empty, actionable reasons, and the gap-production rate per 100 composes is measured and stable or falling across two consecutive drains.
  • Gate 3 — trustworthy dedup. Rung 2's done-when holds on the live population.
  • Gate 4 — stable grammar surface. ISN grammar suite green at the pinned commit; the codex drift tests (both axes introspected) green; no consumer reads SEGMENT_TOKEN_MAP outside the audited reader list.
  • Gate 5 — the WEST residual is fully instrumented. Every unaccepted manifest source maps to exactly one instrument (rescore / review / compose / supersede-pair / documented non-nameable) with no unexplained bucket — the rehearsal plan's own partition requirement.
